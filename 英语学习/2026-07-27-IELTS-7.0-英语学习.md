## 2. 阅读训练：从“看懂”到“雅思阅读速度”

### 文章中文摘要

这篇 Guardian 报道介绍了澳大利亚墨尔本西北部 Plumpton 一个拟建“超级数据中心”项目。该项目由 Syncline Energy 推动，规划用地约 350 公顷，其中约 140 公顷将用于建设四座数据中心建筑，规模接近澳大利亚现有数据中心之最，并可能消耗极高电力。支持方把它称为维多利亚 AI 枢纽，认为其与新能源、电网和未来 AI 产业发展相关；但当地居民和环保人士担心，项目会带来噪音、用水、用电、景观破坏、电价上涨和更广泛的环境压力。文章还提到，已有数千人签署请愿书，要求政府全面评估该项目的环境、文化、社会和基础设施影响。报道的核心并不是简单反对 AI，而是提出一个雅思常见问题：当技术基础设施迅速扩张时，社会是否已经准备好承担其能源和环境成本？

### 答案与解析

1. **False**  
文章说项目位于 Plumpton，约在墨尔本 CBD 西北 30 公里，并不是市中心。

2. **True**  
文章提到居民对澳大利亚数据中心建设激增有更深层的不适，不只是单个项目。

3. **False**  
公司表示建设至少两年内不会开始，因为还要完成审批。

4. **B**  
争议核心是项目规模、电力消耗、环境影响和基础设施压力。A、C、D 都没有文章依据。

5. **C**  
文章不是简单反 AI，而是在讨论 AI 基础设施扩张带来的能源、环境和监管问题。

6. **residents**  
local residents 与 community concern 同义。

7. **approvals**  
planning and environmental approvals 是原文核心表达。

8. **petition**  
文章提到已有数千人签署请愿书，要求全面评估。

### 长难句拆解

**句子**：  
The proposed centre, larger than any currently operating in Australia, would consume more electricity than Victoria’s largest power station generates.

**结构拆解**：  
- 主干：**The proposed centre would consume more electricity**  
- 插入说明：**larger than any currently operating in Australia**，补充说明该中心规模超过澳大利亚现有数据中心。  
- 比较结构：**more electricity than...**，用来突出能源消耗的严重程度。  
- 逻辑关系：规模巨大 → 电力需求巨大 → 引发公共担忧。

**可模仿表达**：  
- The proposed project, larger than most existing facilities, would require more resources than local communities expected.  
- The new policy, more ambitious than previous measures, could affect more people than officials initially estimated.

### 第二遍精听任务：6-10 句关键表达听写

请播放材料并听写以下 8 类表达。你不需要逐字覆盖整段，只要抓到 TED 中相近意思的完整句或完整短语即可。

1. A sentence about algorithms shaping people’s choices.  
2. A sentence about data being collected in everyday online activities.  
3. A sentence about whether people can use the internet without losing privacy.  
4. A sentence about pushing back against big tech.  
5. A sentence about AI making decisions for humans.  
6. A sentence showing contrast, such as “but / however / the problem is...”  
7. A sentence that gives an example of manipulation or influence.  
8. A sentence that suggests a solution or form of resistance.

### 易听错点提醒

- **连读**：  
  - “data collection and AI” 可能听成一个长音组。  
  - “shape the choices you make” 中 shape the 会连读。

- **弱读**：  
  - “what does it take to...” 里的 does it 常弱读。  
  - “whether it’s possible to...” 里的 it’s possible to 语速会很快。

- **近音词**：  
  - privacy / private  
  - affect / effect  
  - allowed / aloud  
  - decide / design

- **转折信号**：  
  重点听 but, however, yet, the problem is, on the other hand。这些词后面通常是观点核心。

- **抽象名词**：  
  manipulation, autonomy, consent, surveillance 这类词不一定好听清，要结合上下文判断。

### 听后复述模板：80-120 词英文

Today’s talk discusses how algorithms and AI influence people’s choices online. The speakers argue that the problem is not only about individual users being careless, but also about how technology companies collect data and design systems to keep people engaged. They raise concerns about privacy, manipulation and whether AI should be allowed to make certain decisions for humans. In my view, the most important point is that users need more awareness, but governments and companies should also take responsibility. We should not treat convenience as the only goal of technology, because digital systems can affect freedom, trust and social behaviour.

## 4. 口语训练：从中式表达到 IELTS 7.0

### Part 1 Question 1

**Question**：Do you often use apps that recommend content to you?

**普通答案思路**：  
说你经常用短视频、音乐或购物软件，它们会根据兴趣推荐内容。

**7 分答案结构**：  
直接回答 → 举例 → 说明优点 → 补充一点担忧。

**高分表达**：  
- I use recommendation-based apps on a daily basis.  
- They are convenient, but they can also narrow my attention.  
- I sometimes feel I am being nudged toward certain choices.

### Part 1 Question 2

**Question**：Are people in your country concerned about online privacy?

**普通答案思路**：  
很多人嘴上担心，但实际不太看隐私设置。

**7 分答案结构**：  
总体判断 → 对比年轻人与年长者 → 给具体例子。

**高分表达**：  
- People are becoming more privacy-conscious.  
- There is still a gap between awareness and action.  
- Many users trade privacy for convenience without thinking too much.

### Part 1 Question 3

**Question**：Do you think technology saves people time?

**普通答案思路**：  
是的，比如导航、网购、AI 工具，但也可能让人浪费时间。

**7 分答案结构**：  
平衡回答 → 两个例子 → 总结条件。

**高分表达**：  
- It depends on how intentionally we use it.  
- Technology can streamline routine tasks.  
- However, it can also create endless distractions.

### Part 2 Cue Card

**Describe a piece of technology that has changed the way people live.**

You should say:  
- what it is  
- how people use it  
- what benefits it brings  
- and explain whether it has any negative effects

**普通答案思路**：  
可以讲智能手机、AI 推荐系统、导航软件或在线支付。

**7 分答案结构**：  
开头点题 → 功能描述 → 生活变化 → 优缺点平衡 → 个人观点。

**高分表达**：  
- It has become deeply embedded in everyday life.  
- The main benefit is efficiency and instant access to information.  
- The downside is that people may become overly dependent on it.  
- I would say it is powerful, but it needs to be used with self-control.

### Part 3 Question 1

**Question**：Should governments regulate AI companies more strictly?

**普通答案思路**：  
应该，因为 AI 影响隐私、就业和信息安全。

**7 分答案结构**：  
明确立场 → 解释风险 → 承认创新需要空间 → 提出平衡方案。

**高分表达**：  
- Regulation should focus on high-risk uses rather than banning innovation.  
- Companies should be required to explain how sensitive decisions are made.  
- Public accountability is essential when technology affects basic rights.

### Part 3 Question 2

**Question**：Why do people accept technology even when they know it may collect their data?

**普通答案思路**：  
因为方便、免费、大家都用，很难退出。

**7 分答案结构**：  
心理原因 → 现实原因 → 举例 → 总结。

**高分表达**：  
- Convenience often outweighs abstract concerns about privacy.  
- Many people feel they have no realistic alternative.  
- The cost is invisible at the moment of use.

### Part 3 Question 3

**Question**：Do you think AI will make society fairer or more unequal?

**普通答案思路**：  
两种可能都有，看谁能使用 AI、谁控制数据和资源。

**7 分答案结构**：  
双边讨论 → 条件判断 → 举教育或就业例子。

**高分表达**：  
- AI could widen existing inequalities if access is uneven.  
- It may benefit people who already have strong digital skills.  
- Fair outcomes depend on education, regulation and public access.

### Part 3 Question 4

**Question**：What responsibilities do technology users have?

**普通答案思路**：  
用户要保护隐私、辨别信息、控制使用时间。

**7 分答案结构**：  
列出三项责任 → 解释最重要的一项 → 联系社会影响。

**高分表达**：  
- Users should be more critical of what they consume online.  
- Digital literacy is no longer optional.  
- Personal habits can collectively shape the online environment.
