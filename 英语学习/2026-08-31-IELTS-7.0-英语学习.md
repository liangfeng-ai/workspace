## 2. 阅读训练：从“看懂”到“雅思阅读速度”

### 文章中文摘要
这篇文章讲的是，印度、葡萄牙和墨西哥近期接连出现与考试有关的抗议，背后原因包括试题泄露、AI 作弊、考试评分数字化失败，以及高考式压力把学生前途押在单次考试上。专家认为，作弊已经不再只是个体品行问题，而是变成了系统性问题。学校和政府一方面想用技术防作弊，另一方面又因为技术本身出错而引发新的危机。文章最后强调，真正重要的应该是学习本身，而不是把考试神圣化。

### 8 个核心词汇或短语
- **systematic issue** / 系统性问题  
  原文语境：cheating becomes a systematic issue  
  可替换表达：structural problem

- **exam paper leak** / 试卷泄露  
  原文语境：an exam paper leak affected millions of students  
  可替换表达：paper breach

- **digitise the exam marking process** / 将阅卷流程数字化  
  原文语境：Portugal’s attempt to digitise the exam marking process  
  可替换表达：move marking online

- **marking errors** / 评分错误  
  原文语境：serious marking errors  
  可替换表达：grading mistakes

- **public trust** / 公众信任  
  原文语境：a breakdown of public trust  
  可替换表达：confidence in the system

- **untenable pressure** / 难以承受的压力  
  原文语境：creates untenable pressure on young people  
  可替换表达：intense pressure

- **dehumanise** / 去人性化  
  原文语境：mass examinations ... dehumanises the learning process  
  可替换表达：make something impersonal

- **at the heart of the issue** / 问题核心  
  原文语境：AI cheating and attempts ... are at the heart of the issue  
  可替换表达：the main cause

### 长难句拆解
原句核心意思：  
**When a single test can determine a young person’s entire future, cheating is no longer just about individuals but becomes a systematic issue.**

结构拆解：  
- **When a single test can determine...** 是时间/条件状语从句，先交代背景。  
- 主句是 **cheating is no longer just about individuals**。  
- **but becomes a systematic issue** 是并列转折，强调性质升级。  

逻辑关系：  
先说“单次考试决定命运”，再推出“作弊就不只是个人道德问题，而是系统问题”。

可模仿表达：  
- **When X has such a high stake, Y is no longer just about individuals but becomes a structural problem.**  
- **...is not merely..., but also...**

- 数字表达要盯紧：**40 percent**, **two-year-olds**

### 听后复述模板
This episode argues that AI should support human parenting rather than replace it. Dana Suskind explains that children develop language, empathy, and self-control through real interaction, especially in the early years. She worries that AI companions may feel convenient, but they could weaken the messy human friction that helps children grow. Her view is not anti-technology. Instead, she wants AI to reduce the burden on parents and increase human presence. The main idea is simple: use AI as a tool, but do not let it take over human connection.

## 4. 口语训练：从中式表达到 IELTS 7.0

### Part 1
**Q1. Do you think students should use AI to help with homework?**  
普通答案思路：可以，但不能直接复制答案。  
7 分答案结构：先表态，再说用途，再补一个限制。  
可直接套用的高分表达：**AI can be a useful support tool, but it shouldn’t replace actual thinking.**

**Q2. How often do you use your phone when studying?**  
普通答案思路：尽量少用，容易分心。  
7 分答案结构：频率 + 场景 + 后果。  
可直接套用的高分表达：**I try to keep it out of reach because even a quick check can break my concentration.**

**Q3. Did your teachers use technology in class when you were a student?**  
普通答案思路：有，但没那么多。  
7 分答案结构：过去情况 + 具体工具 + 影响。  
可直接套用的高分表达：**We used technology occasionally, but it was more of a supplement than a core part of learning.**

### Part 2 Cue Card
**Describe a time when technology changed the way you learned something.**  
你可以讲：  
- 学了什么  
- 用了什么技术  
- 之前和之后有什么不同  
- 这个变化是好是坏

7 分答案结构：  
开头一句定主题 + 事件经过 + 对比前后 + 个人评价 + 总结影响。  
可直接套用的高分表达：  
- **What changed most was my approach, not just my results.**  
- **It made the learning process more efficient, but also more demanding.**  
- **I realized that convenience and understanding are not always the same thing.**

### Part 3
**Q1. Should schools reduce screen use?**  
普通答案思路：适度减少，但不能完全禁止。  
7 分结构：承认问题 + 平衡观点 + 举例。  
高分表达：**A blanket ban sounds simple, but a more balanced policy is usually more effective.**

**Q2. Can AI make exams fairer?**  
普通答案思路：有帮助，但也会带来新问题。  
7 分结构：优点 + 风险 + 条件。  
高分表达：**AI can improve efficiency, but fairness depends on how well the system is designed and supervised.**

**Q3. Why do some students feel so much pressure from exams?**  
普通答案思路：未来机会太少，考试像唯一出口。  
7 分结构：社会压力 + 家庭期待 + 制度问题。  
高分表达：**When one exam is treated as the gatekeeper of the future, pressure becomes almost inevitable.**

**Q4. What is the best way to assess students besides exams?**  
普通答案思路：平时作业、口头表达、项目展示。  
7 分结构：多元评价 + 适应能力 + 公平性。  
高分表达：**A mixed assessment system usually tells us more about real learning than a single high-stakes test.**
