## 2. 阅读训练：从“看懂”到“雅思阅读速度”

### 文章中文摘要

文章讨论了大型科技公司在发展人工智能时面临的气候承诺困境。Google、Amazon、Microsoft 和 Meta 都曾把自己塑造成科技行业的气候领导者，承诺实现净零排放，并投资风能、太阳能等清洁能源项目。然而，AI 竞赛带来了大量数据中心建设和电力需求，使这些公司的碳排放明显上升。文章指出，Google 和 Amazon 最近发布的可持续发展报告显示，排放量因 AI 数据中心、配送燃料和用电扩张而增加；Microsoft 和 Meta 也面临类似问题。为了满足 AI 基础设施的巨大能源需求，一些公司开始签订天然气发电合同，这使它们原本的减排目标变得更难实现。文章进一步指出，数据中心对化石燃料的需求可能并非短期现象，因为美国已有大量面向数据中心的燃气电厂规划。整体来看，文章强调了 AI 产业增长与环境责任之间的冲突：科技公司既想抓住 AI 带来的商业机会，又难以兑现过去的气候承诺。

### IELTS 阅读题型练习

#### Questions 1-2：True / False / Not Given

1. Google and Amazon reported higher emissions partly because of AI-related infrastructure and electricity use.  
2. The article says Microsoft has already achieved its 2030 net-zero target.

#### Questions 3-4：Multiple Choice

3. What is the main conflict described in the article?  
A. Tech companies are refusing to publish sustainability reports.  
B. AI investment is making climate promises harder to meet.  
C. Renewable energy is becoming cheaper than gas power.  
D. Consumers no longer care about environmental issues.

4. Why does the article mention gas-fired power plants?  
A. To show that data centres may create long-term demand for fossil-fuel energy.  
B. To argue that gas power is always cleaner than renewable energy.  
C. To prove that AI companies have stopped using electricity.  
D. To explain why Australia has reduced its emissions.

#### Question 5：Summary Completion

Complete the summary with words from the list below.

Tech companies once promoted themselves as climate leaders, but the growth of AI has increased demand for electricity and new __________. As a result, their __________ have risen, making earlier net-zero promises harder to achieve.

Words:  
A. emissions  
B. schools  
C. data centres  
D. salaries  
E. advertisements

### 答案与解析

1. **True**  
解析：文章明确说明 Google 和 Amazon 的排放上升与 AI 数据中心建设、配送燃料和用电增加有关。题干是原文信息的概括。

2. **False**  
解析：文章说 Microsoft 曾有净零目标，但其排放相较基线增加，并预计之后可能继续上升；并未说它已经实现目标。

3. **B**  
解析：全文核心是 AI 投资与气候承诺之间的矛盾。A、C、D 都不是文章主旨。

4. **A**  
解析：燃气电厂被用作证据，说明 AI 数据中心可能持续依赖化石燃料供电，而不是短期问题。

5. **C；A**  
完整句：Tech companies once promoted themselves as climate leaders, but the growth of AI has increased demand for electricity and new **data centres**. As a result, their **emissions** have risen, making earlier net-zero promises harder to achieve.

### 长难句拆解

**句子**：Their desire for huge amounts of power has outstripped their commitments to sustainability.

**结构拆解**：  
- 主语：Their desire for huge amounts of power  
- 谓语：has outstripped  
- 宾语：their commitments to sustainability  
- 核心逻辑：电力需求增长 > 可持续发展承诺的承受能力  
- 难点：outstrip 不是“超过”这么简单，而是“增长速度或强度超过某事物，使其难以跟上”。

**可模仿表达**：  
- The demand for convenience has outstripped people’s concern for privacy.  
- The pace of urban development has outstripped the capacity of public transport.  
- The growth of online learning has outstripped schools’ ability to regulate quality.

### 易听错点提醒

- **连读**：find a transcript, read a lot, go to sleep 这类词组中，词与词之间会连在一起。  
- **弱读**：to, and, of, the, a 在 BBC 对话中通常读得很轻，不要试图每个词都听成重音。

## 4. 口语训练：从中式表达到 IELTS 7.0

### Part 1 问题 1

**Question**：Do you often read articles about technology?

**普通答案思路**：  
说“有时读”，主要为了了解 AI、手机、互联网新闻。

**7 分答案结构**：  
直接回答 + 阅读频率 + 具体内容 + 个人影响。

**高分表达**：  
- I try to keep up with technology news.  
- It helps me understand how digital tools are reshaping daily life.  
- I wouldn’t say I’m an expert, but I’m fairly interested in this area.

### Part 1 问题 2

**Question**：Do you prefer reading long articles or watching short videos?

**普通答案思路**：  
短视频轻松，但长文章更有深度。

**7 分答案结构**：  
对比两者 + 说明场景 + 给出偏好。

**高分表达**：  
- Short videos are convenient, but they can be quite superficial.  
- Long-form articles force me to think more carefully.  
- It depends on whether I want quick updates or deeper analysis.

### Part 1 问题 3

**Question**：Has technology changed the way you learn English?

**普通答案思路**：  
改变很大，可以听播客、查词、跟读。

**7 分答案结构**：  
明确说 yes + 举两个工具 + 说明效果。

**高分表达**：  
- Technology has made language learning far more accessible.  
- I can expose myself to authentic English every day.  
- The challenge is to use these tools actively instead of consuming content passively.

### Part 2 Cue Card

**Describe a technology that has changed the way people study.**

You should say:  
- what the technology is  
- how students use it  
- what advantages it has  
- and explain whether it has any disadvantages

**普通答案思路**：  
说 AI 学习工具或在线课程平台。优点是方便、个性化、资源多；缺点是容易依赖、注意力分散。

**7 分答案结构**：  
1. 开头点题：选择 AI learning assistants。  
2. 使用场景：查资料、练口语、改作文、生成题目。  
3. 好处：即时反馈、个性化学习、节省时间。  
4. 缺点：可能偷懒、答案不一定准确、影响深度思考。  
5. 总结：工具本身中性，关键看使用方式。

**高分表达**：  
- It has lowered the barrier to self-study.  
- Students can receive instant feedback instead of waiting for a teacher.  
- The downside is that some learners may become overly dependent on it.  
- Used wisely, it can supplement traditional learning rather than replace it.

### Part 3 深度追问 1

**Question**：Do you think AI will make students better learners?

**普通答案思路**：  
不一定。自律的人会受益，不自律的人会依赖。

**7 分答案结构**：  
balanced answer + 条件限制 + 例子。

**高分表达**：  
- It depends largely on the learner’s discipline.  
- AI can accelerate learning, but it cannot replace effort.  
- The real value lies in feedback, not in ready-made answers.

### Part 3 深度追问 2

**Question**：Should companies be responsible for the environmental cost of new technology?

**普通答案思路**：  
应该，因为公司盈利，也消耗资源。

**7 分答案结构**：  
立场 + 原因 1：资源消耗 + 原因 2：社会影响 + 政策建议。

**高分表达**：  
- Companies should be held accountable for their environmental footprint.  
- Innovation should not come at the expense of public well-being.  
- Governments could require more transparent sustainability reporting.

### Part 3 深度追问 3

**Question**：Why do people still support technologies that may harm the environment?

**普通答案思路**：  
因为方便、经济利益、影响不够直接。

**7 分答案结构**：  
解释三个原因：便利性、短期利益、心理距离。

**高分表达**：  
- The benefits are immediate, while the environmental costs feel distant.  
- Convenience often outweighs long-term concerns.  
- Many people underestimate the hidden infrastructure behind digital services.

### Part 3 深度追问 4

**Question**：How can governments balance technological development and climate goals?

**普通答案思路**：  
制定规则、鼓励清洁能源、要求透明报告。

**7 分答案结构**：  
政策组合：监管 + 激励 + 公众监督。

**高分表达**：  
- A balanced approach would combine regulation with incentives.  
- Governments should encourage clean energy while setting clear limits on emissions.  
- Public transparency can push companies to match their promises with action.
