The academy aims to help educators use AI without harming students’ (1) ________, independent thinking, data privacy, and mental health. Trainers also want lessons that require human (2) ________ and reasoning, so students do not simply outsource their thinking to (3) ________.

**Answer:**

**长难句拆解**  
句子：**The academy’s leaders hope to work with teachers to find ways to use the technology without compromising students’ cognitive development, independent thinking, data privacy and mental health.**

结构：
- 主干：**The academy’s leaders hope**
- 不定式1：**to work with teachers**
- 不定式2：**to find ways**
- 不定式3：**to use the technology**
- 介词短语：**without compromising...**，表示“前提是不能损害……”

逻辑关系：
- 先说目标：和教师合作；
- 再说方法：找到使用技术的办法；
- 最后加限制：不能伤害学生的认知发展、独立思考、隐私和心理健康。

可模仿表达：
- **The team hopes to work with schools to find ways to use AI without compromising student safety.**

## 3. 听力训练：精听 + 抓主旨
**听前预测：根据标题猜 5 个关键词**

2. Repetitive work, drafting, summarizing, and other tasks with clear structure.

**第二遍精听任务：听写 8 句关键表达**  
1. AI tools are improving so quickly that a task that failed six weeks ago may work today.  
2. A useful test is whether the tool saves real time on a real job.  
3. Early AI outputs could sound confident even when they were wrong.  
4. A strong first draft is often more valuable than a perfect final answer.  
5. Humans still need to handle editing, judgment, and the final touch.

**易听错点提醒**  
- 连读：`six weeks ago`, `kind of`, `sort of`, `last mile`  
- 弱读：`to`, `for`, `of`, `and`  
- 同音近音：`their / there`, `work / worth`  
- 数字：`six weeks`, `80%`, `20 bucks`  
- 人名地名：`Molly Graham`, `Max Mullen`, `Instacart`  
- 转折信号：`but`, `however`, `actually`, `instead`

**听后复述模板（80-120词）**  
At first, the host was skeptical about AI because it felt like another hype cycle. After trying it on a real writing task, she changed her mind because it produced a useful first draft in minutes. Max Mullen argues that AI is improving very fast, so people should retry it regularly instead of judging it by old results. He thinks AI is best for repetitive work, drafting, and summarizing, but humans still need to provide judgment, editing, and the final touch. Overall, the episode suggests that AI is a practical tool, not a full replacement for human work.

## 4. 口语训练：从中式表达到 IELTS 7.0
**Part 1**

1. **Do you use AI tools when you study or work?**  
- 普通答案思路：说“用/不用”+ 一个具体场景 + 一个限制  
- 7 分答案结构：直接回答 -> 举例 -> 说明收益 -> 补一句自己如何防止依赖  
- 可直接套用的表达：`It saves me a fair amount of time.` / `I still cross-check the output.` / `I use it as a first-draft tool.`

2. **Should schools teach students how to use AI?**  
- 普通答案思路：先表态，再说为什么，最后提风险  
- 7 分答案结构：立场 -> 教会学生判断和验证 -> 提到隐私/作弊风险 -> 总结  
- 可直接套用的表达：`AI literacy is becoming a basic skill.` / `Students need to know when not to use it.` / `It should be taught with guardrails.`

3. **Has technology changed the way you learn new things?**  
- 普通答案思路：说变化最大的地方是效率或资源获取  
- 7 分答案结构：过去 vs 现在 -> 一个具体工具 -> 一个负面边界 -> 结论  
- 可直接套用的表达：`It has lowered the barrier to entry.` / `I can get instant feedback.` / `Technology is useful, but not a substitute for deep thinking.`

**Part 2 Cue Card**  
Describe a time when you used a tool or app to learn something more effectively.  
You should say:
- what the tool was
- what you learned
- how it helped you
- and explain whether you would use it again

- 普通答案思路：讲一个真实学习场景，强调效率提升  
- 7 分答案结构：背景 -> 工具 -> 过程 -> 结果 -> 反思  
- 可直接套用的表达：`It helped me break the task into manageable steps.` / `It gave me a clearer structure.` / `The biggest benefit was saving time without lowering quality.`

**Part 3**

1. **Should students be allowed to use AI for homework?**  
- 思路：有条件允许，不是全面放开  
- 7 分答案结构：立场 -> 条件 -> 例子 -> 风险控制  
- 高分表达：`There needs to be a clear distinction between assistance and replacement.`

2. **How can schools stop students from over-relying on AI?**  
- 思路：改作业形式，增加口头解释和过程性任务  
- 7 分答案结构：方法1 -> 方法2 -> 为什么有效  
- 高分表达：`Process matters as much as the final answer.`

3. **What skills become more valuable when AI improves?**  
- 思路：批判性思维、判断力、沟通和创造力  
- 7 分答案结构：列技能 -> 解释为什么 -> 连接工作场景  
- 高分表达：`Human judgment becomes a competitive advantage.`

4. **Will human teachers become less important?**  
- 思路：不会消失，但角色会转向指导和激励  
- 7 分答案结构：先否定极端判断 -> 说明 AI 的局限 -> 强调人类价值  
- 高分表达：`AI can support teaching, but it cannot replace empathy or accountability.`
