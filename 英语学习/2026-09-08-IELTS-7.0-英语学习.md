## 2. 阅读训练：从“看懂”到“雅思阅读速度”

**文章中文摘要**  
纽约市宣布对公立小学和初中学生实施为期一年的 AI 使用暂停，这是美国目前最严格的校园 AI 限制之一。市长表示，孩子需要教师和同伴之间的真实互动，需要在解决问题时培养独立性。政策将影响约 60 万名八年级及以下学生，但高中生在部分情况下仍可使用 AI，教师则可在备课和安排等行政工作中使用相关工具。文章还提到，纽约此前曾短暂禁用 ChatGPT，后来又改为引入定制化的 AI 教学助手。整体来看，这则新闻反映出美国学校正在加速形成对 AI 的新规则，而争议焦点集中在“效率提升”和“儿童发展”之间的平衡。

**核心词汇 / 短语**
1. `moratorium`：暂停令，临时禁令  
   - 原文语境：纽约市对学生使用 AI 实施 one-year moratorium  
   - 可替换表达：temporary ban / suspension

2. `restrictive`：限制最严格的  
   - 原文语境：the most restrictive to date across the U.S.  
   - 可替换表达：strict / tight / stringent

3. `guidelines`：指导原则，规则框架  
   - 原文语境：set guidelines around new technology in the classroom  
   - 可替换表达：rules / policy framework

4. `human connection`：人与人之间的真实连接  
   - 原文语境：children need teachers and human connection  
   - 可替换表达：face-to-face interaction / personal contact

5. `lesson planning`：备课  
   - 原文语境：teachers will be permitted to use AI for lesson planning  
   - 可替换表达：class preparation / planning classes

6. `administrative tasks`：行政事务  
   - 原文语境：and tasks such as scheduling  
   - 可替换表达：paperwork / office work

7. `rollout`：推出，实施  
   - 原文语境：the current use of about 40 educational tools  
   - 可替换表达：launch / implementation

8. `custom AI-powered teaching assistant`：定制化 AI 教学助手  
   - 原文语境：replaced by a custom AI-powered teaching assistant  
   - 可替换表达：tailored AI tutor / school-built assistant

## 4. 口语训练：从中式表达到 IELTS 7.0

**主题：AI、学校和学习动机**

**Part 1**

1. Do you think students should use AI at school?  
   - 普通答案思路：说 yes/no，给一个简单原因。  
   - 7 分答案结构：立场 + 1 个原因 + 1 个例子 + 简短让步。  
   - 可直接套用：`I think AI should be used with clear limits, mainly as a support tool rather than a shortcut.`

2. Do you prefer studying alone or with classmates?  
   - 普通答案思路：选一个并说舒服。  
   - 7 分答案结构：偏好 + 什么时候切换 + 原因。  
   - 可直接套用：`It depends on the task, but for deep focus I usually prefer studying alone.`

3. Have your learning habits changed since you started using technology more often?  
   - 普通答案思路：说 changed a lot / not much。  
   - 7 分答案结构：过去 vs 现在 + 具体变化 + 结果。  
   - 可直接套用：`Technology has made me faster at finding information, but I try not to let it replace real thinking.`

**Part 2 Cue Card**  
Describe a time when you learned something difficult by yourself.  
You should say:
- what you learned
- why it was difficult
- how you managed to learn it
- and explain what you gained from the experience

- 普通答案思路：讲一个真实经历，重点放在困难和过程。  
- 7 分答案结构：背景 1 句 + 困难 2 句 + 过程 3 句 + 收获 2 句。  
- 可直接套用：`At first, I felt overwhelmed, but I broke the task into smaller steps and kept going every day.`

**Part 3**

1. Why do some students rely too much on AI?  
   - 普通答案思路：因为方便。  
   - 7 分答案结构：便利性 + 压力 + 学校环境。  
   - 高分表达：`It offers instant answers, which can make effort feel optional.`

2. Should schools ban AI for younger students?  
   - 普通答案思路：yes/no。  
   - 7 分答案结构：年龄分层 + 风险 + 允许范围。  
   - 高分表达：`A blanket ban may be too rigid, but younger learners probably need tighter boundaries.`

3. How can teachers keep students motivated without overusing technology?  
   - 普通答案思路：让课堂有趣。  
   - 7 分答案结构：任务设计 + 反馈机制 + 学生参与。  
   - 高分表达：`The key is to design tasks that feel purposeful, not just polished.`

4. Will AI change education more positively or negatively?  
   - 普通答案思路：both。  
   - 7 分答案结构：优点 + 风险 + 条件句。  
   - 高分表达：`Its impact depends less on the tool itself and more on how responsibly people use it.`

**90 秒口语任务**  
请用英语大声回答：  
`Should schools limit AI use for younger students, or should they teach children to use it early?`  
要求：连续说 90 秒，不要停太久。录音后重点听自己有没有出现：
- 观点不清
- 句子太短
- 中文直译
- 逻辑跳跃

**自评标准**
- fluency：是否能连续说满 90 秒  
- vocabulary：是否用了 `limit / support / responsibility / guidance / independent thinking` 这类词  
- grammar：是否有清楚的从句、让步句、条件句  
- pronunciation：重音是否稳定，连读是否自然  
- idea development：是否有观点、原因、例子、让步
