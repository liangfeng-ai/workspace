## 2. 阅读训练：从“看懂”到“雅思阅读速度”

**文章中文摘要，150-250 词**

这篇文章讨论了一个越来越现实的问题：当医生、程序员和其他知识工作者频繁依赖 AI 工具时，他们原本通过训练积累的专业能力是否会退化。文章引用了医疗和软件工程领域的早期研究。比如，一些内镜医生在使用 AI 辅助识别病变后，一旦没有 AI 帮助，检测表现反而下降；另一个关于软件工程师的实验发现，使用 AI 完成编码任务的人虽然能完成任务，但在之后的概念理解测验中表现较差。文章把这种现象称为 AI-driven deskilling，也就是 AI 驱动的技能退化。专家指出，问题不只是工具替代体力劳动，而是 AI 开始替代思考、解释和判断等认知过程。作者并没有完全否定 AI，而是强调使用者需要保持警觉：要知道自己把哪些思考外包给了 AI，也要理解 AI 的局限，避免在高效完成任务的同时失去真正的学习和判断能力。

**8 个核心词汇或短语**

| 英文 | 中文 | 原文语境 | 可替换表达 |
|---|---|---|---|
| over-reliance | 过度依赖 | 医护人员担心过度依赖 AI 会削弱自身技能 | excessive dependence / heavy reliance |
| deskilling | 技能退化，去技能化 | 文章核心概念：AI 使用可能导致专业能力下降 | skill erosion / loss of expertise |
| atrophy | 萎缩，退化 | 用来描述长期不用的能力逐渐变弱 | deteriorate / weaken |
| cognitive faculties | 认知能力 | AI 不只替代操作，也影响思考和解释能力 | mental abilities / intellectual capacities |
| preserve expertise | 保留专业能力 | 研究者讨论如何在 AI 时代维持人类专长 | maintain professional skills |
| randomized controlled trial | 随机对照试验 | 软件工程师实验采用的研究方式 | controlled experiment |
| diagnose errors | 诊断错误，找出错误原因 | 使用 AI 的程序员在错误诊断题上表现较差 | identify bugs / detect mistakes |
| mindfully vigilant | 有意识地保持警觉 | 使用 AI 时既要利用工具，也要主动质疑输出 | critically alert / consciously cautious |

**Questions 1-2: True / False / Not Given**

**Questions 3-4: Multiple Choice**

4. Why does the article mention GPS navigation?

**Question 5: Summary Completion**

Complete the summary using words from the box.

Box: **questioning / outsourcing / entertainment / foundations / automatic**

The article argues that generative AI may create a gap between task performance and real learning. If users keep ________ their thinking to AI, they may fail to understand the ________ of their work. Therefore, professionals should keep ________ AI outputs rather than accepting them passively.

**答案与解析**

1. **False**。文章提到高技能医生也可能受到影响，并不只是不熟练工作者。
2. **True**。软件工程实验中，使用 AI 的参与者在后续学习测验中平均表现更差。
3. **B**。文章核心不是反 AI，而是指出 AI 可能让人“完成得快”，但“学得少”。
4. **B**。GPS 例子用于说明：技术过去也曾削弱人类某些能力，比如方向感和导航能力。
5. **outsourcing / foundations / questioning**。逻辑是：把认知外包给 AI，可能不了解工作基础，因此要持续质疑 AI 输出。

**长难句拆解**

句子：

Although AI-assisted professionals may appear to perform at a high level, their dependence on automated suggestions can prevent them from developing the underlying skills that would allow them to solve similar problems independently.

结构拆解：

- **Although AI-assisted professionals may appear to perform at a high level**：让步状语从句，表示“虽然他们看起来表现很好”。
- **their dependence on automated suggestions can prevent them from developing...**：主句，核心是 dependence can prevent them from developing skills。
- **the underlying skills that would allow them to solve similar problems independently**：skills 后接定语从句，说明这些“底层技能”的作用。
- 逻辑关系：表面表现好 ≠ 真正学会；依赖工具可能阻碍独立解决问题。

可模仿表达：

- Although online courses may seem convenient, excessive dependence on them can prevent students from developing the discipline that independent learning requires.
- Although translation apps can improve communication, over-reliance on them may stop learners from building real language competence.

**第二遍精听任务：6-10 句关键表达听写练习**

请打开 TED 页面，先不要看 transcript。听第二遍时，暂停并听写下面这些“意思对应”的关键表达。你可以听原句，也可以写出你听到的近似表达。

1. Digital privacy is not only about reading terms of service.
2. The problem goes much deeper than individual online choices.
3. Companies and governments can track people’s movements, searches and habits.
4. Personal data can be collected for profit and control.
5. Surveillance technology is becoming part of ordinary devices.
6. People often underestimate how much information they give away.
7. Fighting back starts with understanding the systems around us.
8. Privacy protection requires more than simply clicking carefully.

**易听错点提醒**

- **连读**：track your movements 中 track your 容易连成 /trækjər/。
- **弱读**：for profit and control 中 for、and 会弱读，不要逐词期待清晰重音。
- **近音词**：privacy / private / piracy 容易混；本材料重点是 privacy。
- **抽象名词**：surveillance, colonialism, corporation, government 都是 IELTS 常见抽象词。
- **转折信号**：but, however, not just, deeper than 之后通常是核心观点。
- **并列信息**：movements, searches and habits 这类三连信息常用于 IELTS 填空题。

**听后复述模板，80-120 词英文**

The talk discusses how modern surveillance technology affects personal privacy. The speaker argues that the problem is not just about making careful choices online, such as reading terms of service or avoiding suspicious links. Instead, data collection has become a larger system involving companies, governments and everyday devices. These systems can track what people do, where they go and what they search for, often for profit or control. The speaker suggests that individuals need to understand how surveillance works and take practical steps to protect their privacy, rather than assuming that digital privacy is only a personal responsibility.

## 4. 口语训练：从中式表达到 IELTS 7.0

### Part 1 Question 1

**Question:** Do you often use AI tools in your studies or work?

普通答案思路：说会用，用来查资料、改句子、总结文章，但不会完全依赖。

7 分答案结构：

- Direct answer：Yes, quite often.
- Specific use：summarising articles / checking grammar / brainstorming.
- Balance：useful but should not replace thinking.

高分表达：

- I use it as a support tool rather than a substitute for thinking.
- It helps me work more efficiently, but I still need to verify the output.
- I try not to outsource the whole learning process to AI.

### Part 1 Question 2

**Question:** Are people in your country concerned about online privacy?

普通答案思路：年轻人知道隐私重要，但行动不多；很多人为了方便会接受数据收集。

7 分答案结构：

- General trend：awareness is increasing.
- Contrast：concerned in theory, careless in practice.
- Example：apps, face recognition, online shopping.

高分表达：

- People are becoming more privacy-conscious.
- There is often a trade-off between convenience and privacy.
- Many users click “agree” without fully understanding what they are giving up.

### Part 1 Question 3

**Question:** Do you think technology makes learning easier?

普通答案思路：更容易获取资料，但也更容易分心或浅层学习。

7 分答案结构：

- Balanced opinion：yes, but not automatically.
- Benefit：access to resources.
- Risk：reduced concentration and independent thinking.

高分表达：

- Technology lowers the barrier to information.
- Easy access does not necessarily lead to deep learning.
- Learners still need discipline and critical thinking.

### Part 2 Cue Card

**Describe a technology that has changed the way people learn.**

You should say:

- what the technology is
- how people use it
- what benefits it brings
- and explain whether it has any disadvantages

普通答案思路：讲 AI 学习工具，比如 ChatGPT。它能解释知识、改作文、模拟口语，但可能让学生偷懒。

7 分答案结构：

1. 开头点题：I’d like to talk about AI learning assistants.
2. 具体功能：summarise texts, explain concepts, correct language mistakes.
3. 好处：personalised, fast, accessible.
4. 风险：over-reliance, shallow understanding, plagiarism.
5. 总结态度：powerful if used critically.

高分表达：

- It can provide instant feedback, which is especially useful for language learners.
- The biggest drawback is that students may confuse completion with learning.
- Used properly, it can enhance learning; used passively, it can weaken it.

### Part 3 Question 1

**Question:** Should schools limit students’ use of AI tools?

普通答案思路：不应完全禁止，但要设规则，比如哪些任务能用、哪些不能用。

7 分答案结构：

- Balanced view：restriction is necessary, but bans are unrealistic.
- Reason：students need AI literacy.
- Policy：clear rules and assessment redesign.

高分表达：

- A complete ban would be difficult to enforce.
- Schools should teach students how to use AI responsibly.
- Assessment should focus more on reasoning, process and oral explanation.

### Part 3 Question 2

**Question:** How can people prevent technology from weakening their skills?

普通答案思路：定期不用工具独立完成任务；用 AI 后复盘；保持基础训练。

7 分答案结构：

- Identify risk：passive dependence.
- Method 1：manual practice.
- Method 2：explain reasoning.
- Method 3：verify and reflect.

高分表达：

- People need to keep practising the underlying skills.
- One useful habit is to solve the problem first and use AI only for comparison.
- Reflection is what turns tool use into learning.

### Part 3 Question 3

**Question:** Who should be responsible for protecting people’s digital privacy?

普通答案思路：个人、公司、政府都有责任。个人注意设置，公司透明，政府立法。

7 分答案结构：

- Shared responsibility.
- Individuals：awareness and habits.
- Companies：transparent data practices.
- Governments：regulation and penalties.

高分表达：

- Privacy protection should not be left entirely to individuals.
- Companies should be held accountable for how they collect and use data.
- Strong regulation is needed because ordinary users have limited bargaining power.

### Part 3 Question 4

**Question:** Do the benefits of AI outweigh the risks?

普通答案思路：看使用场景。医疗、教育有好处，但风险包括错误、偏见、依赖和隐私。

7 分答案结构：

- Conditional answer：it depends on governance and user behaviour.
- Benefits：efficiency, access, personalisation.
- Risks：bias, deskilling, surveillance.
- Final judgment：benefits can outweigh risks only with safeguards.

高分表达：

- AI is not inherently good or bad; its impact depends on how it is designed and used.
- The benefits are significant, but they should not blind us to the long-term risks.
- Without proper safeguards, efficiency may come at the cost of autonomy.
