### 文章中文摘要，约 180 词

文章讨论美国教师工会 American Federation of Teachers 推出的 AI 教师培训项目。该项目获得 Microsoft、OpenAI 和 Anthropic 共 2300 万美元资助，目标是在五年内培训 40 万名教育工作者，帮助教师理解 AI 的潜在用途、风险以及课堂应用边界。支持者认为，AI 已经进入课堂，学生也在使用相关工具，教师如果没有系统培训，就很难判断如何应对作弊、偏见、隐私和学习质量等问题。工会主席强调，教师不是在推广 AI，而是必须了解这种工具。

但批评者担心，科技公司资助教师培训可能会让 AI 产品在学校中获得更高可信度，并进一步扩大大科技公司对教育系统的影响。文章还提到，一些教师已经用 AI 生成练习题、辅助差异化教学或分析学生需求，但也有人担心学生会依赖 AI，削弱独立思考。整体来看，文章不是简单支持或反对 AI，而是在讨论：当 AI 已经进入教育场景时，谁来制定规则、教师应拥有多大话语权、学生思维能力如何被保护。

### 长难句拆解

**句子**：  
Although the training program is presented as a way to help teachers understand a tool that students are already using, critics argue that its funding by major technology companies could make AI products seem more acceptable in classrooms before educators have fully debated their risks.

**结构拆解**：

- **Although 引导让步状语从句**：  
  Although the training program is presented as a way to help teachers understand a tool...  
  虽然这个培训项目被包装成帮助教师理解 AI 工具的方式。

- **that students are already using**：  
  定语从句，修饰 **a tool**，说明这个工具已经被学生使用。

- **主句**：  
  critics argue that...  
  批评者认为……

- **that 从句作 argue 的宾语**：  
  its funding by major technology companies could make AI products seem more acceptable...

- **before 引导时间状语从句**：  
  before educators have fully debated their risks  
  在教育者充分讨论风险之前。

**逻辑关系**：  
让步 + 批评观点 + 潜在后果 + 时间限制。  
核心意思是：即使培训有现实必要，资金来源仍可能影响教育决策。

**可模仿表达**：

- Although X is presented as a way to solve Y, critics argue that it could lead to Z.
- Before schools fully embrace new technology, educators need to debate its long-term risks.
- A tool that appears efficient may still raise questions about privacy, fairness and independence.

### 听后复述模板，80-120 词英文

Today’s podcast discusses whether AI chatbots can replace human relationships in children’s development. Dana Suskind argues that children do not only need information or quick responses; they need imperfect, emotional and face-to-face human interaction. She is not against technology, but she believes AI should enhance human connection rather than replace parents, teachers or caregivers. A useful idea from the episode is the HOPE framework: human connection is irreplaceable, people should own their imperfections, the early years need protection, and technology should enhance rather than replace human experience. ## 4. 口语训练：从中式表达到 IELTS 7.0

### Part 1 Question 1

**Question**：Do you often use AI tools in your studies?

**普通答案思路**：  
说自己会用 AI 查资料、改句子、整理思路，但不会完全依赖。

**7 分答案结构**：  
直接回答 + 具体用途 + 限制意识 + 个人态度。

**可套用高分表达**：

- I use AI as a study assistant rather than a substitute for thinking.
- It helps me organise ideas and check language accuracy.
- I still try to make the final judgement myself.

**示范答案**：  
Yes, I use AI tools quite often, especially when I need to organise ideas or check whether my English sounds natural. However, I try to use them as a study assistant rather than a substitute for thinking. For example, I may ask for feedback on my speaking outline, but I still make the final decision myself.

### Part 1 Question 2

**Question**：Do you think students should be allowed to use AI for homework?

**普通答案思路**：  
可以有限使用，但不能直接复制答案。

**7 分答案结构**：  
平衡观点 + 明确边界 + 举例。

**可套用高分表达**：

- It depends on how they use it.
- There should be clear boundaries.
- Using AI to brainstorm is different from outsourcing the whole task.

**示范答案**：  
It depends on how they use it. If students use AI to brainstorm ideas or understand a difficult concept, I think it can be helpful. But if they simply copy an answer, they are outsourcing the whole task. Schools should set clear boundaries so that AI supports learning instead of replacing it.

### Part 1 Question 3

**Question**：What technology was useful to you when you were at school?

**普通答案思路**：  
词典、网课、学习 app。

**7 分答案结构**：  
过去工具 + 具体帮助 + 对学习习惯的影响。

**可套用高分表达**：

- It made learning more accessible.
- I could review difficult points at my own pace.
- It was useful, but self-discipline still mattered.

**示范答案**：  
Online courses were probably the most useful technology for me. They made learning more accessible because I could review difficult points at my own pace. That said, technology itself was not enough. I still needed self-discipline, otherwise I would just watch videos passively without really learning anything.

### Part 2 Cue Card

**Describe a piece of technology that has helped you learn something.**

You should say:

- what the technology is
- when you started using it
- how it helped you learn
- and explain whether you think it has any disadvantages

**普通答案思路**：  
讲 AI 工具、英语听力 app、在线课程平台。重点说“帮助”和“局限”。

**7 分答案结构**：

1. 开头点题：I’d like to talk about...
2. 背景：when and why you started using it
3. 具体帮助：vocabulary, listening, feedback, organisation
4. 局限：overreliance, distraction, shallow learning
5. 总结态度：useful if used wisely

**可套用高分表达**：

- It lowers the barrier to learning.
- It gives me instant feedback, which is hard to get from textbooks.
- The downside is that it can make me mentally lazy.
- I see it as a tool for guided practice, not a replacement for real effort.

### Part 3 深度追问 1

**Question**：How is AI changing the role of teachers?

**普通答案思路**：  
教师不只是传授知识，而是引导、判断、激励学生。

**7 分答案结构**：  
变化 + 不可替代部分 + 风险。

**高分表达**：

- Teachers may become learning designers rather than simple knowledge providers.
- Human judgement is still essential.
- A teacher can notice emotional and social signals that AI may miss.

### Part 3 深度追问 2

**Question**：Should schools ban AI tools completely?

**普通答案思路**：  
不应该完全禁止，应制定规则。

**7 分答案结构**：  
反对极端做法 + 解释现实原因 + 提出规则。

**高分表达**：

- A complete ban may be unrealistic.
- The more practical approach is to teach responsible use.
- Schools need transparent rules on when AI is acceptable and when it counts as cheating.

### Part 3 深度追问 3

**Question**：What risks might children face if they rely too much on AI?

**普通答案思路**：  
失去独立思考、社交能力、判断力。

**7 分答案结构**：  
认知风险 + 社交风险 + 长期影响。

**高分表达**：

- Overreliance on AI may weaken independent thinking.
- Children may become less comfortable with uncertainty and productive struggle.
- Learning is not only about getting answers; it is also about developing judgement.

### Part 3 深度追问 4

**Question**：Who should decide how AI is used in education: governments, schools, teachers, parents or companies?

**普通答案思路**：  
不能由公司单独决定，需要多方参与。

**7 分答案结构**：  
明确立场 + 分角色说明 + 总结原则。

**高分表达**：

- Tech companies should not be the only decision-makers.
- Teachers understand classroom realities, while parents understand children’s needs.
- Policy should protect students’ privacy, independence and long-term development.

### 随机抽查 5 个旧词汇

请先不要看答案，写出中文意思并造句。

1. **overreliance**  
2. **guidance**  
3. **privacy**  
4. **replace**  
5. **interaction**

### 参考答案

1. **overreliance**：过度依赖  
   Example: Overreliance on AI may prevent students from developing independent thinking.

2. **guidance**：指导  
   Example: Teachers need clear guidance on how to use AI responsibly.

3. **privacy**：隐私  
   Example: Schools should protect students’ privacy when using digital tools.

4. **replace**：替代  
   Example: AI can support teachers, but it should not replace them.

5. **interaction**：互动  
   Example: Face-to-face interaction is important for children’s social development.

### 复用 2 个旧表达造新句

请用下面两个表达各造 1 句：

1. **enhance rather than replace**  
2. **clear boundaries**

**示范**：

- Technology should enhance rather than replace meaningful communication between teachers and students.
- Schools need clear boundaries to prevent AI from becoming a shortcut for homework.

### 昨天最该重复的训练点

今天先建立基准：你最需要重复的是 **“AI 不是简单 good / bad，而是 support / replace / regulate 的平衡讨论框架”**。  
口语里不要只说：

- AI is convenient.
- AI is bad for students.

要升级成：

- AI can be useful if it enhances learning rather than replaces independent thinking.
- The key issue is not whether students use AI, but whether schools set clear boundaries.
