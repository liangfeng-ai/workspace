5. **C**

### 长难句拆解

**句子**：  
While each death due to extreme heat is a preventable tragedy, mortality only represents the tip of the iceberg of how heat is impacting our health.

**结构拆解**：  
- **While...**：让步状语从句，表示“虽然……”。  
- **each death due to extreme heat**：主语，意为“每一例由极端高温造成的死亡”。  
- **is a preventable tragedy**：系表结构，说明这些死亡本可避免。  
- **mortality only represents...**：主句，mortality 是主语，represents 是谓语。  
- **the tip of the iceberg**：比喻“冰山一角”，表示死亡只是可见部分。  
- **of how heat is impacting our health**：解释“冰山”具体指高温对健康的更广泛影响。

**逻辑关系**：  
作者先承认每一例死亡都很严重，再进一步推进：死亡还不是全部问题，高温对健康的影响更广。

**可模仿表达**：  
While each case of ______ is serious, it only represents the tip of the iceberg of how ______ is affecting ______.

例句：  
While each case of student burnout is serious, it only represents the tip of the iceberg of how academic pressure is affecting young people’s mental health.

### 易听错点提醒

- **连读**：  
  - *good at doing* 容易听成 “gooda doing”。  
  - *straight away after* 中 *away after* 会连在一起。  
  - *kind of more formal* 中 *kind of* 常弱化成 /kaindəv/。

- **弱读**：  
  - *to* 在 *to agree / to contribute / to record* 中通常弱读。  
  - *of* 在 *a bit of a waste of time* 中非常轻。

- **近音词**：  
  - *notes* / *minutes*：一个是普通笔记，一个是正式会议纪要。  
  - *actions* / *action points*：后者更像会议后要完成的具体任务。

- **转折和重点信号**：  
  - *actually*：常引出真实观点。  
  - *because*：后面通常是原因。  
  - *rather than*：表示“不是 A，而是 B”，雅思听力常考。  
  - *the crucial thing*：后面往往是答案重点。

## 4. 口语训练：从中式表达到 IELTS 7.0

今日口语主题：**extreme heat, public health, and practical action**

### Part 1 Question 1

**Question**：Do you prefer hot weather or cold weather?

**普通答案思路**：  
说自己更喜欢冷一点或温和天气，因为热天容易累、注意力下降。

**7 分答案结构**：  
直接回答 + 具体原因 + 个人例子 + 小总结。

**高分表达**：  
- I function better in mild weather.  
- Hot weather tends to drain my energy.  
- It makes it harder for me to concentrate for long periods.

**示范答案**：  
I prefer mild or slightly cold weather because I function better when the temperature is comfortable. Hot weather tends to drain my energy, especially when I have to study or commute. For example, during a heatwave, I find it much harder to concentrate for long periods, so I usually try to study early in the morning.

### Part 1 Question 2

**Question**：How do people in your city stay cool in summer?

**普通答案思路**：  
空调、遮阳、喝水、减少户外活动。

**7 分答案结构**：  
常见做法 + 区分不同人群 + 补充评价。

**高分表达**：  
- avoid going out during peak hours  
- rely heavily on air conditioning  
- simple but effective measures  
- people who work outdoors are more exposed to heat

**示范答案**：  
Most people rely heavily on air conditioning, especially at home and in shopping malls. Some also avoid going out during peak hours and carry water with them. These are simple but effective measures, but people who work outdoors, such as delivery workers, are still much more exposed to heat.

### Part 1 Question 3

**Question**：Do you think hot weather affects people’s mood?

**普通答案思路**：  
会，容易烦躁、失眠、效率低。

**7 分答案结构**：  
观点 + 心理影响 + 生理原因 + 例子。

**高分表达**：  
- It can make people more irritable.  
- Poor sleep can affect emotional stability.  
- There is a link between physical discomfort and mental wellbeing.

**示范答案**：  
Yes, definitely. Hot weather can make people more irritable, partly because they may sleep badly or feel physically uncomfortable throughout the day. I think there is a strong link between physical discomfort and mental wellbeing. When I cannot sleep well in summer, I’m usually less patient the next day.

### Part 2 Cue Card

**Describe a time when weather affected your daily plan.**

You should say:

- when it happened  
- what the weather was like  
- how it changed your plan  
- and explain how you felt about it

**普通答案思路**：  
讲一次高温、暴雨或台风影响出行、学习或考试安排。

**7 分答案结构**：  
背景一句 + 天气细节 + 原计划 + 被迫改变 + 情绪变化 + 反思。

**高分表达**：  
- My original plan was to...  
- The weather turned out to be much worse than expected.  
- I had no choice but to adjust my schedule.  
- Looking back, it taught me to be more flexible.

**示范框架**：  
I’d like to talk about a day last summer when a heatwave affected my study plan. My original plan was to spend the whole afternoon in the library preparing for an English exam. However, the weather turned out to be much worse than expected. It was extremely hot, and even the short walk to the bus stop made me feel exhausted. In the end, I had no choice but to adjust my schedule. I stayed at home, studied in shorter blocks, and took more breaks. At first, I felt frustrated because I wanted to follow my plan strictly. But looking back, it taught me to be more flexible and to plan around my energy level, not just my timetable.

### Part 3 Question 1

**Question**：Why are some groups of people more vulnerable during heatwaves?

**普通答案思路**：  
老人、小孩、慢性病患者、户外工作者更危险。

**7 分答案结构**：  
分人群解释 + 说明原因 + 联系公共服务。

**高分表达**：  
- older people may have weaker physical resilience  
- outdoor workers face prolonged exposure  
- public-health warnings should be targeted rather than general

**答案方向**：  
Some groups are more vulnerable because they either have weaker physical resilience or greater exposure to heat. Older people and people with chronic illnesses may find it harder to regulate body temperature, while outdoor workers face prolonged exposure. That is why public-health warnings should be targeted rather than general.

### Part 3 Question 2

**Question**：Should governments take more responsibility for protecting people from extreme weather?

**普通答案思路**：  
应该，个人努力不够，政府要预警、基础设施、帮助弱势群体。

**7 分答案结构**：  
明确立场 + 个人责任局限 + 政府可做事项 + 结论。

**高分表达**：  
- individual choices have obvious limits  
- invest in climate-resilient infrastructure  
- provide cooling centres and emergency alerts  
- prevention is usually cheaper than dealing with a crisis

**答案方向**：  
Yes. Individual choices have obvious limits, especially for people who cannot afford better housing or air conditioning. Governments can invest in climate-resilient infrastructure, provide cooling centres, and send emergency alerts. In the long run, prevention is usually cheaper than dealing with a public-health crisis.

### Part 3 Question 3

**Question**：How can cities be designed to cope better with extreme heat?

**普通答案思路**：  
更多树荫、公园、浅色建筑、通风、公共避暑空间。

**7 分答案结构**：  
城市设计 + 交通/住房 + 社会公平。

**高分表达**：  
- expand green spaces  
- reduce the urban heat island effect  
- design shaded public areas  
- make adaptation fair and accessible

**答案方向**：  
Cities can cope better by expanding green spaces, planting more trees, and designing shaded public areas. These changes help reduce the urban heat island effect. However, adaptation should not only benefit wealthy neighbourhoods. Cooling measures need to be fair and accessible, especially in densely populated areas.

### Part 3 Question 4

**Question**：Do you think climate change is mainly an environmental issue or a health issue?

**普通答案思路**：  
两者都是，但健康影响更容易让公众重视。

**7 分答案结构**：  
双边回答 + 解释转化 + 举例 + 总结。

**高分表达**：  
- It is impossible to separate the two.  
- Environmental changes translate into health risks.  
- heat stress, poor sleep, anxiety and respiratory problems  
- framing it as a health issue may make it more urgent to the public

**答案方向**：  
I think it is impossible to separate the two. Climate change is environmental in origin, but environmental changes translate into health risks, such as heat stress, poor sleep, anxiety and respiratory problems. Framing it as a health issue may make it more urgent and easier for the public to understand.
