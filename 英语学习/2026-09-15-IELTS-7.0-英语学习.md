## 2. 阅读训练：从“看懂”到“雅思阅读速度”

### 文章中文摘要

文章讨论了美国 2026 年中期选举中，一些候选人如何把气候问题与选民更关心的日常议题联系起来。由于气候政策具有政治争议性，部分民主党候选人开始减少直接谈论气候变化，这种现象被称为 *climate-hushing*。不过，气候危机仍然影响住房保险、能源账单、就业、人工智能数据中心和电子废弃物。加州候选人 Jane Kim 提议建立政府主导的灾害保险制度，以应对私人保险公司退出高风险地区的问题。密歇根候选人 William Lawrence 反对不受监管的数据中心扩张，担心其消耗大量电力、占用水资源并推高地方成本。内布拉斯加候选人 Dan Osborn 支持维修权，认为消费者应当能够自行维修产品，从而减少浪费并降低维修费用。文章的核心观点是，气候政策更容易获得支持的方式，是将它与经济安全、社区生活和消费者权益结合起来。

### 答案与解析

1. **True**  
   文章将 *climate-hushing* 解释为政治人物减少或停止在政治宣传中直接提及气候危机。

2. **False**  
   文章明确指出，一些调查认为选民不想听到全球变暖，但另一些调查直接反驳了这一结论。题目中的 *every* 使其错误。

3. **True**  
   Jane Kim 的政府主导灾害保险方案参考了新西兰、法国和西班牙的政府支持型保险项目。

4. **C**  
   Dan Osborn 支持 *right to repair*，主张消费者和独立维修店能够获得维修所需的工具和诊断软件。

5. **B**  
   William Lawrence 支持对新数据中心建设实行暂时禁令，以便地方政府先制定严格监管规则。

6. **C**  
   文章并非认为气候议题已经失去重要性，而是强调将气候问题与保险、能源价格、就业、社区安全等日常问题结合，能够扩大政策支持基础。

7. **electricity**  
   文中指出数据中心消耗大量电力，其中相当一部分仍来自化石燃料。

8. **waste stream**  
   *Keep usable equipment out of the waste stream* 意为避免仍可使用的设备进入废弃物处理系统。

### 长难句拆解

> *What’s important is to learn how to embed the climate issue organically amidst the other issues that people care most about.*

**结构分析：**

- **What’s important is...**：主语从句作主语，意思是“重要的是……”。
- **to learn how to...**：不定式作表语，说明具体需要学习什么。
- **embed the climate issue organically**：把气候议题自然地融入其他议题。
- **amidst the other issues**：表示“在其他议题之中”。
- **that people care most about**：定语从句修饰 *issues*。

**逻辑关系：**

这句话不是建议政治人物单独强调气候问题，而是提出“关联策略”：把气候变化与住房、就业、健康和生活成本等人们真正关心的议题联系起来。

**可模仿表达：**

- *What’s important is to learn how to connect abstract issues with people’s everyday experiences.*
- *The challenge is to explain the problem in a way that ordinary voters can relate to.*
- *It is essential to integrate environmental concerns into broader economic policies.*

### 第一遍泛听任务

第一遍不要暂停，也不要看 Transcript。听完后用英文或中文回答：

1. What physical problem is the rapid development of AI creating?
2. Why are data centers closely connected with the future of the power grid?

**听后核对要点：**

1. AI is creating extremely high energy demand that existing power grids may struggle to meet.  
2. Data centers require a stable and continuous supply of electricity, which can affect energy prices and grid management.  
3. If their computing demand is managed flexibly, AI facilities could store, shift or adjust energy use and help stabilize the grid.

### 第二遍精听任务

先听视频并记录你听到的内容，再打开 Transcript 核对。以下句子是根据视频核心内容整理的听写练习句，重点训练主题表达和句子结构。

1. The rapid expansion of artificial intelligence is creating a new challenge for the power grid.  
2. Data centers require enormous amounts of electricity to operate continuously.  
3. The problem is not simply how much energy AI consumes, but also when that energy is consumed.  
4. Some computing tasks can be moved to periods when renewable electricity is more readily available.  
5. This flexibility could allow data centers to respond to changes in the electricity supply.  
6. In that sense, AI facilities could function like virtual batteries for the grid.  
7. The technology responsible for increasing demand may also help us manage that demand more intelligently.  
8. A smarter energy system will require cooperation between technology companies, utilities and policymakers.

**建议做法：**

- 第一次：每句只听一遍，写下关键词。
- 第二次：暂停后补全句子。
- 第三次：打开 Transcript，标出冠词、介词、连词和词尾。

### 易听错点提醒

- **连读：** *data centers require* 可能听起来像一个连续音。
- **弱读：** *to*, *for*, *can*, *and* 在句中通常不重读。
- **同音近音：** *grid*、*greed*；*demand*、*command*；*power*、*tower*。
- **数字和比较：** 注意 *more*, *less*, *higher*, *lower* 等变化词。
- **转折信号：** 留意 *but*, *however*, *rather than*, *in that sense*。
- **因果信号：** 留意 *because*, *therefore*, *as a result*, *which means that*。
- **术语辨认：** *virtual battery* 不是实体电池，而是指通过调节用电时间来帮助电网平衡。
- **重音变化：** *not simply how much energy, but when energy is consumed* 中的对比重音很重要。

> This creates a problem because ____________________.  
> However, the talk also presents a possible solution: ____________________.  
> If data centers can ____________________, they may help ____________________.  
> Overall, the speaker suggests that ____________________.

**参考复述框架：**

Data centers need a continuous supply of electricity, which can put pressure on power grids and increase the use of fossil fuels. However, the speaker argues that these facilities could also become part of the solution. In this way, they might function like virtual batteries and help stabilize the grid. The main idea is that technology should be designed to support a cleaner and more flexible energy system.

## 4. 口语训练：从中式表达到 IELTS 7.0

### Part 1

#### 1. Do you often think about environmental issues in your daily life?

**普通答案思路：**  
会，因为天气、垃圾分类、电费或交通都和环境有关。

**7 分答案结构：**

1. 直接回答频率；
2. 举一个日常例子；
3. 说明这个问题为什么与自己有关。

**可直接套用的高分表达：**

- *I’m increasingly aware of environmental issues, especially when they affect everyday expenses.*
- *It is no longer an abstract problem because it influences the way we travel, consume energy and plan our lives.*
- *I try to make practical choices rather than simply express concern.*

#### 2. Would you support the construction of a large data center near your home?

**普通答案思路：**  
不一定，要看它是否带来工作，以及会不会污染、耗电、耗水。

**7 分答案结构：**

1. 给出有条件的立场；
2. 说明两个潜在好处；
3. 说明两个风险；
4. 给出监管条件。

**可直接套用的高分表达：**

- *I would support it only if the local community had a genuine say in the decision.*
- *The project might create jobs and improve local tax revenue, but those benefits should be weighed against its environmental costs.*
- *Strict requirements on water consumption, emissions and public consultation would be essential.*

#### 3. Do people in your country usually repair things or replace them?

**普通答案思路：**  
年轻人有时直接更换，因为维修贵或不方便；一些人仍然会维修家电和汽车。

**7 分答案结构：**

1. 描述总体趋势；
2. 解释背后的经济原因；
3. 加入环保角度；
4. 说明可能的改变。

**可直接套用的高分表达：**

- *Replacement has become the default option for many consumers.*
- *People often compare the repair cost with the price of a new product.*
- *A stronger right-to-repair policy could make maintenance more affordable and reduce electronic waste.*

### Part 2 Cue Card

**Describe a public policy that could improve people’s lives and protect the environment.**

You should say:

- what the policy would be
- what problem it would address
- how it might affect ordinary people
- and explain whether you think it would be difficult to implement

**普通答案思路：**

可以选择维修权、公共交通补贴、灾害保险、数据中心用电监管或垃圾减量政策。重点说明政策解决什么问题，以及谁会受益、谁可能反对。

**7 分答案结构：**

1. **Policy：** 清晰介绍政策；
2. **Problem：** 解释它针对的现实问题；
3. **Impact：** 分析对消费者、企业和政府的影响；
4. **Evaluation：** 讨论执行难点并给出最终判断。

**可直接套用的高分表达：**

- *The policy I have in mind is a comprehensive right-to-repair law.*
- *It would require manufacturers to provide spare parts, diagnostic software and repair manuals.*
- *The immediate benefit would be that consumers could extend the life of their products instead of replacing them prematurely.*
- *From an environmental perspective, this could reduce resource extraction and the amount of electronic waste entering landfills.*
- *The main obstacle would be resistance from manufacturers, who might argue that the policy threatens their intellectual property.*
- *Despite these concerns, I believe the policy would be worthwhile if it were introduced gradually and supported by clear technical standards.*

### Part 3 深度追问

#### 1. Should governments prioritize economic growth or environmental protection?

**普通答案思路：**  
不能简单二选一，短期经济增长可能带来长期环境成本。

**7 分答案结构：**

- 先否定简单二分；
- 区分短期和长期；
- 提出绿色增长或有条件增长；
- 举例说明。

**高分表达：**

- *The two objectives should be treated as interconnected rather than mutually exclusive.*
- *Economic growth that ignores environmental costs may create hidden liabilities for future generations.*
- *The more sustainable approach is to invest in clean technology and green infrastructure.*

#### 2. Why do some communities oppose renewable-energy or technology projects?

**普通答案思路：**  
担心噪音、土地、房价、能源价格和决策不透明。

**7 分答案结构：**

- 说明反对不一定源于反科技；
- 列举具体生活影响；
- 强调社区参与和利益分配；
- 给出解决方案。

**高分表达：**

- *Opposition is often rooted in concerns about fairness rather than hostility towards technology.*
- *Residents may feel that they bear the inconvenience while companies receive most of the benefits.*
- *Early consultation and a transparent compensation mechanism could reduce social resistance.*

#### 3. Should companies be legally required to make products repairable?

**普通答案思路：**  
应该，但需要考虑安全、知识产权和产品质量。

**7 分答案结构：**

- 明确立场；
- 解释消费者权益；
- 承认反方理由；
- 提出平衡方案。

**高分表达：**

- *Manufacturers should be held responsible for the entire life cycle of their products.*
- *Repairability would improve consumer choice and reduce unnecessary waste.*
- *That said, regulations should distinguish between legitimate safety concerns and restrictions designed mainly to protect profits.*

#### 4. How can politicians make climate policies more acceptable to ordinary voters?

**普通答案思路：**  
不要只讲宏大目标，要联系账单、工作、健康、房屋和社区安全。

**7 分答案结构：**

- 说明沟通方式的重要性；
- 将气候政策与具体利益联系；
- 强调公平；
- 举出有效政策例子。

**高分表达：**

- *Politicians need to frame climate action in terms of household security and public health.*
- *Voters are more likely to support a policy when they can see a direct and credible benefit.*
- *The transition also has to be perceived as fair, particularly by low-income households and workers in declining industries.*

- 如果你连续 3 天完成困难，后续会将阅读材料缩短至 500-700 词、听力精听句减少到 6 句，但保留 IELTS 7.0 所需的观点展开、让步结构和准确词汇目标。
- 明天批改时将重点记录：阅读定位错误、听力漏听类型、口语停顿位置和最常见的中式表达。
