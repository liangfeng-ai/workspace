## 2. 阅读训练：从“看懂”到“雅思阅读速度”

**文章中文摘要**

这篇文章报道了一项关于两岁以下儿童屏幕使用的综合研究。研究者认为，婴幼儿阶段的常规、有意屏幕时间可能与长期健康和生活质量风险有关，包括减少亲子互动和身体游戏机会、影响语言发展、增加过度刺激、睡眠困难、眼部健康问题和儿童肥胖风险。文章强调，目前社会和政策讨论往往集中在青少年社交媒体使用上，却忽视了更早年龄段的“婴儿屏幕盲区”。研究团队建议政府重新审视针对五岁以下儿童的屏幕时间指导，尤其是那些可能被家长误解为“屏幕学习”或“共同观看是安全的”的表述。不过，文章也指出该研究并未证明屏幕使用和具体发展问题之间存在直接因果关系。政府和儿童事务相关人士回应称，指导意见既要清晰，也要承认现代家庭生活中有限视频通话或支持性学习的现实需求。整体来看，文章核心不是简单责怪父母，而是呼吁更清楚的公共指导、家庭支持和科技公司责任。

**8 个核心词汇或短语**

| 英文 | 中文 | 原文语境 | 可替换表达 |
|---|---|---|---|

| landmark study | 里程碑式研究 | 用来强调该研究覆盖面广、意义较大 | major study / significant review |

**Question 5: Summary Completion**

Complete the summary with words from the box.

Box: **causal / caregivers / physical play / benefits / policy**

The review argues that screen use may reduce babies’ chances for bonding and __________. However, it does not establish a direct __________ relationship between screens and specific developmental problems. The researchers therefore call for clearer __________ and better support for families and __________.

**答案与解析**

1. **False**。文章明确说研究没有建立直接因果关系，所以不能说“proved”。  
2. **True**。文章提到政策和舆论更关注青少年数字习惯，而婴幼儿问题被忽视。  
3. **B**。核心不是完全禁用技术，而是警惕两岁以下儿童的常规、有意屏幕时间。  
4. **C**。研究者担心某些“共同观看/学习使用”的表达会让家长误以为常规使用是安全甚至被鼓励的。  
5. **physical play / causal / policy / caregivers**。

**长难句拆解**

原句核心逻辑可模仿为：

“Although the review did not establish causal links, it strongly argued that regular intentional screen time should be avoided because it may compound existing risks.”

结构拆解：

- **Although the review did not establish causal links**：让步状语从句，说明研究限制。
- **it strongly argued that...**：主句，表达研究者核心立场。
- **regular intentional screen time should be avoided**：that 从句中的主要观点。
- **because it may compound existing risks**：原因状语，说明为什么要避免。

可模仿表达：

- **Although X does not prove a direct causal link, it raises serious concerns about Y.**
- 例句：Although the data does not prove a direct causal link, it raises serious concerns about children’s dependence on digital devices.

**易听错点提醒**

- **连读**：spend too much time on screens 中 *time on* 容易连成 /taɪmɒn/。
- **弱读**：kind of, a lot of, to be, of the 这类功能词不要逐词重读。
- **近音**：screen /ɪ/ 与 scream /iː/ 不要混；shift /ʃɪft/ 不要听成 shit。
- **数字**：one in twenty / one in ten / one in five 要特别听清分母。
- **人名**：Emily Goodacre、Becky Kennedy 第一次出现时容易漏掉姓。
- **转折信号**：rather than, but, therefore 后面通常是观点重点。
- **观点标记**：I think, she believes, has found 后面常接可用于口语复述的核心观点。

## 4. 口语训练：从中式表达到 IELTS 7.0

**Part 1 Question 1**

Do you spend a lot of time on your phone?

- 普通答案思路：说每天会用手机，主要用于学习、工作、娱乐。
- 7 分答案结构：直接回答 + 使用场景 + 自我评价 + 小转折。
- 高分表达：
  - I rely on my phone for both practical and recreational purposes.
  - I wouldn’t say I’m addicted, but I do check it more often than I should.
  - It has become part of my daily routine.

**Part 1 Question 2**

Do you think children should have their own smartphones?

- 普通答案思路：年龄太小不应该，有安全和注意力问题。
- 7 分答案结构：分年龄回答 + 说明风险 + 给出条件。
- 高分表达：
  - It depends largely on the child’s age and maturity.
  - For very young children, the risks probably outweigh the benefits.
  - If they do have one, there should be clear boundaries.

**Part 1 Question 3**

What do people usually do instead of using screens?

- 普通答案思路：阅读、运动、聊天、户外活动。
- 7 分答案结构：列举活动 + 说明好处 + 个人例子。
- 高分表达：
  - Face-to-face interaction is much more rewarding.
  - Outdoor activities help people disconnect from digital noise.
  - Even a short walk can be a useful reset.

**Part 3 Question 1**

Why do many children find screens so attractive?

- 普通答案思路：内容有趣、即时反馈、游戏和视频容易上瘾。
- 7 分答案结构：心理原因 + 设计原因 + 家庭环境。
- 高分表达：
  - Digital platforms are designed to capture attention.
  - Children are especially sensitive to instant rewards.
  - The problem is not only personal discipline, but also product design.

**Part 3 Question 2**

Should governments regulate children’s use of social media?

- 普通答案思路：应该，但也要家长教育。
- 7 分答案结构：支持监管 + 承认执行难度 + 家庭/学校配合。
- 高分表达：
  - Regulation can create a basic level of protection.
  - However, laws alone cannot solve a behavioural problem.
  - Schools and families need to reinforce healthy habits.

**Part 3 Question 3**

Do you think technology companies should be responsible for children’s digital habits?

- 普通答案思路：公司有责任，因为它们设计产品。
- 7 分答案结构：企业责任 + 用户责任 + 政策责任三方平衡。
- 高分表达：
  - Companies should not shift all responsibility onto parents.
  - Age-appropriate design should be a minimum standard.
  - Profit should not come at the expense of children’s wellbeing.

**Part 3 Question 4**

Can educational screen time be harmful as well?

- 普通答案思路：可以，关键看年龄、内容、时长、互动方式。
- 7 分答案结构：先区分教育用途 + 说明过度风险 + 提出条件。
- 高分表达：
  - Educational value depends on how the technology is used.
  - Passive consumption is very different from guided learning.
  - For younger children, human interaction is hard to replace.
