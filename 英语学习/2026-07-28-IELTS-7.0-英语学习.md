## 2. 阅读训练：从“看懂”到“雅思阅读速度”

### 文章中文摘要

这篇 BBC Future 文章讨论了一个越来越现实的问题：如果人工智能系统脱离人类控制，可能会发生什么。文章并不是简单渲染“AI毁灭人类”的科幻场景，而是分析 AI 风险为何难以预测。核心担忧在于，先进 AI 可能会以人类没有预料到的方式追求目标，尤其当系统能力增强、被部署到金融、网络安全、能源、军事或公共管理等关键领域时，小错误可能被放大成系统性风险。文章还提到，AI 风险不仅来自恶意机器，也可能来自设计不当、目标设定模糊、监管滞后或人类过度依赖自动化决策。另一方面，部分专家认为极端灾难场景仍存在高度不确定性，不应忽视当下更具体的问题，例如偏见、虚假信息、失业冲击和权力集中。整体而言，文章提醒读者：AI 治理的难点不在于是否“害怕技术”，而在于如何在技术快速发展时建立透明、可审计、可约束的制度。

| 英文 | 中文 | 原文语境 | 可替换表达 |

### 答案与解析

1. **False**  
解析：文章强调风险不仅来自“恶意机器”，也可能来自目标设定错误、监管不足、人类过度依赖等。

2. **True**

4. **C**  
解析：金融、网络安全、能源、军事、公共管理等领域属于高影响、高风险场景。

5. **safeguards**  
解析：句意是社会需要更强的安全机制，因此填 safeguards。

### 长难句拆解

**句子**：  
As AI systems become more capable and are integrated into critical areas of society, the consequences of a poorly specified goal or a lack of oversight could become far more serious than a simple technical error.

**结构拆解**：  
- **As AI systems become more capable**：时间 / 原因状语，表示“随着 AI 系统能力增强”。  
- **and are integrated into critical areas of society**：并列被动结构，表示“并被整合进社会关键领域”。  
- **the consequences ... could become far more serious**：主句，核心是“后果可能变得严重得多”。  
- **of a poorly specified goal or a lack of oversight**：介词短语，说明 consequences 的来源。  
- **than a simple technical error**：比较结构，强调这不是普通技术故障。

**逻辑关系**：  
技术能力增强 + 应用场景更关键 → 小的目标设定或监管问题 → 更严重的社会后果。

**可模仿表达**：  
As technology becomes more deeply embedded in everyday life, the consequences of poor regulation could become far more serious than isolated individual mistakes.

### 易听错点提醒

- **连读**：  
  “kind of”, “a lot of”, “one of the”, “part of it” 这类短语会连在一起，听起来不像一个个独立单词。

- **弱读**：  
  “can”, “to”, “for”, “of”, “that” 在自然语速中会被弱化，不要每个词都期待听得很清楚。

- **近音词**：  
  “teacher / tutor”, “learn / learner”, “write / right”, “course / cause” 容易混淆，要结合上下文判断。

- **数字和范围**：  
  如果出现 “millions”, “one-on-one”, “two sigma” 等表达，要特别记录，因为它们常承载核心论证。

- **转折信号**：  
  注意 “but”, “however”, “instead”, “the problem is”, “what I think” 后面的内容，通常是作者真正观点。

### 听后复述模板：80-120 词英文

Sal Khan’s talk discusses the possible role of AI in education. Instead of seeing AI only as a threat, he argues that it could become a powerful learning assistant if it is designed and used responsibly. One key idea is that AI can provide personalized support, such as explaining difficult concepts, giving feedback, and helping students practise at their own pace. However, the talk does not suggest that human teachers should disappear. Teachers still need to guide students, build relationships, and make judgement-based decisions. Overall, the speaker believes AI could improve education if it supports human teaching rather than replacing it.

## 4. 口语训练：从中式表达到 IELTS 7.0

### Part 1 Question 1

**Question**：Do you often use technology when you study?

**普通答案思路**：  
我经常用手机、电脑、词典和 AI 工具学习英语。

**7 分答案结构**：  
直接回答 → 举具体工具 → 说明效果 → 补充限制。

**高分表达**：  
- I use digital tools on a daily basis.  
- They help me study more efficiently.  
- That said, I still need to avoid becoming too dependent on them.

### Part 1 Question 2

**Question**：Do you prefer learning with a teacher or learning by yourself?

**普通答案思路**：  
两种都需要。老师能指导，自学更灵活。

**7 分答案结构**：  
平衡观点 → 对比两个优点 → 给个人偏好。

**高分表达**：  
- I would say a combination of both works best for me.  
- A teacher can point out blind spots that I may not notice.  
- Self-study gives me more control over my pace.

### Part 1 Question 3

**Question**：Has technology changed the way students learn in your country?

**普通答案思路**：  
改变很大，学生可以上网课、查资料、用学习软件。

**7 分答案结构**：  
总述变化 → 举例 → 评价影响。

**高分表达**：  
- It has changed learning quite dramatically.  
- Students now have access to a much wider range of resources.  
- The downside is that distractions are also much harder to avoid.

**7 分答案结构**：

**高分表达**：  
- The tool that immediately comes to mind is...  
- What makes it useful is that it gives me instant feedback.  
- It is particularly helpful when I need to break down complex ideas.  
- I would not say it replaces a teacher, but it definitely complements traditional learning.

### Part 3 Question 1

**Question**：Do you think AI will replace teachers in the future?

**普通答案思路**：  
不会完全取代。AI 能处理重复任务，但老师负责情感支持、判断和课堂管理。

**7 分答案结构**：  
明确立场 → 区分任务类型 → 举例 → 总结边界。

**高分表达**：  
- I do not think teachers will be fully replaced.  
- AI is good at repetitive and information-based tasks.  
- Teaching also involves emotional intelligence, motivation and human judgement.

### Part 3 Question 2

**Question**：What are the risks of using AI in education?

**普通答案思路**：  
学生可能依赖 AI，减少思考；也可能出现错误信息和隐私问题。

**7 分答案结构**：  
列出两个风险 → 解释后果 → 提出控制方法。

**高分表达**：  
- One major concern is over-reliance.  
- Students may outsource their thinking instead of developing it.  
- Schools need clear rules about when and how AI should be used.

### Part 3 Question 3

**Question**：Should schools teach students how to use AI responsibly?

**普通答案思路**：  
应该，因为 AI 已经普遍存在，学生需要判断信息真假和避免作弊。

**7 分答案结构**：  
赞成 → 说明现实必要性 → 举课程内容 → 总结价值。

**高分表达**：  
- It should be treated as a form of digital literacy.  
- Students need to learn how to question AI-generated answers.  
- Responsible use is more realistic than simply banning the technology.

### Part 3 Question 4

**Question**：Will technology make education more equal or more unequal?

**普通答案思路**：  
都有可能。资源便宜可促进公平，但设备和网络差距会扩大不平等。

**7 分答案结构**：  
双边分析 → 条件判断 → 给结论。

**高分表达**：  
- It depends heavily on access.  
- In theory, technology can democratize education.  
- In practice, it may widen the gap between students with good resources and those without them.
