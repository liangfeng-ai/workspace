### 2.1 文章中文摘要

纽约市计划禁止公立学校八年级及以下学生使用人工智能工具，相关规定预计于 2026 年 9 月开始实施。与此同时，小学三年级以前的学生在课堂上也将被限制使用笔记本电脑和平板电脑。支持这一政策的教师和家长认为，过度依赖 AI 可能导致学生放弃独立思考，甚至出现研究人员所称的“认知投降”。他们还担心 AI 工具对儿童心理健康、认知发展以及环境造成潜在影响。新规定允许高中生在特定情况下接触 AI，例如学习 AI 技术本身，但禁止使用能够提供心理支持的陪伴型聊天机器人。教师可以利用 AI 准备课程或撰写信息，却不能让 AI 批改作业。教育部门还建议限制课堂屏幕时间，并成立由教师、官员、专家和工会代表组成的工作组，为未来的 AI 教育政策提供依据。文章同时指出，真正的难点在于如何监督学生在校外使用 AI。

### 2.5 长难句拆解

**改写句：**

> Although the policy permits limited AI use in high school, it prohibits younger students from using the technology and prevents teachers from relying on it to grade assignments.

#### 结构分析

- **Although the policy permits limited AI use in high school**  
  让步状语从句，表示“尽管政策允许高中阶段有限使用 AI”。
- **it prohibits younger students from using the technology**  
  主句第一个并列动作：禁止低龄学生使用 AI。
- **and prevents teachers from relying on it to grade assignments**  
  主句第二个并列动作：阻止教师依赖 AI 批改作业。
- **prohibit/prevent somebody from doing something**  
  两个高频结构：
  - prohibit students from using AI
  - prevent teachers from relying on technology

#### 逻辑关系

这是一个典型的 **让步 + 并列限制** 结构：

> Although A is permitted, B and C are restricted.

#### 可模仿表达

1. **Although online learning offers greater flexibility, it may reduce students’ opportunities for face-to-face interaction.**
2. **Although the policy aims to protect children, it may create difficulties for schools that depend heavily on digital tools.**
3. **The government permits limited use of the technology but prohibits institutions from relying on it entirely.**

## 4. 口语训练：从中式表达到 IELTS 7.0

### 4.1 IELTS Speaking Part 1

#### Question 1: How often do you use your smartphone?

- **普通答案思路：**  
  每天使用，主要用于沟通、查资料和娱乐。
- **7 分答案结构：**
  1. 直接回答频率；
  2. 说明主要用途；
  3. 补充一个例外或自我控制措施。
- **高分表达：**
  - **I rely on my phone for a wide range of everyday tasks.**
  - **It is mainly a practical tool rather than a source of entertainment.**
  - **I try to keep my screen time under control by…**
- **示范句：**  
  *I rely on my phone for a wide range of everyday tasks, especially messaging, navigation and looking up information. That said, I try to keep my screen time under control by turning off unnecessary notifications.*

#### Question 2: Did you use much technology when you were a child?

- **普通答案思路：**  
  小时候使用电脑或电视较多，但不像现在这样随时联网。
- **7 分答案结构：**
  1. 对过去情况作总体描述；
  2. 与现在进行对比；
  3. 说明这种变化的影响。
- **高分表达：**
  - **Technology was present, but it was far less immersive.**
  - **The biggest difference is that…**
  - **Children were not constantly connected to…**
- **示范句：**  
  *Technology was present when I was a child, but it was far less immersive. The biggest difference is that we were not constantly connected to social media, so we had more uninterrupted time for outdoor activities and face-to-face conversations.*

#### Question 3: Do you think children should be allowed to use smartphones at school?

- **普通答案思路：**  
  不应该完全禁止，但应根据年龄和用途设置限制。
- **7 分答案结构：**
  1. 给出有条件的观点；
  2. 说明潜在好处；
  3. 说明风险并提出规则。
- **高分表达：**
  - **I would not advocate a blanket ban.**
  - **The key issue is not access itself, but how the device is used.**
  - **There should be clear age-appropriate boundaries.**
- **示范句：**  
  *I would not advocate a blanket ban, because smartphones can be useful for research and emergencies. However, the key issue is how they are used, so schools should establish clear age-appropriate boundaries and keep phones away during lessons unless they are genuinely needed.*

### 4.2 IELTS Speaking Part 2

#### Cue Card

**Describe a time when you had to reduce your use of technology.**

You should say:

- when it happened;
- what kind of technology you reduced;
- why you decided to reduce it;
- and explain how you felt afterwards.

#### 普通答案思路

可以讲：

- 准备考试时减少短视频；
- 睡前不看手机；
- 参加短期 digital detox；
- 因为注意力下降或睡眠受影响而改变习惯。

#### 7 分答案结构

1. **背景：** 交代时间和生活状态；
2. **问题：** 说明技术使用过多带来的具体影响；
3. **行动：** 解释你采取了哪些可执行措施；
4. **结果：** 描述注意力、情绪、睡眠或效率的变化；
5. **反思：** 给出更深层的观点。

#### 可直接套用的高分表达

- **The turning point came when I realised that…**
- **I was spending far more time on it than I had intended.**
- **It was gradually undermining my concentration.**
- **I set myself a fairly strict rule that…**
- **The first few days were surprisingly difficult.**
- **I became more aware of how easily my attention could be hijacked.**
- **Looking back, the experience taught me that moderation is more realistic than total avoidance.**
- **Technology is useful as long as it remains a tool rather than becoming a habit that controls me.**

#### 参考开头

> *I’d like to talk about a period when I deliberately reduced my use of short-video apps while preparing for an important exam. The turning point came when I realised that I was spending far more time on them than I had intended…*

### 4.3 IELTS Speaking Part 3

#### Question 1: Why are children attracted to smartphones and social media?

- **普通答案思路：**  
  好玩、即时反馈、社交需要、算法推荐。
- **7 分答案结构：**
  1. 说明心理原因；
  2. 说明社交原因；
  3. 说明平台设计因素；
  4. 适当加入让步。
- **高分表达：**
  - **Children are particularly responsive to immediate rewards.**
  - **They are also under considerable peer pressure to stay connected.**
  - **Many platforms are deliberately designed to maximise engagement.**
  - **This is not simply a matter of weak self-discipline.**

#### Question 2: Should schools ban smartphones completely?

- **普通答案思路：**  
  可以在课堂禁止，但不一定在整个校园完全禁止。
- **7 分答案结构：**
  1. 先给出有条件的观点；
  2. 分析完全禁止的优点；
  3. 分析完全禁止的缺点；
  4. 提出更可行的折中方案。
- **高分表达：**
  - **A complete ban may be effective, but it could also be unnecessarily rigid.**
  - **A more proportionate approach would be…**
  - **The policy should distinguish between educational use and passive consumption.**
  - **Consistency is essential; otherwise the rule will be difficult to enforce.**

#### Question 3: Does technology make education more equal?

- **普通答案思路：**  
  一方面降低优质教育资源的门槛，另一方面加剧数字鸿沟。
- **7 分答案结构：**
  1. 先承认技术的积极作用；
  2. 指出设备、网络和数字能力不平等；
  3. 得出“技术本身不能自动带来公平”的结论。
- **高分表达：**
  - **Technology has the potential to democratise access to education.**
  - **That potential is not distributed equally.**
  - **The digital divide involves more than simply owning a device.**
  - **Access, guidance and digital literacy are equally important.**

#### Question 4: What responsibilities do parents have regarding children’s screen use?

- **普通答案思路：**  
  设定规则、以身作则、与孩子沟通，而不是简单没收手机。
- **7 分答案结构：**
  1. 说明父母要设立边界；
  2. 强调父母自身的示范作用；
  3. 说明应帮助孩子建立自我管理能力；
  4. 讨论规则应具有灵活性。
- **高分表达：**
  - **Parents should establish boundaries without turning the issue into a constant battle.**
  - **Children are more likely to accept rules when adults follow them as well.**
  - **The long-term aim should be self-regulation rather than permanent surveillance.**
  - **A negotiated routine is often more sustainable than an arbitrary prohibition.**

### 6.1 随机抽查 5 个旧词汇

请先遮住答案，写出中文释义和一个英文例句：

1. **substantial**
2. **contribute to**
3. **in the long run**
4. **a growing concern**
5. **be exposed to**

#### 参考答案

1. **substantial**：大量的；重大的  
2. **contribute to**：促成；导致；为……作贡献  
3. **in the long run**：从长远来看  
4. **a growing concern**：日益严重的担忧  
5. **be exposed to**：接触到；暴露于  

### 6.2 复用 2 个旧表达造新句

请使用以下表达各造一个与今日主题相关的新句：

- **in the long run**
- **contribute to**

参考方向：

- *In the long run, excessive dependence on AI may weaken students’ ability to think independently.*
- *Unregulated screen use may contribute to poor concentration and reduced face-to-face interaction.*

### 6.3 昨日最该重复的训练点

今天继续重点重复：

> **不要只记住观点，要记住观点之间的逻辑关系。**

尤其注意以下表达：

- **Although A, B**
- **While A may…, B can…**
- **This is not simply a matter of…**
- **The key issue is not A, but B.**
- **In the long run,…**

完成口语时，至少主动使用其中三个。

## 7. 难度调节

### 如果你连续 3 天完成度较高

下阶段将进行以下升级：

- 阅读材料提升至 700–900 词，并增加作者态度题和段落信息匹配题；
- 听力材料增加学术讲座或 NPR 深度访谈；
- 口语 Part 3 加入反事实问题、政策比较和因果链分析；
- 听写从 6 句增加到 8–10 句；
- 要求复述时减少模板依赖，增加同义替换和自然衔接。

### 如果你完成困难

采用降级方案，但保留 IELTS 7.0 表达目标：

- 阅读先只精读 500–600 词；
- 每次只完成 5 个核心词汇；
- 听力先使用 0.85 倍速，第二遍再恢复正常速度；
- 听写从 6 句减少到 3–4 句；
- 口语先完成 60 秒，再逐步增加到 90 秒；
- 保留以下高分目标表达：
  - **The key issue is not…, but…**
  - **Although it is true that…, I would argue that…**
  - **In the long run,…**
  - **A more proportionate approach would be…**
