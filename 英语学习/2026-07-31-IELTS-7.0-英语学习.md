## 2. 阅读训练：从“看懂”到“雅思阅读速度”

**文章中文摘要，约 190 词**

这篇 Reuters 报道聚焦美国在 2026 年 7 月同时遭遇的三类极端天气威胁：东部受加拿大和美国野火烟霾影响，南部得克萨斯州发生严重山洪，西部和太平洋西北地区出现新的大火。文章指出，这些灾害并非孤立事件，而是相互叠加的“复合事件”，会让公共健康、救援资源和城市管理面临更大压力。美国多个州出现大型火灾，消防人员、直升机和军用灭火飞机被大量部署；同时，干旱、低积雪量和闪电使火灾风险提前达到通常要到八月中旬才会出现的程度。专家解释说，气候变化可能通过改变急流形态、升高气温和增强大气持水能力，使干旱和强降雨同时变得更极端。文章还提到，超过一亿美国人受到空气质量警报影响，而得州部分地区在数日内出现极端降雨，造成死亡和大规模救援。整体来看，文章重点不是单一灾害，而是说明气候系统变化如何让多个风险同时爆发。

**阅读练习**

**Questions 1-3: True / False / Not Given**

1. The article says the United States was facing wildfire smoke, flooding, and new fires at the same time.  
2. The article states that Chicago had the worst air quality in the world on Friday.  
3. The article claims that all scientists agree jet stream resonance is the only cause of the extreme weather.

**Questions 4-5: Multiple Choice**

4. What is the main purpose of mentioning “compound events”?

A. To argue that wildfires are always more dangerous than floods  
B. To explain why several disasters happening together can worsen the overall impact  
C. To show that weather forecasting has become impossible  
D. To suggest that the disasters were unrelated

5. According to the article, why can a warmer atmosphere contribute to both drought-related fires and heavy rainfall?

A. It prevents clouds from forming in most regions  
B. It removes moisture from soil while also holding more water vapor  
C. It reduces wind speed across the United States  
D. It makes rivers dry up immediately after rainfall

**答案与解析**

1. True  
解析：文章核心就是美国同时面对东部烟霾、南部洪水、西部火灾三类威胁。

2. False  
解析：文章说芝加哥当天空气质量是全球第二差，不是最差。IELTS 中 worst 和 second-worst 是典型细节陷阱。

3. Not Given  
解析：文章提到一位科学家的解释，但没有说“所有科学家都同意”，也没有说这是“唯一原因”。

4. B  
解析：“compound events”强调灾害叠加后的影响可能比单一事件更严重。

5. B  
解析：文章解释了同一个变暖机制可以带来两种看似相反的结果：土壤和植被更干，同时大气储水能力更强，导致强降雨。

**长难句拆解**

句子，改写自文章观点：

Although the effects may appear contradictory, a warmer atmosphere can intensify wildfires by drying out soil and vegetation while also increasing the amount of water vapor available for extreme rainfall.

结构拆解：

- Although the effects may appear contradictory：让步状语从句，意思是“尽管这些影响看起来相互矛盾”。
- a warmer atmosphere can intensify wildfires：主句核心，主语是 a warmer atmosphere，谓语是 can intensify。
- by drying out soil and vegetation：方式状语，解释为什么会加剧野火。
- while also increasing the amount of water vapor：while 连接并列逻辑，表示“与此同时也会……”。
- available for extreme rainfall：后置形容词短语，修饰 water vapor。

可模仿表达：

Although the results may seem inconsistent, X can worsen Y by doing A while also increasing the likelihood of B.

例句：

Although online learning may seem convenient, excessive screen use can reduce concentration by fragmenting attention while also increasing students’ dependence on digital devices.

以下句子为基于节目内容改写的 IELTS 听写句，训练你抓社会话题中的核心表达。听完原材料后，再用这些句子做二次听写和复述。

**易听错点提醒**

- 连读：spend too much time 容易听成 spend-too-much-time，一个整体节奏。
- 弱读：to / for / and / that 在句中通常很轻，不要逐词等重音。
- 近音：screen / scream；content 名词重音在前，content 形容词重音在后。
- 数字：under 16s、six-minute、several hours 这类表达要特别记录。
- 人名地名：Neil、Becca、Australia、the UK，要注意首字母大写和拼写。
- 转折信号：however、but、on the other hand、at the same time 后面通常是答案重点。

**听后复述模板，80-120 词英文**

The programme discusses whether children’s screen time should be limited. It explains that many parents are concerned because children now spend a lot of time on smartphones, tablets and social media. Some governments have introduced or considered rules to protect young people from harmful online content. However, the speakers also suggest that technology is not simply bad, because it can support learning, communication and entertainment. In my view, the key issue is balance. Instead of banning devices completely, parents and schools should help children develop healthier habits and use technology in a more responsible way.

## 4. 口语训练：从中式表达到 IELTS 7.0

**Part 1 Question 1**

Do you often use your phone?

- 普通答案思路：说自己经常用，主要用于学习、聊天、查资料。
- 7 分答案结构：直接回答 + 使用场景 + 轻微反思。
- 高分表达：
  - I use it on a daily basis.
  - It is almost my go-to tool for...
  - I try not to let it dominate my day.

示范答案：

Yes, I use my phone on a daily basis, mainly for messaging, checking news and learning English. It is almost my go-to tool when I need quick information. That said, I try not to let it dominate my day, because too much screen time can make me feel distracted.

**Part 1 Question 2**

Do you think children should have their own smartphones?

- 普通答案思路：不应该太早有；可以在一定年龄后有，但要限制。
- 7 分答案结构：观点 + 条件 + 原因。
- 高分表达：
  - It depends on their age and maturity.
  - under proper supervision
  - set reasonable boundaries

示范答案：

It depends on their age and maturity. I don’t think very young children need their own smartphones, but teenagers may need one for safety and communication. The important thing is that they use it under proper supervision, with reasonable boundaries set by their parents.

**Part 1 Question 3**

What do you usually do to reduce screen time?

- 普通答案思路：关闭通知、设定时间、睡前不用手机。
- 7 分答案结构：两个具体方法 + 效果。
- 高分表达：
  - turn off unnecessary notifications
  - keep my phone out of reach
  - improve my concentration

示范答案：

I usually turn off unnecessary notifications, especially when I am studying. I also try to keep my phone out of reach before going to bed. These small habits help me improve my concentration and prevent me from scrolling without a clear purpose.

**Part 2 Cue Card**

Describe a time when you tried to reduce your screen time.

You should say:

- when it was
- why you wanted to reduce it
- what you did
- and explain whether it was successful

- 普通答案思路：备考期间发现手机影响学习，于是限制社交媒体和通知。
- 7 分答案结构：背景 15 秒 + 问题 20 秒 + 行动 30 秒 + 结果与反思 25 秒。
- 高分表达：
  - I became aware of the problem when...
  - It was affecting my ability to focus.
  - I replaced passive scrolling with more purposeful activities.
  - It was not a dramatic change, but it was sustainable.

**Part 3 Question 1**

Why do many people find it difficult to control their screen time?

- 普通答案思路：手机设计让人上瘾，通知很多，社交压力大。
- 7 分答案结构：心理原因 + 技术原因 + 社会原因。
- 高分表达：
  - digital platforms are designed to capture attention
  - instant rewards
  - fear of missing out
  - self-control alone is often not enough

**Part 3 Question 2**

Should governments regulate children’s use of social media?

- 普通答案思路：应该有一定监管，但不能完全代替家庭教育。
- 7 分答案结构：支持有限监管 + 说明边界 + 提到家长和学校责任。
- 高分表达：
  - age-appropriate restrictions
  - protect minors from harmful content
  - a shared responsibility
  - regulation should be carefully designed

**Part 3 Question 3**

How has technology changed the way students learn?

- 普通答案思路：资源更多、更方便，但也更容易分心。
- 7 分答案结构：正面影响 + 负面影响 + 平衡观点。
- 高分表达：
  - access a wider range of learning resources
  - personalise their learning
  - fragmented attention
  - technology is useful only when it is used intentionally

**Part 3 Question 4**

Do you think people will spend more or less time on screens in the future?

- 普通答案思路：可能更多，因为工作学习都数字化；但健康意识也会增强。
- 7 分答案结构：预测趋势 + 解释原因 + 加入条件。
- 高分表达：
  - screen-based interaction will become even more common
  - digital well-being
  - unless people develop stronger habits
  - the boundary between online and offline life

**90 秒口语任务**

请你录音或大声回答：

Some people believe children should be banned from using social media, while others think they should simply be taught how to use it responsibly. What is your opinion?

要求：

- 回答 90 秒。
- 必须包含 1 个让步句：Although / While / Admittedly...
- 必须包含 1 个例子。
- 必须使用至少 3 个表达：set boundaries / harmful content / digital habits / under supervision / responsible use。

**自评标准**

- Fluency：是否能连续说 90 秒，中间停顿是否过多。
- Vocabulary：是否使用了 topic-specific vocabulary，如 screen time、online safety、digital habits。
- Grammar：是否至少使用了 1 个复杂句和 1 个让步句。
- Pronunciation：重音是否落在关键词上，如 children、social media、responsibly。
- Idea development：是否有观点、原因、例子和结论，而不是只说零散句子。
