## 2. 阅读训练：从“看懂”到“雅思阅读速度”

### 文章中文摘要

这篇文章讨论了美国学校中生成式 AI 工具的快速扩张，以及由此引发的家长、教育专家和儿童发展研究者的担忧。科技公司和部分政策制定者认为，学生需要掌握 AI 素养，因为未来工作环境会普遍使用这类技术；支持者还认为，AI 可以帮助学生理解复杂内容，并为有阅读障碍等学习困难的学生提供辅助。然而，反对者认为，目前缺乏足够证据证明 AI 真能提升儿童学习效果，反而可能削弱学生的独立思考能力，让他们过早依赖机器完成认知任务。文章提到，纽约、俄勒冈等地的家长和组织已经要求暂停或限制学生直接使用 AI 工具，尤其是在小学阶段。部分专家提出折中方案：不应完全禁止 AI，而应让学生学习它的局限和风险。文章的核心冲突是：学校应把 AI 当作未来必备技能来推广，还是应在证据不足时优先保护儿童的基础认知能力和学习自主性。

### 8 个核心词汇或短语

| 英文 | 中文 | 原文语境 | 可替换表达 |
|---|---|---|---|
| generative AI | 生成式人工智能 | 学校引入能生成反馈、文字或互动回答的 AI 工具 | AI-powered content tools |
| moratorium | 暂停令，暂缓实施 | 家长和组织要求学校暂停学生直接使用 AI | temporary ban / suspension |
| cognitive development | 认知发展 | 专家担心 AI 影响儿童思维能力形成 | mental growth / intellectual development |
| student-facing tools | 面向学生使用的工具 | 争议集中在儿童直接接触 AI 产品 | tools used directly by students |
| AI literacy | AI 素养 | 支持者认为学生需要理解和使用 AI | digital literacy / technological competence |
| critical thinking | 批判性思维 | 反对者担心学生依赖 AI 后减少独立判断 | analytical thinking / independent judgement |
| cognitive off-loading | 认知卸载 | 用外部工具替代自己的思考过程 | mental outsourcing / reliance on external aids |
| guardrails | 保护性规则，限制措施 | 学校需要制定 AI 使用边界 | safeguards / restrictions |

### 长难句拆解

**句子：**  
While technology companies argue that AI tools can prepare students for a future workplace where such systems are common, many parents and child-development experts worry that early exposure may weaken children’s ability to think independently.

**结构拆解：**

- **While technology companies argue that...**  
  让步状语从句：表示“尽管科技公司认为……”
- **AI tools can prepare students for a future workplace**  
  从句中的核心观点：AI 可以让学生为未来职场做准备。
- **where such systems are common**  
  定语从句，修饰 workplace：未来职场中 AI 系统很常见。
- **many parents and child-development experts worry that...**  
  主句：许多家长和儿童发展专家担心……
- **early exposure may weaken children’s ability to think independently**  
  worry 的宾语从句：过早接触可能削弱独立思考能力。

**逻辑关系：**  
“支持方理由”与“反对方担忧”形成对比，适合雅思写作和口语 Part 3。

**可模仿表达：**

- While some people argue that..., others worry that...
- Although digital tools may improve efficiency, they may also reduce...
- A future workplace where... requires students to...

### 易听错点提醒

- **连读**：  
  “part of” 可能听成 /par-dəv/；“kind of” 可能弱化成 /kain-də/。

- **弱读**：  
  “to”, “of”, “for”, “that” 在 TED 演讲中常被快速带过，不要逐词硬听，先抓重读词。

- **近音词**：  
  “toy” /tɔɪ/ 与 “tool” /tuːl/ 都可能出现，但含义不同：toy 偏儿童玩具，tool 偏学习或技术工具。

- **转折信号**：  
  注意 but, however, instead, rather than, the real question is... 这些词后面通常是演讲者真正观点。

- **抽象名词**：  
  attachment, development, interaction, authority, creativity 容易听懂声音但反应不过含义，建议单独跟读。

### 听后复述模板：80-120 词英文

Today’s talk discusses how children interact with AI-powered toys and technologies. The speaker argues that the main issue is not whether children should use AI, but whether they understand its limits. Some AI systems can appear friendly or intelligent, so children may develop emotional attachment or trust them too easily. Instead of treating AI as a perfect authority, children should be encouraged to question how it works, what it can and cannot know, and who designed it. The speaker suggests that playful activities can help children become more critical, curious and responsible users of technology.

## 4. 口语训练：从中式表达到 IELTS 7.0

### Part 1 Question 1

**Question:**  
Do you often use AI tools in your studies?

**普通答案思路：**  
说自己会用 AI 查资料、改句子、练口语，但不会完全依赖。

**7 分答案结构：**  
直接回答 + 具体用途 + 限制意识 + 个人态度。

**高分表达：**

- I use AI mainly as a study assistant, not as a replacement for thinking.
- It helps me generate ideas, but I still need to judge whether the answers are reliable.
- I find it useful for language learning, especially when I need instant feedback.

### Part 1 Question 2

**Question:**  
Do you think students should be allowed to use AI at school?

**普通答案思路：**  
可以用，但要有规则，低龄学生尤其需要限制。

**7 分答案结构：**  
平衡回答 + 年龄差异 + 规则重要性。

**高分表达：**

- It depends on the age of the students and the purpose of using it.
- For older students, AI can be helpful if it is used responsibly.
- For younger children, schools should set clear boundaries.

### Part 1 Question 3

**Question:**  
What technology did you use most when you were a student?

**普通答案思路：**  
手机、电脑、在线词典、网课平台。

**7 分答案结构：**  
过去使用 + 具体场景 + 和现在对比。

**高分表达：**

- When I was in school, online dictionaries were probably the tool I used most.
- Compared with today’s AI tools, they were much simpler but also less distracting.
- They helped me learn independently rather than giving me complete answers.

### Part 2 Cue Card

**Describe a piece of technology that has helped you learn something.**

You should say:

- what it is
- when you started using it
- how it helped you learn
- and explain whether you think it has any disadvantages

**普通答案思路：**  
可以讲 AI、在线词典、YouTube、播客或背单词 App。重点不是“工具多高级”，而是“它如何改变你的学习方式”。

**7 分答案结构：**

1. 开头点题：介绍工具。  
2. 具体经历：什么时候、为什么开始用。  
3. 学习帮助：效率、反馈、输入量、兴趣。  
4. 缺点与反思：可能依赖、分心、信息不准确。  
5. 总结：工具有用，但学习者仍要主动思考。

**高分表达：**

- It lowered the barrier to learning English on a daily basis.
- The biggest advantage is that it gives me immediate feedback.
- However, I try not to become overly dependent on it.
- Technology works best when it supports active learning rather than replaces it.

### Part 3 Question 1

**Question:**  
How might AI change education in the next ten years?

**普通答案思路：**  
个性化学习、教师角色变化、自动反馈，但也有公平和依赖问题。

**7 分答案结构：**  
趋势预测 + 好处 + 风险 + 条件。

**高分表达：**

- AI may make education more personalised, especially for students who need extra support.
- Teachers may spend less time on repetitive tasks and more time guiding students.
- The risk is that students may outsource too much of their thinking.
- The impact will depend on how carefully schools design the rules.

### Part 3 Question 2

**Question:**  
Should young children use AI-powered toys?

**普通答案思路：**  
谨慎。玩具可以互动，但儿童可能分不清机器和真实关系。

**7 分答案结构：**  
年龄敏感性 + 心理发展 + 家长监督 + 限制。

**高分表达：**

- Young children are still developing their social and emotional understanding.
- If a toy sounds too human, children may trust it in an unhealthy way.
- Parents should know what data the toy collects and how it responds.
- I would support limited use, but not unsupervised use.

### Part 3 Question 3

**Question:**  
Is banning technology in schools a realistic solution?

**普通答案思路：**  
完全禁止不现实，但可以限制场景和年龄。

**7 分答案结构：**  
否定极端方案 + 提出替代方案 + 解释原因。

**高分表达：**

- A total ban sounds simple, but it may not be practical.
- A better approach is to teach students when and how to use technology.
- Schools need clear guardrails rather than vague encouragement.
- Students should learn both the benefits and the limitations of digital tools.

### Part 3 Question 4

**Question:**  
Who should be responsible for teaching children how to use AI safely?

**普通答案思路：**  
学校、家长、政府、科技公司都有责任。

**7 分答案结构：**  
多方责任 + 分工 + 重点责任方。

**高分表达：**

- It should be a shared responsibility.
- Schools can teach critical thinking and digital literacy.
- Parents can set habits and boundaries at home.
- Technology companies should be required to design safer products for children.
