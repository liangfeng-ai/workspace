## 2. 阅读训练：从“看懂”到“雅思阅读速度”

### 中文摘要
这篇文章讨论儿童的“认知耐力”是否能够训练。作者基于印度 1,636 名小学生的研究指出，持续专注像体力一样可以通过练习增强。无论是做数学题还是玩拼图，只要任务要求连续用脑，学生在阅读、推理和数学测试中的表现下降都会变慢，老师也观察到他们更少分心、能更好完成多步骤指令。研究还发现，来自弱势背景的孩子更容易在长时间任务中掉速，因此学校如果能增加有挑战性的专注练习，可能有助于缩小教育差距。核心结论是：持续专注不是天生固定的，而是可以通过训练逐步建立起来的。

### 核心词汇
| 英文 | 中文 | 原文语境 | 可替换表达 |
|---|---|---|---|
| cognitive endurance | 认知耐力，持续专注力 | 文章主标题核心概念 | mental stamina / sustained attention |
| sustain mental effort | 持续保持脑力 | 指长时间维持思考和专注 | keep concentrating / maintain focus |
| physical stamina | 体力耐力 | 作者把脑力训练类比为体能训练 | endurance / staying power |
| disadvantaged backgrounds | 弱势背景 | 指资源较少、机会较少的学生群体 | less privileged groups |
| deliberate practice | 刻意练习 | 指有目标、有重复的训练 | focused training / structured practice |
| passive activities | 被动活动 | 指听讲、抄写等低主动参与活动 | low-engagement tasks |
| multistep instructions | 多步骤指令 | 老师观察学生是否能跟上复杂任务 | step-by-step directions |
| level the playing field | 缩小差距，让竞争更公平 | 指教育机会更均衡 | equalize opportunities / reduce inequality |

### 长难句拆解
句子：  
**When schools give students repeated opportunities to concentrate on demanding tasks, their ability to sustain attention usually improves, which can later help them perform better across subjects.**

- 句子主干：`their ability ... improves`
- 前半部分 `When schools give students repeated opportunities...` 是条件状语，说明“在什么情况下会提升”
- `which can later help...` 是非限制性定语从句，补充结果
- 逻辑关系：条件 -> 能力提升 -> 跨学科表现更好
- 可模仿表达：  
  `When people get repeated chances to practice X, their ability to Y usually improves, which can later help them achieve better results in Z.`

## 4. 口语训练：从中式表达到 IELTS 7.0

### Part 1
**1. Do you find it easy to stay focused for a long time?**  
- 普通答案思路：先说“有时可以，有时不行”，再举学习或刷手机分心的例子。  
- 7 分答案结构：结论 -> 原因 -> 例子 -> 小结。  
- 可直接套用：  
  - `I can focus for a while, but my concentration drops if the task feels repetitive.`  
  - `What helps most is having a clear goal and a short time limit.`

**2. What helps you study better?**  
- 普通答案思路：说环境、计划、音乐、番茄钟。  
- 7 分答案结构：工具/习惯 -> 为什么有效 -> 一个真实例子。  
- 可直接套用：  
  - `A distraction-free environment makes a huge difference.`  
  - `I tend to work better when the task is broken into small steps.`

**3. Do people in your country care about making eco-friendly choices?**  
- 普通答案思路：先回答“越来越多/还不够”，再说日常行为。  
- 7 分答案结构：总体判断 -> 变化趋势 -> 具体例子。  
- 可直接套用：  
  - `People are becoming more aware, but convenience still matters a lot.`  
  - `They are more likely to act when the green option also feels practical.`

### Part 2 Cue Card
**Describe a habit you changed because it made your life better.**

- 普通答案思路：讲一个从“勉强”到“愿意坚持”的习惯，比如早睡、少刷手机、规律运动、骑车通勤。
- 7 分答案结构：是什么习惯 -> 为什么开始改变 -> 具体怎么做 -> 改变后的感受和结果 -> 一句总结
- 可直接套用的高分表达：  
  - `I used to..., but over time I realized that...`  
  - `What surprised me most was that it made me feel...`  
  - `It turned out to be sustainable because...`  
  - `The real benefit was not only..., but also...`

### Part 3
**1. Why do many people resist good habits even when they know they are useful?**  
- 普通答案思路：觉得麻烦、见效慢、需要自律。  
- 7 分答案结构：心理原因 -> 现实成本 -> 解决思路。  
- 可直接套用：  
  - `People often reject changes that feel costly in the short term.`  
  - `Knowing something is good is not the same as finding it easy to do.`

**2. Is enjoyment more important than discipline for long-term change?**  
- 普通答案思路：两者都重要，但享受感更容易坚持。  
- 7 分答案结构：先平衡判断 -> 再解释优先级 -> 举例。  
- 可直接套用：  
  - `Discipline gets you started, but enjoyment keeps you going.`  
  - `If a habit feels rewarding, it becomes much easier to maintain.`

**3. Should schools train students’ attention explicitly?**  
- 普通答案思路：应该，因为现在分心太多。  
- 7 分答案结构：立场 -> 教育现实 -> 课堂收益。  
- 可直接套用：  
  - `Attention is a skill, not just a personality trait.`  
  - `Schools should treat concentration as something that can be trained.`

**4. How can governments encourage behavior change without using guilt?**  
- 普通答案思路：奖励、便利、默认选项、正向宣传。  
- 7 分答案结构：机制 -> 公共政策例子 -> 结果。  
- 可直接套用：  
  - `Policies work better when the better choice is also the easier choice.`  
  - `Positive framing is usually more persuasive than moral pressure.`
