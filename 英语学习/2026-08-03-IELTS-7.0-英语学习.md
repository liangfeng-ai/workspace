## 2. 阅读训练：从“看懂”到“雅思阅读速度”

### 文章中文摘要

这份 2026 年数字新闻报告指出，全球新闻消费正在进一步“平台化”：社交媒体和视频网络已经成为许多人获取新闻的主要渠道，甚至在总体使用率上超过了电视新闻和新闻机构自有网站/应用。年轻人尤其依赖社交媒体、视频平台和 AI 聊天机器人来接触新闻，而年长群体仍更倾向于电视等传统渠道。报告并不简单地说人们主动选择了社交媒体新闻，而是强调很多新闻接触是“顺带发生”的：用户本来在平台上做别的事，结果被算法推送到新闻内容。与此同时，AI chatbot 用于新闻的比例正在增长，但还没有爆发式普及，信任度仍明显低于对新闻整体的信任。对新闻机构而言，挑战不只是把内容搬到新平台，而是如何在流量下降、用户注意力分散、AI 分发兴起的情况下，重新建立可信度、忠诚度和深度参与。文章适合练习数据定位、趋势判断和观点归纳。

### 8 个核心词汇或短语

| 英文 | 中文 | 原文语境 | 可替换表达 |
|---|---|---|---|
| platformisation | 平台化 | 新闻消费越来越依赖第三方平台 | platform-driven consumption |
| third-party sources | 第三方来源 | 社交媒体、视频网络、AI chatbot 等 | external platforms |
| news consumption | 新闻消费/获取新闻的方式 | 报告分析人们如何接触新闻 | news use / media consumption |
| diffusion | 扩散，普及 | AI 在日常生活和新闻中扩散 | spread / adoption |
| intermediated | 被中介平台介入的 | 新闻通过平台而非官网触达用户 | platform-mediated |
| referral traffic | 推荐/跳转流量 | 搜索引擎给新闻网站带来的访问量 | incoming traffic |
| trustworthiness | 可信度 | 用户是否相信 AI chatbot 的新闻答案 | credibility / reliability |
| loyal audience | 忠实受众 | 新闻机构更重视深度参与的用户 | committed readership |

### 答案与解析

1. **True**。文章明确说社交媒体和视频网络在全球平均层面已经超过新闻机构自有网站和应用。
2. **False**。AI chatbot 新闻使用在增长，但信任度仍低于整体新闻信任度。
3. **Not Given**。文章讨论年轻人是否会继承父母新闻习惯，但没有给出“definitely”的确定结论。
4. **B**。关键逻辑是传统渠道下降，而社交媒体/视频平台成为默认接触新闻的场景。
5. **B**。报告提到搜索、AI Overview、AI chatbot 等可能影响新闻网站流量。
6. **traditional sources**
7. **video networks**
8. **trustworthiness**

### 长难句拆解

**句子**

“Although the use of AI chatbots for news is growing, it does not yet appear to be exploding, partly because many users remain uncertain about whether chatbot-generated answers are trustworthy.”

**结构拆解**

- Although 引导让步状语从句：`Although the use of AI chatbots for news is growing`
- 主句：`it does not yet appear to be exploding`
- 原因补充：`partly because many users remain uncertain`
- about 后接宾语从句：`whether chatbot-generated answers are trustworthy`

**逻辑关系**

先承认趋势：AI chatbot 新闻使用在增长；再限制判断：增长不是爆发式；最后解释原因：可信度仍是障碍。

**可模仿表达**

- Although X is growing, it does not yet appear to be transforming Y.
- This is partly because users remain uncertain about whether...
- The trend is significant, but it should be interpreted with caution.

### 易听错点提醒

- 连读：`spend too much time` 容易听成一串，注意 `spend-too-much-time`。
- 弱读：`of`, `to`, `and`, `that` 通常很轻，不要只听实词。
- 近音：`screen time` 和 `screentime` 听起来几乎一样，写作时统一写 `screen time`。
- 数字：`under 16s` 要听清是年龄限制，不是数量。
- 国家名：`Australia` 可能出现在政策背景中，注意其后动作 `banned access`。
- 转折信号：注意 `but`, `so`, `well`, `yes, and`，这些词常提示话题推进。

### 听后复述模板：80-120 词英文

Today’s programme discusses the issue of children’s screen time. The speakers explain that screen time refers to the time people spend using devices such as smartphones, tablets and laptops. Many parents are worried that children are spending too much time on these devices, especially on social media. The programme also mentions that Australia has introduced restrictions for under-16s, while other countries are considering similar measures. In my view, the key point is not that technology is always harmful, but that children need clearer boundaries and healthier habits so that screens do not replace sleep, study, exercise or face-to-face communication.

## 4. 口语训练：从中式表达到 IELTS 7.0

### Part 1 问题 1

**Question:** How often do you use social media to get news?

**普通答案思路：** 说频率 + 常用平台 + 为什么方便。

**7 分答案结构：** 频率 → 具体场景 → 优点 → 小缺点。

**高分表达：**

- I check social media for news on a daily basis.
- It is convenient, but not always reliable.
- I usually cross-check important stories with more established sources.

**示范答案：**

I use social media for news almost every day, especially when I’m commuting or taking short breaks. It’s convenient because headlines reach me very quickly, but I don’t fully trust everything I see there. If a story seems important, I usually cross-check it with more established news sources.

### Part 1 问题 2

**Question:** Do you think young people read newspapers nowadays?

**普通答案思路：** 很少读纸质报纸，但可能读电子新闻。

**7 分答案结构：** 直接回答 → 区分纸媒和数字新闻 → 原因。

**高分表达：**

- printed newspapers are no longer part of their daily routine
- they still consume news, just in different formats
- short videos and news feeds feel more accessible

### Part 1 问题 3

**Question:** Do you prefer watching news videos or reading articles?

**普通答案思路：** 说偏好 + 原因。

**7 分答案结构：** 偏好 → 任务场景区分 → 对学习英语的影响。

**高分表达：**

- It depends on the purpose.
- Videos are easier to digest, but articles give me more depth.
- Reading forces me to process details more carefully.

### Part 2 Cue Card

**Describe a time when you learned something useful from an online source.**

You should say:

- what you learned
- where you found the information
- why you chose that source
- and explain how it helped you

**普通答案思路：** 讲一次用 YouTube / 新闻网站 / 网课学到技能或知识。

**7 分答案结构：**

1. 背景：你当时需要解决什么问题  
2. 来源：为什么选择这个平台  
3. 内容：学到了什么  
4. 结果：对学习/生活有什么帮助  
5. 反思：线上学习的优缺点

**高分表达：**

- I came across a well-explained video/article about...
- What made it useful was that it broke down a complex idea into manageable parts.
- It saved me a lot of trial and error.
- However, I had to be selective because online information varies in quality.

### Part 3 深度追问 1

**Question:** How has social media changed the way people consume news?

**普通答案思路：** 更快、更碎片化、更依赖算法。

**7 分答案结构：** 总观点 → 两个变化 → 风险 → 平衡观点。

**高分表达：**

- It has made news consumption faster but more fragmented.
- People are exposed to news incidentally rather than deliberately.
- The downside is that algorithms may amplify emotional or misleading content.

### Part 3 深度追问 2

**Question:** Should governments limit children’s access to social media?

**普通答案思路：** 可以限制，但不能只靠政府。

**7 分答案结构：** 条件性同意 → 保护理由 → 执行难点 → 家庭和学校责任。

**高分表达：**

- Some level of regulation is justified.
- A blanket ban may be difficult to enforce.
- Digital literacy should go hand in hand with restrictions.

### Part 3 深度追问 3

**Question:** Can AI chatbots become a reliable source of news?

**普通答案思路：** 有潜力，但要透明和核查。

**7 分答案结构：** 承认效率 → 指出风险 → 提出条件。

**高分表达：**

- They can be useful as a starting point, but not as the final authority.
- Reliability depends on source transparency and fact-checking.
- Users need to know where the information comes from.

### Part 3 深度追问 4

**Question:** Why do younger and older people prefer different news sources?

**普通答案思路：** 成长环境不同、习惯不同、信任对象不同。

**7 分答案结构：** 代际差异 → 技术习惯 → 信任模式 → 例子。

**高分表达：**

- It largely comes down to media habits formed early in life.
- Younger people value speed and accessibility.
- Older people may associate traditional broadcasters with authority and credibility.
