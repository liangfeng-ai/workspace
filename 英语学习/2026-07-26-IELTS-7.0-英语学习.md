## 2. 阅读训练：从“看懂”到“雅思阅读速度”

**文章中文摘要**

这篇文章报道了美国南部和中部正在经历本月第三次大范围“热穹顶”天气，约 7000 万人面临高温警报。所谓 heat dome，是指高压大气系统像盖子一样把热空气困在某一区域，使高温持续数天甚至数周。文章提到，部分地区气温预计达到 95-105°F，西南沙漠地区甚至可能接近 120°F。作者不仅描述了本次热浪的范围和强度，还把它放在更大的气候背景下分析：虽然美国夏季热浪并不罕见，但近年来热浪的频率、严重程度、夜间高温和湿度都在增加。文章强调，高温是美国最致命的天气现象之一，老年人、孕妇、儿童和有基础疾病的人尤其脆弱。与此同时，欧洲也在经历危险高温和野火，说明极端热事件已成为全球性公共健康与气候风险问题。

**长难句拆解**

句子：*The elevated heat is expected to linger into the coming week, held in place by the heat dome.*

结构拆解：

- 主干：**The elevated heat is expected to linger**  
  “升高的热量预计会持续。”
- 时间状语：**into the coming week**  
  “持续到接下来的一周。”
- 过去分词结构：**held in place by the heat dome**  
  补充说明原因：热量被 heat dome 固定、困住。

逻辑关系：  
这是“现象 + 预测 + 原因”的新闻写法。先说高温会持续，再解释为什么持续。

可模仿表达：

- The problem is expected to worsen, driven by rising demand.
- The trend is likely to continue, supported by strong consumer interest.
- The risk may increase, intensified by poor planning and limited resources.

## 4. 口语训练：从中式表达到 IELTS 7.0

### Part 1 问题 1

**Question:** How much time do you spend on your phone every day?

普通答案思路：  
说一个大概时间，说明主要用途，比如学习、聊天、看视频。

7 分答案结构：  
直接回答 + 区分工作/娱乐用途 + 简短反思。

高分表达：

- I probably spend more time on my phone than I would like to admit.
- Most of it is practical, but some of it is just mindless scrolling.
- I’m trying to be more intentional about how I use it.

### Part 1 问题 2

**Question:** Do you think children should have their own smartphones?

普通答案思路：  
可以说不应太早拥有，但可以在安全需要时使用。

7 分答案结构：  
观点 + 条件限制 + 原因。

高分表达：

- I don’t think there should be a one-size-fits-all answer.
- It depends on the child’s age, maturity and daily routine.
- A phone can be useful, but unrestricted access can be harmful.

### Part 1 问题 3

**Question:** What do you usually do to reduce your screen time?

普通答案思路：  
说关通知、把手机放远、设定时间限制。

7 分答案结构：  
具体方法 + 效果 + 仍然存在的困难。

高分表达：

- I turn off non-essential notifications.
- I keep my phone out of reach when I need to focus.
- The hardest part is breaking the habit of checking it automatically.

### Part 2 Cue Card

**Describe a time when you tried to reduce your use of digital devices.**

You should say:

- when it was
- why you wanted to do it
- what you did
- and explain whether it was successful

普通答案思路：  
讲一次备考、睡前、旅行或工作期间减少手机使用的经历。

7 分答案结构：

1. 背景：I realised I was spending too much time...
2. 原因：It affected my concentration / sleep / mood.
3. 措施：I set app limits, removed distracting apps, and put my phone away.
4. 结果：It was difficult at first, but I became more focused.

高分表达：

- I had fallen into the habit of checking my phone every few minutes.
- It was affecting my ability to concentrate for a sustained period of time.
- I didn’t quit completely; I just created clearer boundaries.
- The biggest benefit was that I felt less mentally scattered.

### Part 3 深度追问 1

**Question:** Why do many people find it difficult to control their screen time?

普通答案思路：  
因为手机方便、算法吸引人、社交压力大。

7 分答案结构：  
个人习惯 + 平台设计 + 社会压力。

高分表达：

- Digital platforms are designed to capture and hold our attention.
- People often underestimate how addictive short-form content can be.
- There is also social pressure to be constantly available.

### Part 3 深度追问 2

**Question:** Should governments regulate children’s access to social media?

普通答案思路：  
应该适度监管，但不能只靠政府。

7 分答案结构：  
支持监管 + 承认执行困难 + 强调家庭和学校责任。

高分表达：

- Some regulation is necessary, especially when children’s wellbeing is at stake.
- However, rules alone cannot replace parental guidance and digital education.
- The challenge is to protect children without cutting them off from useful technology.

### Part 3 深度追问 3

**Question:** How has technology changed the way young people learn?

普通答案思路：  
资源更多，但注意力更分散。

7 分答案结构：  
积极影响 + 消极影响 + 平衡观点。

高分表达：

- Technology has made learning more flexible and accessible.
- At the same time, it has shortened many students’ attention spans.
- The key is not to reject technology, but to use it purposefully.

### Part 3 深度追问 4

**Question:** Do you think people will use screens less in the future?

普通答案思路：  
不一定，可能更多，但方式会改变。

7 分答案结构：  
预测 + 原因 + 条件。

高分表达：

- I doubt screen use will decrease dramatically.
- What may change is the way people manage their digital habits.
- If awareness grows, people may become more selective about their screen time.

- Pronunciation：`screen time`, `social media`, `responsibility`, `technology` 是否清楚？
- Idea development：是否有具体例子，而不是只说抽象观点？
