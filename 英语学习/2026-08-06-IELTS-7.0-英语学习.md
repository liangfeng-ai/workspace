## 2. 阅读训练：从“看懂”到“雅思阅读速度”

### 文章中文摘要

这篇 Guardian 文章讨论了美国学校中使用生成式 AI 所引发的争议。科技公司和部分教育倡导者认为，AI 可以帮助教师提高教学效率，支持个性化学习，并让学生掌握未来职场所需的 AI 素养。例如，一些平台可以给学生写作反馈，或帮助有阅读障碍的学生把文字转换成语音。然而，越来越多家长、教育专家和儿童发展研究者担心，AI 进入课堂过快，缺乏足够证据证明它真的能提升学习效果。反对者尤其关注“认知外包”：学生可能把思考、修改、分析等核心学习过程交给机器，从而削弱批判性思维和基础学术能力。文章还提到，美国一些家长组织和地方教育机构正在推动暂停或限制学生直接使用生成式 AI，尤其是在小学阶段。争论的核心不是“是否完全禁止技术”，而是学校应如何在 AI 素养、儿童发展、证据基础、家庭参与和学习质量之间找到平衡。

### 8 个核心词汇或短语

| 英文 | 中文 | 原文语境 | 可替换表达 |
|---|---|---|---|
| generative AI | 生成式人工智能 | 学校让学生使用 Gemini、MagicSchool 等工具完成反馈或学习任务 | AI-powered content tools / large language models |
| cognitive off-loading | 认知外包 | 专家担心学生借助外部工具逃避思考 | outsourcing thinking / mental outsourcing |
| critical thinking | 批判性思维 | 文章担心 AI 依赖可能削弱学生分析和判断能力 | analytical reasoning / independent judgment |
| moratorium | 暂停令；暂缓实施 | 家长组织要求学校暂停学生直接使用生成式 AI | temporary ban / suspension |
| student-facing tools | 面向学生的工具 | 一些组织反对低龄学生直接接触 AI 聊天机器人 | tools used directly by students |
| evidence-based | 基于证据的 | 学区被要求移除缺乏证据支持的教育技术 | research-backed / empirically supported |
| foundational academic skills | 基础学术能力 | 反对者认为学校应先培养阅读、写作、判断等基本能力 | core learning skills / basic academic competence |
| AI literacy | AI 素养 | 支持者认为学生需要理解 AI 的工作方式和局限 | digital literacy / technological competence |

#### Question 5: Summary Completion

Complete the summary using words from the box.

**Box**：moratorium / evidence / creativity / parents / entertainment / critical

The article describes a debate in which some schools and technology companies promote AI as useful for learning, while many ________ and experts are worried about its impact. Their concerns include weak ________ for learning benefits and possible harm to students’ ________ thinking. Some groups have therefore called for a temporary ________ on student-facing AI tools.

### 答案与解析

1. **True**  
解析：文章中的家长明确担心，AI 会让孩子认为机器可以替他们思考，核心是“思考过程被替代”。

2. **False**  
解析：文章提到一些家长、组织和地方机构呼吁暂停或限制，但并不是全美国学校都已经禁止。

3. **B**  
解析：cognitive off-loading 指学生借助外部工具避免脑力劳动，因此 B 最准确。

4. **C**  
解析：支持者提到 AI 可以帮助有 dyslexia 等学习困难的学生，比如文字转语音。

5. **parents / evidence / critical / moratorium**  
解析：summary 对应文章主线：家长和专家担忧；证据不足；批判性思维受影响；提出暂停令。

### 长难句拆解

**句子**：  
While technology companies argue that AI can make learning more personalised and efficient, many parents and experts warn that using it too early may weaken the mental effort children need to develop independent judgment.

**结构拆解**：  
- **While technology companies argue that...**：让步状语从句，表示“虽然科技公司认为……”  
- **AI can make learning more personalised and efficient**：argue 的宾语从句，说明支持方观点  
- **many parents and experts warn that...**：主句，说明反对方观点  
- **using it too early may weaken...**：warn 的宾语从句，核心意思是“过早使用 AI 可能削弱……”  
- **children need to develop independent judgment**：定语从句，修饰 mental effort，意思是“孩子发展独立判断所需要的脑力努力”

**可模仿表达**：  
While some people argue that X can make Y more efficient, others warn that it may weaken Z.  
例句：While some people argue that online learning can make education more flexible, others warn that it may weaken students’ self-discipline.

### 易听错点提醒

- **连读**：take over our jobs 中 take over 容易连成 /teikouvər/。  
- **弱读**：can produce new things 里的 can 常弱读，不要听成 cannot。  
- **近音词**：work / walk, job / jump, skill / school 要靠上下文判断。  
- **长数字**：2009, 2019, five or ten years, fifty years 这类年份和时间跨度要单独记。  
- **转折信号**：but, however, in the longer run, at the moment 后面常出现真正观点。  
- **抽象名词**：implications, economy, employment, automation 不一定每个音都听清，但要抓住重音。

## 4. 口语训练：从中式表达到 IELTS 7.0

### Part 1 Question 1

**Question**：Do you often use AI tools in your studies?

**普通答案思路**：  
说你会用 AI 查资料、改表达、练口语，但不会完全依赖它。

**7 分答案结构**：  
直接回答 + 具体用途 + 限制意识 + 个人态度

**高分表达**：  
- I use it as a learning assistant rather than a shortcut.  
- It helps me polish my language, but I still need to do the thinking myself.  
- I try not to outsource the whole learning process to AI.

### Part 1 Question 2

**Question**：What subjects are easier to learn with technology?

**普通答案思路**：  
语言、编程、数学可能更适合，因为有即时反馈和大量资源。

**7 分答案结构**：  
举 1-2 个学科 + 解释技术优势 + 补充局限

**高分表达**：  
- Technology is particularly useful for subjects that require repeated practice.  
- Instant feedback can make the learning process more efficient.  
- However, it cannot replace deep understanding or classroom discussion.

### Part 1 Question 3

**Question**：Do you think children should use digital devices at school?

**普通答案思路**：  
可以适度使用，但低龄儿童要限制，重点是学习目的。

**7 分答案结构**：  
平衡观点 + 年龄差异 + 使用边界

**高分表达**：  
- It depends on the age of the students and the purpose of using the device.  
- For younger children, face-to-face interaction is still essential.  
- Digital tools should support learning, not dominate the classroom.

### Part 2 Cue Card

**Describe a useful technology you have used for learning.**

You should say:  
- what the technology is  
- when you started using it  
- how it helps you learn  
- and explain whether you think it has any disadvantages

**普通答案思路**：  
讲 AI 词典、ChatGPT、听力 App 或网课平台。重点说它如何帮你查词、纠错、练发音，但也容易让人懒得思考。

**7 分答案结构**：  
1. 简短介绍技术  
2. 描述具体学习场景  
3. 说明效果  
4. 评价优缺点，体现批判性

**高分表达**：  
- One tool that has genuinely changed the way I study is...  
- It gives me immediate feedback, which is something I cannot always get from a teacher.  
- The downside is that it can make me over-dependent if I use it uncritically.  
- I see it as a supplement to traditional learning, not a replacement for it.

### Part 3 Question 1

**Question**：How might AI change education in the next ten years?

**普通答案思路**：  
AI 会让学习更个性化，老师可以减轻重复工作，但也会带来作弊和依赖问题。

**7 分答案结构**：  
趋势预测 + 好处 + 风险 + 条件

**高分表达**：  
- AI is likely to make education more personalised and data-driven.  
- Teachers may spend less time on routine marking and more time on mentoring.  
- The real challenge is how to prevent students from becoming passive users of technology.

### Part 3 Question 2

**Question**：Should schools ban students from using AI?

**普通答案思路**：  
不应完全禁止，但低龄学生要限制，高年级学生应学习负责任使用。

**7 分答案结构**：  
反对绝对化 + 区分年龄 + 提出规则

**高分表达**：  
- A complete ban may be unrealistic, but unrestricted use would also be risky.  
- Schools need clear boundaries rather than vague warnings.  
- Students should be taught when AI use is acceptable and when it becomes academic dishonesty.

### Part 3 Question 3

**Question**：What skills will be more valuable in an AI-driven workplace?

**普通答案思路**：  
创造力、沟通、批判性思维、学习能力、判断力更重要。

**7 分答案结构**：  
列举技能 + 解释原因 + 举例

**高分表达**：  
- Routine knowledge is becoming less valuable, while judgment is becoming more important.  
- People who can ask better questions may get better results from AI.  
- Human skills such as empathy, negotiation and leadership will be harder to automate.

### Part 3 Question 4

**Question**：Do you think AI will increase or reduce inequality in education?

**普通答案思路**：  
两种可能都有。资源好的学生会用得更好，资源差的学校可能被低质量工具替代。

**7 分答案结构**：  
双边讨论 + 解释机制 + 给出判断

**高分表达**：  
- In theory, AI could make high-quality support more accessible.  
- In practice, it may widen the gap between students who use it wisely and those who use it passively.  
- The impact depends heavily on teacher guidance and school policy.
