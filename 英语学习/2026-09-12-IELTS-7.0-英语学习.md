## 2. 阅读训练：从“看懂”到“雅思阅读速度”

### 文章中文摘要 150-250 词

这篇文章介绍了一项关于气候“反馈循环”的新研究。传统讨论全球变暖时，人们往往关注人类燃烧煤炭、石油和天然气所产生的直接排放；但这项研究指出，随着地球升温，自然系统本身也可能释放更多温室气体，从而进一步推动升温。例如，高纬度地区的永久冻土融化会释放被封存的碳；森林变干后更容易发生更猛烈的野火；湿地、湖泊等生态系统升温后也可能释放更多甲烷和二氧化碳。研究认为，这些自然来源的额外排放可能到本世纪末将全球变暖放大约 20%-30%。文章强调，即使这些变化听起来只是零点几摄氏度，也会显著增加热浪、洪水、干旱等极端天气风险。研究人员认为，目前许多气候模型和政策规划没有充分纳入这些“隐藏倍增器”，因此可能低估未来升温幅度。文章最后指出，快速削减化石燃料排放仍然至关重要，因为人类减排越早，触发这些自然反馈的风险越低。

### 8 个核心词汇或短语

| 英文 | 中文 | 原文语境 | 可替换表达 |
|---|---|---|---|
| feedback loops | 反馈循环 | 指升温触发自然系统释放更多温室气体，而这些气体又继续加剧升温 | self-reinforcing cycles / reinforcing mechanisms |
| amplify global warming | 放大全球变暖 | 自然排放可能使原本的升温幅度进一步增加 | intensify warming / worsen heating |
| natural sources | 自然来源 | 与人类直接排放相对，指永久冻土、湿地、湖泊、野火等来源 | non-human sources / ecosystem-based emissions |

### 5 道 IELTS 阅读题型练习

#### Questions 1-3：True / False / Not Given

判断下列说法是否与文章信息一致。

1. The study argues that emissions from natural systems could make global heating worse.  
2. The article says natural feedback loops have already been fully included in all climate models.  
3. According to the article, cutting fossil fuel emissions can still reduce the scale of extra warming from natural sources.

#### Questions 4-5：Multiple Choice

4. What is the main function of examples such as permafrost, wildfires and wetlands in the article?

A. To show that climate change only affects remote regions  
B. To explain how warming can trigger further greenhouse gas release  
C. To argue that human emissions are no longer important  
D. To prove that climate models are always inaccurate

5. What is the writer’s overall message?

A. Climate feedbacks make action unnecessary because warming is inevitable.  
B. Climate models are useless and should be abandoned.  
C. Hidden natural feedbacks may mean future warming is underestimated, so faster action is needed.  
D. Only methane matters in future climate policy.

### 答案与解析

1. **True**  
   解析：文章核心观点就是自然系统释放的 methane 和 CO₂ 会进一步加剧气候变暖。

2. **False**  
   解析：文章明确指出这些自然来源的额外排放在很多气候预测中没有被充分计算，而不是“已经完全纳入”。

3. **True**  
   解析：文章提到，快速削减化石燃料污染仍能减少由自然景观产生的额外排放。

4. **B**  
   解析：permafrost、wildfires、wetlands 都是为了说明“升温 → 自然系统变化 → 更多温室气体 → 继续升温”的因果链。

5. **C**  
   解析：文章不是说行动无用，而是强调现有模型可能低估风险，因此需要更快减排。

### 长难句拆解

**改写参考句**：  
As temperatures rise, natural systems such as thawing permafrost and warming wetlands may release additional greenhouse gases, which could intensify climate change beyond what current models predict.

**结构拆解**：

- **As temperatures rise**：时间/背景状语，表示“随着气温上升”
- **natural systems ... may release additional greenhouse gases**：主句，核心信息是“自然系统可能释放更多温室气体”
- **such as thawing permafrost and warming wetlands**：举例说明 natural systems
- **which could intensify climate change**：非限定性定语从句，补充说明这些气体的结果
- **beyond what current models predict**：介词短语，表示“超过当前模型预测的范围”

**逻辑关系**：  
背景变化 → 自然系统反应 → 额外排放 → 预测被低估

**可模仿表达**：

- As X increases, Y may trigger Z, which could worsen...
- These effects are often underestimated in current projections.
- Even a small rise in temperature can significantly increase the risk of...

### 易听错点提醒

- **连读**：  
  - *spend too much time on screens* 中 *time on* 容易连成 /taɪmɒn/  
  - *looking at their phones* 中 *looking at* 容易弱化
- **弱读**：  
  - *are introducing* 中 *are* 可能很轻  
  - *the amount of time* 中 *of* 常弱读成 /əv/ 或 /ə/
- **近音词**：  
  - *screen* vs *scream*  
  - *access* vs *excess*  
  - *measure* vs *major*
- **转折信号**：注意 *but, however, not just..., rather than...*，这些词后面通常是观点重点。
- **数字与政策表达**：如果听到年龄、小时数、国家名称，要单独记下，不要只记大意。
- **人名地名**：Neil、Becca、UK、Australia、China 这类词容易在快语速中被忽略。

## 4. 口语训练：从中式表达到 IELTS 7.0

今日口语主题：**Technology, children and climate awareness**

### Part 1 问题 1

**Question**：How much time do you usually spend on your phone every day?

- **普通答案思路**：说大概时间 + 用手机做什么 + 是否觉得太多。
- **7 分答案结构**：直接回答 → 解释用途 → 加一个反思。
- **高分表达**：
  - I’d say I spend roughly... hours a day on my phone.
  - A large proportion of that time is spent on...
  - I’m trying to be more intentional about my screen use.

**示范答案**：  
I’d say I spend roughly four or five hours a day on my phone, although not all of it is wasted. A large proportion of that time is spent on messaging, reading news and studying English. That said, I do think I sometimes scroll without a clear purpose, so I’m trying to be more intentional about my screen use.

### Part 1 问题 2

**Question**：Do you think technology helps students learn better?

- **普通答案思路**：有帮助，但取决于怎么用。
- **7 分答案结构**：给出平衡观点 → 举例 → 总结条件。
- **高分表达**：
  - It depends on how it is used.
  - Digital tools can make learning more flexible.
  - Without self-discipline, technology can become a distraction.

**示范答案**：  
Yes, but it depends on how it is used. Digital tools can make learning more flexible because students can watch lectures, look up information and practise listening anytime. However, without self-discipline, technology can easily become a distraction, especially when social media notifications keep interrupting students.

### Part 1 问题 3

**Question**：Are people in your country concerned about environmental problems?

- **普通答案思路**：越来越关注，但行动不一定足够。
- **7 分答案结构**：趋势 → 例子 → 评价。
- **高分表达**：
  - Public awareness has grown significantly.
  - Extreme weather has made the issue feel more urgent.
  - There is still a gap between awareness and action.

**示范答案**：  
Yes, public awareness has grown significantly in recent years. For example, more people talk about extreme heat, air pollution and recycling. Extreme weather has made the issue feel more urgent. However, I think there is still a gap between awareness and action, because many people know the problem but do not change their daily habits.

### Part 3 深度追问 1

**Question**：Should governments limit children’s use of social media?

- **普通答案思路**：应该有一定限制，但不能只靠禁令。
- **7 分答案结构**：立场 → 原因 → 让步 → 解决方案。
- **高分表达**：
  - A blanket ban may be too simplistic.
  - Children need protection from addictive design and harmful content.
  - Digital literacy should be taught alongside regulation.

### Part 3 深度追问 2

**Question**：Do you think schools rely too much on digital devices?

- **普通答案思路**：有些学校确实过度依赖。
- **7 分答案结构**：承认好处 → 指出风险 → 给出平衡方案。
- **高分表达**：
  - Technology should support learning rather than replace thinking.
  - Over-reliance on screens may reduce students’ attention span.
  - The key is purposeful integration.

### Part 3 深度追问 3

**Question**：Why do many people fail to take climate change seriously?

- **普通答案思路**：问题太大、太远、个人感觉无力。
- **7 分答案结构**：心理原因 → 信息原因 → 现实压力。
- **高分表达**：
  - The consequences can feel distant and abstract.
  - People tend to prioritise immediate concerns.
  - Scientific warnings need to be translated into everyday language.

### Part 3 深度追问 4

**Question**：Can technology solve environmental problems?

- **普通答案思路**：能帮忙，但不能单独解决。
- **7 分答案结构**：技术贡献 → 局限 → 需要政策和行为改变。
- **高分表达**：
  - Technology is part of the solution, but not a magic bullet.
  - Renewable energy and better data can accelerate change.
  - Systemic change requires policy, investment and public participation.
