## 2. 阅读训练：从“看懂”到“雅思阅读速度”

**文章中文摘要，约 190 词**

这篇文章讨论美国学校中引入生成式 AI 所引发的争议。科技公司和部分政府、教育机构认为，AI 可以提高课堂效率，帮助学生获得未来工作所需的 AI 素养，也可能为有阅读障碍等学习困难的学生提供支持。然而，许多家长、儿童发展专家和神经科学研究者担心，AI 可能让学生过早依赖机器完成思考，削弱写作、问题解决和批判性思维能力。文章提到，纽约和俄勒冈等地的家长组织要求暂停学校中面向学生的 AI 工具，一些教育团体也建议限制小学生直接使用生成式 AI。支持者则认为，完全禁止并不现实，学生应该学习 AI 的工作方式和局限，从而更批判地使用工具。文章的核心不是简单反对技术，而是追问：学校引入 AI 时，究竟是在帮助学生形成持久能力，还是只是让他们更快完成任务？

The debate is not only about whether schools should use technology, but whether AI supports ______ learning. Critics worry that students may develop ______ on AI tools instead of building their own reasoning skills.

**答案与解析**

1. True。文章开头家长 Kelly Clancy 明确担心，AI 会让孩子觉得机器可以替他们思考。  
2. False。文章提到一些城市和组织呼吁暂停或限制，但并没有说全美国学校都已禁止。

7. D。American Federation of Teachers 建议从小学移除面向学生的 AI 工具。

**长难句拆解**

句子：While big tech and the Trump administration have pushed teachers to use AI and claimed it helps students learn and gives them the skills needed to succeed in a world in which the technology is ubiquitous, some parents and child development experts argue that there is little evidence that AI helps children and may even harm their cognitive development.

结构拆解：

- While 引导让步状语从句：表示“尽管……”
- 主干 1：big tech and the Trump administration have pushed teachers to use AI and claimed...
- claimed 后接两个并列宾语从句：it helps students learn / gives them the skills...
- in which the technology is ubiquitous 修饰 world，意思是“在一个 AI 无处不在的世界里”
- 主句：some parents and child development experts argue that...
- argue 后的宾语从句：there is little evidence that... and may even...

逻辑关系：先承认支持方理由，再引出反对方质疑，是 IELTS Writing / Speaking Part 3 很好用的让步反驳结构。

可模仿表达：

Although many people argue that AI prepares students for the future workplace, others worry that it may weaken the very skills that education is supposed to develop.

请打开 TED 页面，播放音频或视频，听到相关意思时暂停并写下原句。你不需要一次听全篇，重点找这些信息附近的句子。

**易听错点提醒**

- 连读：quick answers / real learning / designed to strengthen 这类词组容易连成一串。
- 弱读：should be, can be, to be, of the 里的功能词会被弱化，不要逐词硬抓。
- 近音：learn / learner / learning；think / thinking；challenge / change。
- 转折信号：but, however, rather than, instead, not just...but... 后面通常是核心观点。
- 抽象名词：knowledge, feedback, challenge, effort 容易听懂单词但漏掉句子逻辑。
- 专有名词：Priya Lakhani 是演讲者姓名，不必花太多时间纠结拼写。

**听后复述模板，80-120 词英文**

The talk discusses how children should learn with AI rather than simply use it to get quick answers. The speaker argues that real learning requires challenge, effort and active thinking. If AI only makes tasks easier, students may become dependent on it and fail to develop deeper understanding. However, AI does not have to weaken education. When it is designed carefully, it can guide learners, provide useful feedback and help teachers support students more effectively. In my view, the key point is that AI should not replace thinking; it should push students to think better.

## 4. 口语训练：从中式表达到 IELTS 7.0

**Part 1 Question 1: Do you often use AI tools when studying English?**

- 普通答案思路：说自己会用 AI 查单词、改句子、练口语。
- 7 分答案结构：频率 + 具体用途 + 限制意识。
- 高分表达：I use it as a learning assistant rather than a shortcut.

示范答案：  
Yes, quite often. I use AI tools to check vocabulary, polish my speaking answers and get examples of more natural expressions. But I try not to rely on them completely, because language learning still requires active recall and repeated practice. I see AI as a learning assistant rather than a shortcut.

**Part 1 Question 2: Did your school use much technology when you were younger?**

- 普通答案思路：以前主要是黑板和课本，后来有投影和网课。
- 7 分答案结构：过去情况 + 对比现在 + 简短评价。
- 高分表达：Technology was present, but it was not central to the learning process.

示范答案：  
Not really. When I was in school, technology was present, but it was not central to the learning process. We sometimes used projectors or online materials, but most lessons were still based on textbooks, note-taking and teacher explanations. Compared with today, it felt much less interactive but perhaps more focused.

**Part 1 Question 3: Do you think students learn better with digital tools?**

- 普通答案思路：有时可以，但不一定。
- 7 分答案结构：条件回答 + 举例 + 风险。
- 高分表达：It depends on whether the tool encourages thinking or replaces it.

示范答案：  
It depends on whether the tool encourages thinking or replaces it. For example, a vocabulary app can be useful if it helps students review words regularly. But if students use AI to generate essays without understanding the ideas, the tool may actually damage their learning habits.

**Part 2 Cue Card**

Describe a technology that has changed the way students learn.

You should say:

- what the technology is
- how students use it
- what benefits it brings
- and explain whether you think it is mostly positive or negative

**普通答案思路**

讲 AI 学习工具：能解释概念、改作文、生成练习，但可能导致依赖。

**7 分答案结构**

1. 开头明确对象：I’d like to talk about AI learning tools.  
2. 描述使用场景：vocabulary, essay feedback, problem-solving, speaking practice。  
3. 分析好处：personalised feedback, instant explanation, accessibility。  
4. 加入平衡观点：risk of over-reliance。  
5. 结尾判断：positive if used as support, negative if used as replacement.

**高分表达**

- personalised feedback 个性化反馈
- over-reliance on technology 过度依赖技术
- develop independent thinking 培养独立思考
- a tool for guidance, not a substitute for effort 指导工具，而不是努力的替代品
- make learning more accessible 让学习更容易获得

**Part 3 深度追问 1: Should schools limit students’ use of AI?**

- 普通答案思路：应该限制，尤其是低龄学生。
- 7 分答案结构：年龄区分 + 使用目的区分 + 学校责任。
- 高分表达：The issue is not access itself, but unregulated access.

答案思路：  
Yes, especially for younger students. The issue is not access itself, but unregulated access. Primary school students are still building basic reading, writing and reasoning skills, so AI should not be used to bypass those processes. For older students, schools can allow AI for brainstorming or feedback, but they need clear rules.

**Part 3 深度追问 2: Can AI replace teachers in the future?**

- 普通答案思路：不能完全替代。
- 7 分答案结构：承认 AI 优势 + 强调教师独特价值。
- 高分表达：Teaching is not just information delivery; it is also emotional and social guidance.

答案思路：  
I don’t think AI can fully replace teachers. It may be better than humans at providing instant explanations or practice questions, but teaching is not just information delivery; it is also emotional and social guidance. A good teacher notices confusion, motivates students and creates a classroom culture, which AI cannot easily replicate.

**Part 3 深度追问 3: What skills will students need in an AI-driven world?**

- 普通答案思路：批判性思维、沟通、创造力。
- 7 分答案结构：列三类技能 + 解释为什么。
- 高分表达：Students need to become better questioners, not just faster answer-seekers.

答案思路：  
Students will need critical thinking, communication skills and digital literacy. Since AI can produce information quickly, students need to judge whether that information is accurate and useful. They also need to become better questioners, not just faster answer-seekers, because the quality of the output often depends on the quality of the prompt.

**Part 3 深度追问 4: Is traditional education still useful when information is easily available online?**

- 普通答案思路：有用，因为学校教思维和纪律。
- 7 分答案结构：信息可得性 vs 理解能力。
- 高分表达：Access to information is not the same as the ability to make sense of it.

答案思路：  
Yes, traditional education is still useful, although it needs to adapt. Access to information is not the same as the ability to make sense of it. Schools help students build structure, discipline and reasoning skills. Without those foundations, students may have unlimited information but very limited understanding.
