## 2. 阅读训练：从“看懂”到“雅思阅读速度”

### 文章中文摘要

这篇 Guardian 文章讨论了美国学校中使用生成式 AI 引发的争议。科技公司和部分政府、教育机构认为 AI 可以帮助学生学习、提高课堂效率，并让学生提前掌握未来职场所需的 AI literacy。然而，越来越多家长、儿童发展专家和教育倡导组织担心，AI 可能让学生把思考过程外包给机器，削弱基础阅读、写作、批判性思维和问题解决能力。文章提到纽约、俄勒冈等地的家长和组织呼吁暂停或限制学生直接使用生成式 AI 工具，一些学校也开始制定更严格的技术使用标准。反对者尤其担心低龄学生在认知发展阶段过早依赖聊天机器人，而支持者则认为，完全禁止 AI 并不现实，关键是教会学生理解 AI 的局限并批判性使用。整篇文章的核心冲突是：AI 到底是在帮助孩子学习，还是在替代孩子学习？

### 8 个核心词汇或短语

| 词汇/短语 | 中文 | 原文语境 | 可替换表达 |
|---|---|---|---|
| prompt a tide of concern | 引发大量担忧 | AI 进入课堂后，家长和专家的担忧不断增加 | trigger widespread concern / spark public anxiety |
| generative AI | 生成式人工智能 | 学生使用聊天机器人完成反馈、写作或学习任务 | AI chatbot tools / content-generating AI |
| student-facing tools | 面向学生直接使用的工具 | 一些组织要求暂停低龄学生直接接触 AI 产品 | tools used directly by students |
| cognitive off-loading | 认知卸载 | 专家担心学生用 AI 避免自己动脑 | outsourcing mental effort |
| critical thinking | 批判性思维 | AI 可能影响学生独立分析和判断能力 | analytical thinking / independent reasoning |
| moratorium | 暂停令；暂缓实施 | 家长组织呼吁学校暂停使用 AI | temporary ban / suspension |
| AI literacy | AI 素养 | 支持者认为学生需要学习如何理解和使用 AI | digital literacy / technological competence |
| foundational academic skills | 基础学术能力 | 反对者认为学校应先培养阅读、写作、思考等基本能力 | core learning skills / basic academic abilities |

#### Question 5: Summary Completion

Complete the summary with words from the list.

The article presents a debate about whether AI should be used in schools. Critics argue that AI may weaken students’ ________ skills by encouraging them to depend on external tools. Supporters, however, believe students need ________ so that they can understand the technology and use it more critically.

Words:  
AI literacy / critical thinking / entertainment / physical exercise / classroom decoration

### 答案与解析

1. **True**  
解析：文章多次提到家长担心孩子让机器替自己思考，这与题干一致。

2. **False**  
解析：文章提到部分地区、家长组织和学校在推动限制或暂停，但并没有说全美国学校都已经禁止。

3. **B**  
解析：专家担心的是 cognitive off-loading，即学生借助外部工具逃避认知努力。

4. **C**  
解析：支持者认为未来公司会期待员工有 Gen AI literacy，因此学生需要学习如何理解和使用 AI。

5. **critical thinking; AI literacy**  
解析：第一空对应“批判性思维可能被削弱”；第二空对应“支持者主张培养 AI 素养”。

### 长难句拆解

**句子**：  
While technology companies argue that AI can make learning more personalised and efficient, many parents and experts worry that young students may use it to complete tasks without developing the underlying skills those tasks are meant to build.

**结构拆解**：  
- **While technology companies argue that...**：让步状语从句，表示“尽管科技公司认为……”
- **AI can make learning more personalised and efficient**：argue 的宾语从句，说明支持 AI 的理由
- **many parents and experts worry that...**：主句，转向反方观点
- **young students may use it to complete tasks**：worry 的宾语从句核心
- **without developing the underlying skills**：介词短语，说明问题所在
- **those tasks are meant to build**：定语从句，修饰 skills

**逻辑关系**：  
支持方强调效率和个性化；反对方强调学习过程和能力形成。IELTS Reading 中这类句子常用于呈现 opposing views。

**可模仿表达**：  
While some people argue that technology improves efficiency, others worry that it may weaken the skills people are supposed to develop through practice.

### 易听错点提醒

- **连读**：spend too much time / looking at my phone / access to social media  
- **弱读**：to、of、and、that 在自然语速中会被弱化，不要逐词硬听  
- **近音词**：screen / scream；access / excess；measure / major  
- **数字信息**：注意 under 16s、six minutes、dates 或年龄限制  
- **转折信号**：but、however、although、on the other hand 后面通常是答案重点  
- **人名地名**：Australia、UK、Cambridge 这类专有名词要先预测，再听确认

### 听后复述模板：80-120 词英文

Some governments are considering rules to reduce children’s access to social media, especially for younger users. The episode also introduces expert opinions on how screen use may affect children’s behaviour, attention and development. In my view, the key issue is not simply the number of hours children spend online, but whether screen use replaces sleep, physical activity, reading and face-to-face communication.

## 4. 口语训练：从中式表达到 IELTS 7.0

### Part 1 Question 1

**Question**：How much time do you spend on your phone every day?

**普通答案思路**：  
说每天大概多久，用手机做什么，是否觉得太多。

**7 分答案结构**：  
直接回答 + 具体用途 + 轻微反思。

**高分表达**：  
- I probably spend more time on my phone than I’d like to admit.  
- Most of it is practical, but some of it is just mindless scrolling.  
- I’m trying to be more intentional about my screen use.

### Part 1 Question 2

**Question**：Do you think students should use AI tools for schoolwork?

**普通答案思路**：  
可以用，但不能完全依赖。

**7 分答案结构**：  
平衡观点 + 条件限制 + 例子。

**高分表达**：  
- It depends on how the tool is used.  
- AI can be helpful for brainstorming, but it should not replace independent thinking.  
- Students still need to develop their own judgement.

### Part 1 Question 3

**Question**：Did you use many digital devices when you were a child?

**普通答案思路**：  
小时候没现在多，主要看电视或玩电脑。

**7 分答案结构**：  
过去对比现在 + 影响评价。

**高分表达**：  
- Digital devices were much less central to my childhood.  
- Compared with children today, I had more offline time.  
- That probably made it easier to concentrate for longer periods.

### Part 2 Cue Card

**Describe a time when technology helped you learn something.**

You should say:  
- what technology you used  
- what you learned  
- how it helped you  
- and explain whether you think it was better than learning without technology

**普通答案思路**：  
说一次用网课、App、AI 或 YouTube 学英语/技能。

**7 分答案结构**：  
背景 15 秒 + 学习过程 40 秒 + 具体好处 25 秒 + 反思 20 秒。

**高分表达**：  
- It gave me instant access to explanations and examples.  
- The biggest advantage was flexibility.  
- However, I still had to practise actively instead of just consuming information.  
- Technology worked best when I used it as a support tool, not as a shortcut.

### Part 3 Question 1

**Question**：Should governments limit children’s screen time?

**普通答案思路**：  
应该有指导，但不能只靠政府。

**7 分答案结构**：  
承认必要性 + 说明限制 + 提出共同责任。

**高分表达**：  
- Government guidance can set a baseline, but it cannot replace parental judgement.  
- A one-size-fits-all rule may be difficult to enforce.  
- Schools, families and technology companies all have a role to play.

### Part 3 Question 2

**Question**：What are the risks of using AI in education?

**普通答案思路**：  
学生懒得思考、作弊、依赖工具。

**7 分答案结构**：  
列两个风险 + 解释长期影响。

**高分表达**：  
- The biggest risk is cognitive dependency.  
- Students may complete the task without internalising the skill.  
- Over time, this could weaken problem-solving and critical thinking.

### Part 3 Question 3

**Question**：Can technology make education more equal?

**普通答案思路**：  
有可能，但也可能扩大差距。

**7 分答案结构**：  
双边观点 + 条件判断。

**高分表达**：  
- In theory, technology can widen access to high-quality resources.  
- In practice, it may deepen inequality if some students lack devices, guidance or digital literacy.  
- Access alone does not guarantee meaningful learning.

### Part 3 Question 4

**Question**：Do you think future schools will rely more on AI teachers?

**普通答案思路**：  
AI 会辅助老师，但不能取代老师。

**7 分答案结构**：  
预测趋势 + 区分功能 + 强调人类教师价值。

**高分表达**：  
- AI may handle repetitive tasks such as quizzes or basic feedback.  
- Human teachers are still essential for motivation, emotional support and classroom judgement.  
- Education is not just information delivery; it is also social development.
