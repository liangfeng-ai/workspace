## 2. 阅读训练：从“看懂”到“雅思阅读速度”

**文章中文摘要，约 190 词**

这篇文章讨论了一个越来越受关注的问题：人们长期依赖 AI 工具后，是否会逐渐丧失原本需要自己掌握的专业技能。文章先引用医疗从业者的担忧，指出许多护士和医生害怕自己因为过度依赖 AI 而出现能力退化。随后，文章介绍了一项关于内镜医生的研究：医生使用 AI 辅助识别病变后，在没有 AI 帮助的情况下，检测表现明显下降。这说明即使是经验丰富的专业人士，也可能因为 AI 改变了注意力和责任感而降低独立判断能力。文章还提到一项针对软件工程师的随机实验：使用 AI 助手完成编程任务的人，在之后的理解测验中表现更差，尤其不擅长诊断代码错误。作者进一步指出，AI 与 GPS 等旧技术不同，因为生成式 AI 自动化的是思考、解释和判断等认知能力。文章最后建议，人们不应盲目信任 AI 输出，而要意识到自己把哪些思考任务外包给了工具，并保持警觉。

**8 个核心词汇或短语**

| 英文 | 中文 | 原文语境 | 可替换表达 |
|---|---|---|---|
| over-reliance on AI | 对 AI 的过度依赖 | 医护人员担心长期依赖 AI 会削弱技能 | excessive dependence on AI |
| deskilling | 技能退化；去技能化 | 文章核心概念，指 AI 让人逐渐丧失专业能力 | skill erosion |

| diagnose errors | 诊断错误；找出问题 | AI 组在识别代码错误方面表现较差 | identify mistakes |
| outsource cognition | 外包认知 | 人们把思考、判断、解释交给 AI | offload thinking |

**5 道雅思阅读题型练习**

**Questions 1-3: True / False / Not Given**

1. The article says that only junior professionals are at risk of losing skills because of AI.  
2. In the colonoscopy study, doctors performed worse without AI after they had begun using the AI tool.  
3. The article claims that AI tools should be banned in medical settings.

**Questions 4-5: Multiple Choice**

4. What is the main purpose of the software-engineer experiment mentioned in the article?  
A. To prove AI can replace junior programmers  
B. To test whether using AI affects what people learn from a task  
C. To compare different programming languages  
D. To show that all AI-generated code is inaccurate

5. Which statement best reflects the article’s final recommendation?  
A. People should avoid all forms of automation.  
B. People should use AI only for entertainment.  
C. People should understand AI’s limits and question its outputs.  
D. People should trust AI more because it improves speed.

**答案与解析**

1. **False**  
解析：文章明确提到医生、护士、软件工程师等不同层级专业人士都有风险，并非只有初级人员。

2. **True**  
解析：研究显示，医生开始使用 AI 后，在没有 AI 辅助时，检测表现下降。这是文章支持“deskilling”的关键证据。

3. **Not Given**  
解析：文章讨论风险和警觉性，但没有主张在医疗环境中完全禁止 AI。

4. **B**  
解析：该实验关注的是 AI 辅助是否影响完成任务后的学习和理解，而不是证明 AI 替代人类。

5. **C**  
解析：文章最后强调要意识到自己外包了多少思考，理解 AI 的局限，并避免不加质疑地信任输出。

**长难句拆解**

句子：  
*The findings suggest that even highly skilled professionals might get worse at tasks that their job requires as they become more dependent on AI tools.*

结构拆解：

- 主干：**The findings suggest that...**  
  “这些发现表明……”
- 宾语从句：**even highly skilled professionals might get worse at tasks**  
  “即使高技能专业人士也可能在某些任务上变差”
- 定语从句：**that their job requires**  
  修饰 tasks，表示“他们工作所要求的任务”
- 时间 / 因果关系：**as they become more dependent on AI tools**  
  as 在这里表示“随着……”，也带有轻微因果含义。

可模仿表达：

- *The evidence suggests that even experienced learners may lose accuracy as they rely more heavily on translation tools.*
- *The findings indicate that students might become less independent as they outsource more thinking to AI.*

**第二遍精听任务：6 句关键表达听写**

请先播放音频，不看 transcript。每句暂停 2-3 次，写出你听到的内容。以下为“听写目标句意”，你写完后再对照 transcript。

1. 主持人询问：你是否相信地球之外存在生命？  
2. 嘉宾解释：这是一颗不在我们太阳系中的行星。  
3. 这颗行星与地球大小相近，并位于适合生命存在的区域。  
4. 科学家正在研究它可能拥有的大气层。  
5. 他们已经排除某些会让行星过热的气体。  
6. 如果温度合适，这将让人类离发现生命更近一步。

**易听错点提醒**

- 连读：*life exists away from...* 中 exists away 容易连在一起，听成一个音块。
- 弱读：*whether or not it’s...* 中 or not it’s 会被快速带过，不要逐词硬听。
- 近音：*planet* 和 *plant*；*life* 和 *live* 要靠上下文判断。
- 数字：*40 light years away* 和 *100km an hour* 是定位细节，听到数字马上记。
- 科学词：*TRAPPIST-1e*, *hydrogen*, *carbon dioxide*, *nitrogen* 只需抓核心音，不必第一次拼写完美。
- 转折信号：*but*, *however*, *or maybe* 后面通常出现限制、怀疑或修正。

**听后复述模板，80-120 词**

*The episode discusses whether life could exist beyond Earth, focusing on a planet called TRAPPIST-1e. It is an exoplanet, which means it is outside our solar system, and scientists are interested in it because it is rocky, similar in size to Earth, and located in a potentially habitable zone. Researchers are studying its atmosphere to see whether it contains gases that could support life. They have ruled out some greenhouse gases, which is encouraging because those gases might make the planet too hot. Overall, the episode suggests that scientists are not claiming they have found life, but they may be one step closer.*

## 4. 口语训练：从中式表达到 IELTS 7.0

今日口语主题：**AI, learning, and human skills**

### Part 1 问题 1

**Question:** Do you often use AI tools when you study English?

- 普通答案思路：说会用，用来查单词、改句子、练口语。
- 7 分答案结构：直接回答 + 具体用途 + 限制意识。
- 高分表达：  
  - *I use AI as a study assistant rather than a replacement for thinking.*  
  - *It helps me notice mistakes that I might otherwise overlook.*  
  - *That said, I try not to depend on it blindly.*

### Part 1 问题 2

**Question:** What skills do students still need to develop by themselves?

- 普通答案思路：阅读理解、独立思考、表达能力。
- 7 分答案结构：列两类技能 + 举例 + 说明原因。
- 高分表达：  
  - *Students still need to build core skills through repeated practice.*  
  - *Critical thinking cannot be fully outsourced to a machine.*  
  - *If they skip the struggle, they may also miss the learning.*

### Part 1 问题 3

**Question:** Do you think technology makes learning easier?

- 普通答案思路：是的，但也可能让人懒。
- 7 分答案结构：承认优点 + 指出风险 + 给平衡观点。
- 高分表达：  
  - *Technology lowers the barrier to learning.*  
  - *The downside is that convenience can sometimes reduce effort.*  
  - *It depends on whether learners use it actively or passively.*

### Part 2 Cue Card

**Describe a technology that has helped you learn something.**

You should say:

- what the technology is
- when you started using it
- how it helped you
- and explain whether you think it has any disadvantages

**普通答案思路：**  
可以讲 AI 翻译、ChatGPT、英语播客 App、Anki、YouTube 字幕。重点不要只说“方便”，要说具体学习行为。

**7 分答案结构：**

1. 开头点题：我想讲 AI 学习工具。
2. 背景：备考 IELTS 时开始用。
3. 具体帮助：改口语表达、解释阅读长句、生成练习题。
4. 风险：如果直接复制答案，会削弱独立表达。
5. 总结：把它当教练，而不是代写工具。

**高分表达：**

- *It gives me instant feedback, which is something I cannot always get from a teacher.*
- *It is particularly useful when I need to rephrase a sentence in a more natural way.*
- *However, the biggest risk is becoming mentally lazy.*
- *I have to make sure I still produce my own ideas first.*

### Part 3 深度追问 1

**Question:** Do you think AI will change the role of teachers?

- 普通答案思路：会，老师不只是讲知识，而是引导。
- 7 分答案结构：变化 + 不会完全替代 + 教师新价值。
- 高分表达：  
  - *Teachers may become more like mentors and learning designers.*  
  - *AI can deliver information, but it cannot fully understand a student’s emotional state.*  
  - *The human side of education will become more important, not less.*

### Part 3 深度追问 2

**Question:** Why might people lose skills when they rely too much on technology?

- 普通答案思路：因为不练习，习惯让工具代替自己。
- 7 分答案结构：解释机制 + 举例 + 结果。
- 高分表达：  
  - *Skills deteriorate when they are not actively used.*  
  - *People may confuse getting a correct answer with understanding the process.*  
  - *There is a gap between performance and learning.*

### Part 3 深度追问 3

**Question:** Should schools restrict students’ use of AI?

- 普通答案思路：不应该完全禁止，但要有规则。
- 7 分答案结构：反对极端 + 提出规范 + 给例子。
- 高分表达：  
  - *A complete ban would be unrealistic.*  
  - *Schools should teach students how to use AI transparently and responsibly.*  
  - *For example, students could use AI for feedback, but not for writing the entire assignment.*

### Part 3 深度追问 4

**Question:** Which is more important in the future: technical knowledge or critical thinking?

- 普通答案思路：批判性思维更重要，但技术知识也需要。
- 7 分答案结构：比较 + 解释原因 + 平衡结论。
- 高分表达：  
  - *Technical knowledge can become outdated quite quickly.*  
  - *Critical thinking helps people evaluate new information and adapt to change.*  
  - *Ideally, people need both: technical competence and independent judgement.*
