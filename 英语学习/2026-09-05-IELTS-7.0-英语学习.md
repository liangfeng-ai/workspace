## 2. 阅读训练：从“看懂”到“雅思阅读速度”

**中文摘要**
纽约市宣布将把学生在公立学校使用 AI 的限制扩大到八年级以下，同时小学低年级还会进一步限制平板和笔记本使用。支持者认为，过度依赖技术会削弱学生的思考能力、注意力和责任感；反对者则担心政策过于简单化，难以真正监督校外和课外的 AI 使用。文章还提到，新规允许高中阶段在特定场景下使用 AI，并计划成立跨部门工作组，继续完善学校的 AI 政策。这篇报道的核心不是“AI 能不能用”，而是“在什么年龄、什么场景、以什么方式用”。

**8 个核心词汇或短语**
1. **ban students’ use of artificial intelligence**
   - 中文：禁止学生使用人工智能
   - 原文语境：New York City will **ban students’ use of artificial intelligence** in public schools through eighth grade.
   - 可替换表达：prohibit student AI use / restrict AI access

2. **through eighth grade**
   - 中文：直到八年级为止
   - 原文语境：... in public schools **through eighth grade** ...
   - 可替换表达：up to eighth grade / for students in grades K-8

3. **sweeping overhaul**
   - 中文：大刀阔斧的改革
   - 原文语境：The change is part of a **sweeping overhaul** of the use of technology...
   - 可替换表达：major reform / comprehensive revision

4. **cognitive surrender**
   - 中文：认知让渡，放弃独立思考
   - 原文语境：... concerns about ... a phenomenon researchers have dubbed **cognitive surrender** ...
   - 可替换表达：mental laziness / overreliance on software

5. **screen time**
   - 中文：屏幕使用时间
   - 原文语境：The city’s education department recommends limiting **screen time**...
   - 可替换表达：time spent on devices / digital exposure

6. **go into effect**
   - 中文：生效
   - 原文语境：The prohibition is scheduled to be announced ... and will **go into effect** next week.
   - 可替换表达：take effect / come into force

7. **companion chatbots**
   - 中文：陪伴型聊天机器人
   - 原文语境：AI “**companion chatbots**” offering psychological support will also be banned.
   - 可替换表达：AI companions / support chatbots

8. **taskforce**
   - 中文：工作组
   - 原文语境：The department will set up a **taskforce** made up of teachers, elected officials, experts and union representatives.
   - 可替换表达：working group / special panel

**5 道雅思阅读题型练习**

1. **True / False / Not Given**
   - 题目：New York City will allow students to use AI freely in all grades as long as teachers supervise them.
   - 答案：**False**
   - 解析：文章明确说八年级及以下将禁止学生使用 AI，高中阶段才有限开放，不是“所有年级都可自由使用”。

2. **True / False / Not Given**
   - 题目：The education department recommends 30 minutes of screen time for elementary school students.
   - 答案：**True**
   - 解析：原文说小学阶段建议每节课屏幕时间限制为 30 分钟。

3. **Multiple Choice**
   - 题目：What is one of the main concerns mentioned in the article?
     - A. AI makes teachers less creative
     - B. Students may stop thinking independently
     - C. Schools will run out of devices
     - D. The policy will cost parents more money
   - 答案：**B**
   - 解析：文中直接提到“cognitive surrender”，意思就是学生可能把思考过程让给软件。

4. **Matching**
   - 题目：Match the policy item with its description.
     - 1. AI use in public schools through eighth grade
     - 2. Laptops or tablets in elementary classrooms
     - 3. AI companion chatbots
     - 4. AI for grading assignments
     - A. banned
     - B. limited to 30 minutes
     - C. banned
     - D. allowed only in high school / limited use
   - 答案：1-D, 2-B, 3-C, 4-A
   - 解析：文章分别说明了不同年级、不同设备和不同 AI 功能的限制层级。

5. **Summary Completion**
   - 题目：Complete the summary with words from the box.
   - Word box: `taskforce / high school / cognitive / screen time`
   - Summary: New York City’s new policy will restrict AI use in lower grades, allow limited use only in ________, and create a ________ to refine future rules. Supporters say the policy may prevent ________ surrender, while the department also recommends shorter ________ in class.
   - 答案：**high school / taskforce / cognitive / screen time**
   - 解析：四个词分别对应文章中的四个核心信息点。

**1 个长难句拆解**
- 原句：**The change is part of a sweeping overhaul of the use of technology in the US’s largest school district, which enrolls roughly 900,000 students a year.**
- 结构分析：
  - 主干：**The change is part of a sweeping overhaul**
  - 后置修饰 1：**of the use of technology in the US’s largest school district**，说明“改革”的范围
  - 非限制性定语从句：**which enrolls roughly 900,000 students a year**，补充说明这个学区规模巨大
- 逻辑关系：
  - 先说“这项变化属于一场大改革”
  - 再说明改革对象是“技术使用”
  - 最后用定语从句补充“这个学区有多大”
- 可模仿表达：
  - **The policy is part of a broader reform in ... , which ...**
  - **This decision is part of a wider effort to ...**

## 3. 听力训练：精听 + 抓主旨

2. 她为什么反对把手机/社交媒体简单当作青少年心理健康危机的主因？

**第二遍精听任务：听写 8 句关键表达**
1. I’ve been working with teens for the past 25 years.
2. We are really getting it wrong in the way that we talk about teen mental health.
3. What we’re talking about these days is their phones.
4. Twenty-five years of data have just not shown that to be true.
5. The health and the behavior of adults around young people is one of the most important things.
6. Social media is not a strong or robust predictor of mental health symptoms.
7. Adolescents need to be engaged in the problem-solving.
8. The way we get there is through cleaning up these spaces for everyone.

**易听错点提醒**
- 连读：**working with / going to / kind of / not shown that**
- 弱读：**for the past**, **about teen mental health**, **to be true**
- 同音近音：**their / there**, **effect / affect**, **whole / hold**
- 数字：**25 years**, **one in five**, **1999**, **2011**, **2021**
- 人名地名：**Candice Odgers**, **Chris Duffy**
- 转折信号：**but**, **that’s a separate question**, **I’m not saying ...**

**听后复述模板（80-120词）**
The speaker argues that people often overstate the role of phones and social media in teen mental health. She says the data do not support the idea that technology is the main cause of the crisis. Instead, adult mental health, family support, and the broader social environment matter more. She also believes that simple bans are not effective for adolescents, because teenagers need to be involved in problem-solving. Overall, her message is that online harms should be addressed, but public debate should be more evidence-based and less fearful.

## 4. 口语训练：从中式表达到 IELTS 7.0

**Part 1**
1. **Do you think teenagers spend too much time on phones?**
   - 普通答案思路：Yes, they use phones a lot, especially for social media and games.
   - 7分答案结构：先表态，再给一个现实例子，再补一个平衡点。
   - 可直接套用的高分表达：**It depends on how they use it, but excessive screen time can easily crowd out sleep, exercise, and face-to-face interaction.**

2. **Should schools limit the use of AI tools?**
   - 普通答案思路：Yes, because students may depend on AI too much.
   - 7分答案结构：先说“限制有必要”，再区分年龄段，最后补充教育目标。
   - 可直接套用的高分表达：**A staged approach makes more sense than a blanket ban.**

3. **Do parents influence teenagers’ mental health a lot?**
   - 普通答案思路：Yes, parents are very important.
   - 7分答案结构：用“家庭氛围 + 情绪支持 + 沟通方式”展开。
   - 可直接套用的高分表达：**The emotional climate at home often matters more than any single app or platform.**

**Part 2 cue card**
- **Describe a rule that you think is useful for students.**
  - You should say:
    - what the rule is
    - who should follow it
    - why it is useful
    - and explain whether you would change it

- 普通答案思路：说一个校园规则，比如限制手机、按时交作业、课堂不打断别人。
- 7分答案结构：
  - 开头直接定义规则
  - 说明适用对象和场景
  - 解释它解决了什么问题
  - 给一个个人经历或观察
  - 结尾评价这个规则是否需要调整
- 可直接套用的高分表达：
  - **It creates a clearer learning environment.**
  - **It encourages self-discipline rather than blind compliance.**
  - **I’d prefer a balanced policy instead of a one-size-fits-all rule.**

**Part 3**
1. **Why do many adults panic about teenagers and technology?**
   - 普通答案思路：Because they worry about addiction and safety.
   - 7分答案结构：从“恐惧叙事、媒体放大、控制感下降”三点展开。
   - 高分表达：**People often confuse discomfort with evidence.**

2. **What is the downside of banning technology too quickly?**
   - 普通答案思路：It may not work and students may still find ways around it.
   - 7分答案结构：讲“执行难、监督难、可能转向地下使用”。
   - 高分表达：**A ban can look decisive on paper but weak in practice.**

3. **Should schools teach students how to use AI responsibly?**
   - 普通答案思路：Yes, because AI is part of modern life.
   - 7分答案结构：讲“现实需要 + 风险意识 + 批判性使用”。
   - 高分表达：**The goal is not just access, but informed judgment.**

4. **How can societies improve young people’s mental health?**
   - 普通答案思路：More support from family, school, and community.
   - 7分答案结构：把重点放在“成年人支持、学校资源、线上线下环境”。
   - 高分表达：**We should treat youth mental health as a system issue, not a device issue.**

**90 秒口语任务**
- 现在请你就这个题目连续说 90 秒：**Should schools ban AI use for younger students?**
- 要求：
  - 先表态
  - 说 2 个理由
  - 给 1 个例子
  - 最后补一句平衡观点
- 回答时请录音，或直接大声完整说出来。

**自评标准**
- fluency：是否停顿过多，能否连续表达
- vocabulary：是否用了 ban / restrict / stage / evidence-based / balanced 等词
- grammar：是否能稳定使用复合句、让步句、原因结果句
- pronunciation：重音、连读、句尾是否清楚
- idea development：是否有观点、理由、例子、总结
