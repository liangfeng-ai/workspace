### 长难句拆解

**句子：**  
While there is consensus that datacentres can provide some essential services, there are concerns that developments with a huge environmental impact and local opposition are being prioritised regardless of their use.

**结构拆解：**

- **While there is consensus that...**：让步状语从句，表示“尽管人们普遍同意……”
- **datacentres can provide some essential services**：that 引导的同位/内容从句，说明 consensus 的内容。
- **there are concerns that...**：主句，表示“仍然存在担忧”。
- **developments ... are being prioritised**：that 从句中的核心结构，被动语态，表示“这些开发项目正在被优先考虑”。
- **with a huge environmental impact and local opposition**：介词短语作后置修饰，说明 developments 的特点。
- **regardless of their use**：让步/忽略条件，表示“不管它们实际用途是什么”。

**逻辑关系：**  
先承认数据中心有必要性，再转折指出监管和资源分配风险。

**可模仿表达：**

- While there is consensus that **AI can improve productivity**, there are concerns that **its social costs are being underestimated**.
- While there is consensus that **online learning is convenient**, there are concerns that **students may lose face-to-face communication skills**.

### 易听错点提醒

| 类型 | 易错点 | 训练提醒 |
|---|---|---|
| 连读 | **a lot of fear out there** | 听起来像 “alotta fear out there” |
| 弱读 | **could eliminate a ton of jobs** | could / a / of 都会弱读，不要逐词期待清晰重音 |
| 术语 | **token tax** | token 不是“代币”语境，而是 AI 模型处理语言的基本单位 |
| 数字 | **127% since November 2022** | 注意百分比和起始时间，雅思听力常考数字定位 |
| 人名 | **Alex Bores / Martha Gimbel** | 第一次听只需抓身份：politician / tax expert |
| 转折信号 | **but / whereas / in other words** | 这些词后面常出现真正观点或反驳 |
| 近音 | **tax / task** | “up to the task” 不是 “up to the tax” |

## 4. 口语训练：从中式表达到 IELTS 7.0

### Part 1 Question 1

**Do you often use AI tools in your daily life?**

**普通答案思路：**  
说你会用 AI 查资料、改英文、总结文章，但不会完全依赖。

**7 分答案结构：**  
直接回答 + 具体使用场景 + 好处 + 小限制。

**高分表达：**

- I use AI mainly as a productivity tool rather than a replacement for thinking.
- It helps me summarise information and refine my wording.
- I still try to double-check important facts instead of taking the answer at face value.

### Part 1 Question 2

**Do you think technology makes people’s lives easier?**

**普通答案思路：**  
总体是的，但也带来信息过载和隐私问题。

**7 分答案结构：**  
让步开头 + 两个例子 + 平衡观点。

**高分表达：**

- To a large extent, yes, especially when it comes to communication and access to information.
- That said, convenience sometimes comes at the cost of privacy.
- Technology is useful only when people use it deliberately.

### Part 1 Question 3

**Are people in your country concerned about data privacy?**

**普通答案思路：**  
越来越关心，但很多人仍然为了方便接受风险。

**7 分答案结构：**  
趋势 + 原因 + 对比 + 个人观点。

**高分表达：**

- People are becoming more aware of how their data is collected and used.
- However, many users still trade privacy for convenience.
- There is a gap between public concern and actual behaviour.

### Part 2 Cue Card

**Describe a technology that has changed the way people work or study.**

You should say:

- what the technology is
- how people use it
- what benefits it brings
- and explain whether you think it has more advantages or disadvantages

**普通答案思路：**  
讲 AI 工具，如 ChatGPT。它能帮助学习、写邮件、总结材料，但也可能让人变懒。

**7 分答案结构：**

1. 开头点题：选择 AI writing assistants  
2. 使用场景：学习英语、整理资料、工作沟通  
3. 优点：效率、个性化反馈、降低门槛  
4. 缺点：依赖、准确性、原创性问题  
5. 结论：利大于弊，但需要规则和自律

**高分表达：**

- It has dramatically lowered the barrier to accessing specialised knowledge.
- The main drawback is that people may outsource too much of their thinking.
- I would say it is beneficial as long as it is used as an assistant, not as a substitute.
- It changes not only how fast people work, but also what skills are valued.

### Part 3 Question 1

**Should governments regulate artificial intelligence more strictly?**

**普通答案思路：**  
应该，尤其是隐私、就业、虚假信息方面；但不要扼杀创新。

**7 分答案结构：**  
明确立场 + 高风险领域 + 创新平衡 + 例子。

**高分表达：**

- Regulation should focus on high-risk applications rather than the technology as a whole.
- A blanket ban would be unrealistic and counterproductive.
- Governments need to set clear rules on accountability and transparency.

### Part 3 Question 2

**Who should pay for the environmental costs of datacentres?**

**普通答案思路：**  
受益最多的公司应该承担更多成本，消费者也可能间接付费。

**7 分答案结构：**  
原则 + 公司责任 + 政府角色 + 公平性。

**高分表达：**

- The companies that profit from these facilities should internalise the environmental costs.
- Public resources such as water and electricity should not be treated as unlimited.
- A fair system would link usage, profit and environmental responsibility.

### Part 3 Question 3

**Do you think AI will increase or reduce inequality?**

**普通答案思路：**  
都有可能。如果教育和政策跟上，能减少不平等；否则会扩大差距。

**7 分答案结构：**  
双边讨论 + 条件判断 + 长期影响。

**高分表达：**

- It depends on whether the benefits are broadly distributed.
- If only highly skilled workers and large companies benefit, inequality may widen.
- But with proper education and retraining, AI could also create new opportunities.

### Part 3 Question 4

**Should local communities have a say in building large technology infrastructure?**

**普通答案思路：**  
应该，因为他们承受噪音、用水、用电和土地影响。

**7 分答案结构：**  
社区权益 + 国家利益 + 决策机制。

**高分表达：**

- Local communities should not be excluded from decisions that directly affect their environment.
- National infrastructure may be necessary, but it still requires democratic oversight.
- Public consultation can improve trust and reduce resistance.
