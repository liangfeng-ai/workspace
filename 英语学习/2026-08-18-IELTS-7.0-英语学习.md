## 2. 阅读训练：从“看懂”到“雅思阅读速度”

### 文章中文摘要

这篇文章讨论了一个越来越受关注的问题：当医生、护士、软件工程师等专业人士频繁依赖 AI 工具时，他们原本通过长期训练形成的判断力和操作能力是否会退化。文章指出，AI 确实能提高效率，例如帮助医疗人员分析图像、辅助程序员写代码、减少重复性任务。但早期研究显示，过度依赖 AI 可能导致“deskilling”，也就是技能弱化。比如，医生如果长期依靠 AI 判断病灶，可能在没有 AI 辅助时更难独立识别问题；程序员如果经常接受 AI 生成的代码，可能减少主动思考和调试能力。文章并不是简单反对 AI，而是强调：AI 的真正风险不只是“出错”，还包括让人逐渐失去保持专业判断所需的练习机会。对 IELTS 来说，这篇文章的核心可概括为：**AI should be used as support, not as a substitute for human expertise.**

### 8 个核心词汇或短语

| 词汇 / 短语 | 中文 | 原文语境 | 可替换表达 |
|---|---|---|---|
| over-reliance on AI | 对 AI 的过度依赖 | 文中用来解释医生和护士担心技能退化的原因 | excessive dependence on AI |
| deskilling | 技能弱化 / 去技能化 | 指专业人士因为依赖技术而失去原有能力 | erosion of professional skills |
| atrophy | 萎缩；退化 | 用于比喻长期不用的技能会变弱 | deteriorate / weaken |
| cognitive faculties | 认知能力 | 指思考、解释、判断等人类能力 | mental abilities |
| professional judgement | 专业判断 | 医生、工程师等不能完全交给 AI 的能力 | expert assessment |
| automate | 自动化 | AI 可以自动完成部分认知或工作任务 | mechanise / perform automatically |
| justified concern | 合理的担忧 | 文章认为对 AI 造成技能退化的担心有证据支持 | valid worry |
| human oversight | 人类监督 | 使用 AI 时仍需要人的审查和责任判断 | human supervision |

#### Question 5: Summary Completion

Complete the summary with ONE phrase from the vocabulary list.

The article argues that AI can improve efficiency, but excessive dependence may lead to **__________**, especially when professionals stop practising the skills needed for independent judgement.

### 答案与解析

1. **True**  
解析：文章核心就是 AI 可能造成专业能力退化，尤其是医生、护士、程序员等依赖 AI 后，独立判断能力可能下降。

2. **False**  
解析：文章并没有主张立即禁止所有医疗 AI，而是强调需要谨慎使用、保持人类监督和专业训练。

3. **B**  
解析：主旨不是成本、替代全部职业或 AI 完全无能，而是“over-reliance may weaken expertise”。

4. **C**  
解析：文章承认 AI 的效率价值，但重点提醒早期证据显示存在技能退化风险，态度是谨慎而非极端。

5. **deskilling**  
解析：summary 中的 “excessive dependence” 对应 “over-reliance”，而 “professionals stop practising skills” 对应 “deskilling”。

### 长难句拆解

**句子**：Evidence suggests that AI-driven deskilling is starting to happen in medicine, computer science and other fields.

**结构拆解**：

- **主干**：Evidence suggests that...
- **宾语从句**：AI-driven deskilling is starting to happen...
- **核心名词**：deskilling = 技能弱化
- **修饰成分**：AI-driven = 由 AI 推动的 / 与 AI 使用相关的
- **范围补充**：in medicine, computer science and other fields = 在医学、计算机科学和其他领域

**逻辑关系**：  
这句话不是说 AI 一定会毁掉所有技能，而是说“已有证据表明，这种趋势正在一些领域开始出现”。语气比 “AI destroys skills” 更学术、更谨慎。

**可模仿表达**：

- Evidence suggests that excessive screen time may affect students’ concentration.
- Evidence suggests that remote work is reshaping communication habits.
- Evidence suggests that regular reading can strengthen cognitive flexibility.

### 第二遍精听任务：6-10 句关键表达听写

请听第二遍，暂停并听写以下关键句。可以先写出你听到的版本，再对照 transcript 修改。

2. **Scientific research has shown that learning to read changes the way our brains work.**

4. **The longest novel in the world is widely thought to be by French author Marcel Proust.**

### 易听错点提醒

- **连读**：  
  “what it was like” 容易听成 /wɒtɪtwəzlaɪk/，不要逐词期待清晰停顿。

- **弱读**：  
  “a part of the brain” 中 **of the** 会弱化，重点听 part 和 brain。

- **近音词**：  
  **read / red**：read 的过去式和 red 同音，要靠上下文判断。  
  **brain / train**：开头辅音不同，但快语速中要注意。

- **人名**：  
  **Marcel Proust** 是法国作家，听到陌生人名不要卡住，先记首音或大概拼写。

- **数字**：  
  quiz 中会出现 **130,000 / 1.3 million / 13 million**，注意 thousand 和 million 的差别。

- **转折信号**：  
  注意 **not only that**, **but**, **because**, **depending on**，这些词后面通常跟关键信息。

### 听后复述模板：80-120 词英文

Today’s programme explains how reading can shape the human brain. It points out that reading is not a natural ability we are born with, so when people learn to read, the brain has to use existing areas for a new purpose. The speakers also mention that different languages may influence the brain in different ways because reading systems are not all the same. One useful idea from the episode is that reading depends on an intricate circuit of brain areas rather than one single reading centre. Overall, the programme shows that reading is not just a school skill, but a powerful activity that can change how we process information.

## 4. 口语训练：从中式表达到 IELTS 7.0

### Part 1 问题 1

**Question**：Do you often use AI tools when you study English?

**普通答案思路**：  
说“会用”，比如查单词、改作文、练口语，但有时会依赖太多。

**7 分答案结构**：  
直接回答 + 具体用途 + 风险意识 + 个人策略。

**高分表达**：

- I use AI mainly as a learning assistant rather than a shortcut.
- It helps me notice mistakes that I might otherwise miss.
- I try not to let it replace my own thinking.

### Part 1 问题 2

**Question**：Do you prefer reading on paper or on a screen?

**普通答案思路**：  
纸质书更专注，屏幕更方便。

**7 分答案结构**：  
比较两者 + 场景区分 + 给出个人偏好。

**高分表达**：

- For deep reading, I still prefer paper because it feels less distracting.
- Digital reading is more convenient when I need to search for information quickly.
- It depends on whether I’m reading for pleasure or for efficiency.

### Part 1 问题 3

**Question**：What skills do you think students should not lose in the age of AI?

**普通答案思路**：  
批判性思维、阅读能力、表达能力不能丢。

**7 分答案结构**：  
列出 2-3 个技能 + 解释为什么 + 联系现实学习。

**高分表达**：

- Critical thinking is probably the most important one.
- Students still need to evaluate whether an answer is reliable.
- Communication skills cannot be fully outsourced to technology.

### Part 2 Cue Card

**Describe a skill you learned that changed the way you think.**

You should say:

- what the skill is
- when and how you learned it
- why it was difficult at first
- and explain how it changed the way you think

**普通答案思路**：  
可以讲英语阅读、演讲、编程、做笔记、健身等。重点不是技能本身，而是它如何改变你的思维方式。

**7 分答案结构**：

1. 开头明确技能：I’d like to talk about...
2. 说明学习过程：At first..., but gradually...
3. 描述困难：The hardest part was...
4. 升华影响：It trained me to think more logically / independently / patiently.

**高分表达**：

- It changed the way I process information.
- At the beginning, I found it mentally demanding.
- Over time, I became more comfortable dealing with complex ideas.
- It taught me to slow down and examine things more carefully.

### Part 3 深度追问 1

**Question**：Do you think AI will make people less intelligent?

**普通答案思路**：  
不会直接让人变笨，但可能让人懒得思考。

**7 分答案结构**：  
平衡观点 + 区分使用方式 + 给出例子。

**高分表达**：

- I wouldn’t say AI makes people less intelligent by itself.
- The real issue is how passively people use it.
- If users simply accept AI-generated answers, their reasoning skills may weaken.

### Part 3 深度追问 2

**Question**：Should schools restrict students’ use of AI?

**普通答案思路**：  
不能完全禁止，但要规范。

**7 分答案结构**：  
反对一刀切 + 支持明确规则 + 强调学习目标。

**高分表达**：

- A complete ban would probably be unrealistic.
- Schools should teach students how to use AI responsibly.
- The priority should be learning, not just producing a polished answer.

### Part 3 深度追问 3

**Question**：Why is reading still important when information is available in videos and short posts?

**普通答案思路**：  
阅读更能训练深度思考，短视频容易碎片化。

**7 分答案结构**：  
说明阅读优势 + 对比短内容 + 联系认知能力。

**高分表达**：

- Reading forces people to follow a line of reasoning.
- Short videos are useful, but they often simplify complex issues.
- Deep reading builds patience and analytical ability.

### Part 3 深度追问 4

**Question**：What responsibilities do professionals have when using AI at work?

**普通答案思路**：  
不能盲信 AI，要检查结果，对后果负责。

**7 分答案结构**：  
指出责任主体 + 说明检查义务 + 强调高风险行业。

**高分表达**：

- Professionals should remain accountable for the final decision.
- AI output needs to be checked against human expertise.
- This is especially important in high-stakes fields such as medicine and law.
