## 2. 阅读训练：从“看懂”到“雅思阅读速度”

**文章中文摘要，约 190 词**

这篇文章讨论了一个越来越现实的问题：人们在工作和学习中过度依赖 AI，是否会导致自身技能退化。文章引用医学和计算机科学领域的研究指出，AI 工具虽然能短期提高表现，却可能削弱使用者真正掌握任务的能力。在医学场景中，一些经验丰富的内镜医生在使用 AI 辅助检测后，当 AI 不可用时，独立检测表现明显下降。软件工程实验也显示，使用 AI 助手完成编码任务的人，在之后的知识测试中表现更差，尤其不擅长解释或诊断代码错误。专家认为，这种现象说明“完成任务”和“真正学习”之间可能出现脱节：人们看似效率更高，但实际是在借用 AI 的能力，而不是发展自己的能力。文章最后强调，AI 并不一定要被禁止，但使用者必须清楚自己把哪些认知工作外包给了 AI，并保持批判性检查，避免在便利中失去基础能力。

**8 个核心词汇或短语**

1. **over-reliance**
   - 中文：过度依赖
   - 语境：文章讨论专业人士对 AI 系统依赖过多可能带来的风险。
   - 可替换表达：excessive dependence / depending too heavily on

2. **deskilling**
   - 中文：技能退化；去技能化
   - 语境：AI 可能让使用者逐渐失去原本需要亲自完成的专业能力。
   - 可替换表达：skill erosion / loss of expertise

3. **atrophy**
   - 中文：萎缩；退化
   - 语境：用于描述长期不用某项能力后，该能力可能变弱。
   - 可替换表达：weaken / deteriorate / decline

4. **AI assistance**
   - 中文：AI 辅助
   - 语境：医生或程序员在完成任务时得到 AI 工具帮助。
   - 可替换表达：AI support / help from artificial intelligence

5. **detection rate**
   - 中文：检测率；发现率
   - 语境：医学研究中比较医生在有无 AI 时发现病变的比例。
   - 可替换表达：identification rate / rate of finding cases

6. **randomized controlled trial**
   - 中文：随机对照试验
   - 语境：软件工程研究用实验方式比较 AI 使用者和非使用者的学习效果。
   - 可替换表达：controlled experiment / randomized study

7. **cognitive decisions**
   - 中文：认知决策；思维判断
   - 语境：文章强调 AI 影响的不只是体力任务，也包括判断、分析和解释。
   - 可替换表达：mental judgments / reasoning-based decisions

8. **mindfully vigilant**
   - 中文：有意识地保持警惕
   - 语境：专家建议使用 AI 时不能盲信输出，而要持续检查和反思。
   - 可替换表达：critically alert / consciously cautious

**长难句拆解**

句子：

**The findings are of concern, especially for students and young professionals in the field.**

结构拆解：

- **The findings**：主语，指前文实验结果。
- **are of concern**：系表结构，意思是“令人担忧”。这是比 **are concerning** 更正式的写法。
- **especially for...**：补充说明担忧对象，重点落在学生和年轻从业者身上。
- **in the field**：限定范围，指相关专业领域。

逻辑关系：

这句话把“研究发现”与“潜在受影响人群”连接起来。文章不是只说 AI 有风险，而是进一步指出：风险对早期学习者更大，因为他们还没有建立牢固的基础能力。

可模仿表达：

- **This trend is of concern, especially for young people who are still developing basic skills.**
- **The policy is of concern, particularly for low-income families.**
- **The results are of concern because they reveal a gap between speed and real understanding.**

## 4. 口语训练：从中式表达到 IELTS 7.0

### Speaking Part 1

**Question 1: Do you often use AI tools in your study or work?**

- 普通答案思路：说自己会用 AI 查资料、改句子、练口语，但不会完全依赖。
- 7 分答案结构：直接回答 + 具体用途 + 风险意识 + 简短评价。
- 高分表达：
  - **I use AI as a study assistant rather than a replacement for my own thinking.**
  - **It helps me save time, but I still need to check whether the answer makes sense.**
  - **The biggest benefit is instant feedback, especially when I practise speaking or vocabulary.**

示范答案：

Yes, I use AI quite often, especially when I study English. For example, I ask it to correct my sentences, explain difficult words and give me speaking questions. However, I try to use it as a study assistant rather than a replacement for my own thinking. If I simply copy the answer, I may finish the task faster, but I won’t really improve.

**Question 2: Are people in your country interested in driverless cars?**

- 普通答案思路：很多人感兴趣，但也担心安全和责任问题。
- 7 分答案结构：总体趋势 + 两类态度 + 具体原因。
- 高分表达：
  - **People are curious but not fully convinced.**
  - **Safety is still the biggest barrier to public trust.**
  - **Many people want convenience, but they also want clear accountability.**

示范答案：

I think people are curious but not fully convinced. On the one hand, driverless cars sound convenient because they may reduce fatigue and improve traffic efficiency. On the other hand, many people still worry about safety, especially in complicated road situations. Another issue is accountability: if an accident happens, people want to know who should be responsible.

**Question 3: What skills do you think technology may weaken?**

- 普通答案思路：记忆、方向感、写作、独立思考。
- 7 分答案结构：列举技能 + 解释机制 + 个人例子。
- 高分表达：
  - **Technology can quietly weaken skills that we no longer practise.**
  - **When a tool does the thinking for us, our own judgement may become less sharp.**
  - **Convenience can come at the cost of independence.**

示范答案：

Technology can quietly weaken skills that we no longer practise. For example, many people rely on map apps so much that their sense of direction becomes worse. AI may have a similar effect on writing or problem-solving. When a tool does the thinking for us, our own judgement may become less sharp.

### Speaking Part 2

**Cue Card**

Describe a technology that has changed the way people learn or work.

You should say:

- what the technology is
- how people use it
- what advantages it brings
- and explain whether you think people rely on it too much

**普通答案思路**

可以讲 AI 工具。先说它是什么，再讲学习英语、写邮件、做研究中的用途；优点是快、方便、有反馈；最后转折说过度依赖会削弱思考和表达能力。

**7 分答案结构**

1. 开头明确对象：I’d like to talk about generative AI.
2. 具体场景：language learning, writing, coding, workplace research。
3. 双面评价：efficiency vs skill erosion。
4. 个人立场：use it deliberately, not passively。

**高分表达**

- **It has become a part of people’s daily workflow.**
- **The main advantage is that it lowers the barrier to complex tasks.**
- **However, there is a risk that people may confuse fast output with real understanding.**
- **I believe the key is to use it deliberately, not passively.**

**90 秒参考框架**

I’d like to talk about generative AI, because it has changed the way many people study and work. Students use it to explain difficult concepts, polish essays and practise languages, while office workers may use it to draft emails, summarise documents or analyse information. The biggest advantage is efficiency. It lowers the barrier to complex tasks and gives people instant feedback. For example, when I practise English, AI can correct my grammar and suggest more natural expressions. However, I do think some people rely on it too much. If they simply accept every answer without checking it, they may confuse fast output with real understanding. In my opinion, AI is useful when it supports our thinking, but harmful when it replaces the effort of thinking.

### Speaking Part 3

**Question 1: Should schools allow students to use AI tools?**

- 普通答案思路：应该允许，但要有规则。
- 7 分答案结构：不能简单禁止 + 需要 AI literacy + 改变作业设计。
- 高分表达：
  - **A complete ban would be unrealistic.**
  - **Schools should teach students how to use AI responsibly.**
  - **Assessment needs to focus more on process, reasoning and originality.**

**Question 2: What responsibilities should companies have when developing driverless cars?**

- 普通答案思路：安全测试、透明说明、事故责任。
- 7 分答案结构：技术安全 + 公共沟通 + 法律责任。
- 高分表达：
  - **Companies should not treat public roads as experimental spaces.**
  - **They need to make the system’s intentions understandable to ordinary road users.**
  - **There should be clear accountability when something goes wrong.**

**Question 3: Do you think automation will make people less capable in the future?**

- 普通答案思路：有可能，但取决于使用方式。
- 7 分答案结构：区分 routine tasks 和 core skills + 举例 + 条件判断。
- 高分表达：
  - **Automation does not automatically make people less capable, but passive dependence does.**
  - **Some routine skills may become less important, while critical thinking becomes more valuable.**
  - **The problem begins when people stop understanding the process behind the result.**

**Question 4: How can individuals avoid becoming too dependent on technology?**

- 普通答案思路：定期不用工具、自己先思考、检查答案。
- 7 分答案结构：先独立完成 + 用工具反馈 + 保留基础训练。
- 高分表达：
  - **People should use technology after making an initial attempt on their own.**
  - **They need to keep practising the underlying skill.**
  - **A useful rule is: let technology improve your answer, not produce your answer from zero.**
