## 2. 阅读训练：从“看懂”到“雅思阅读速度”

### 文章中文摘要

这篇 AP News 文章介绍了一项关于美国学校手机禁令的大规模研究。研究使用约 4,600 所学校的数据，重点观察那些通过可上锁手机袋实施全天手机限制的学校。结果并不是简单的“有效”或“无效”：手机禁令确实显著减少了学生在校期间的手机使用，教师也报告课堂干扰有所下降；但在短期内，学生成绩、出勤率、注意力和网络欺凌等指标并没有明显改善。研究还发现，禁令实施第一年，停学等纪律处分可能上升，学生主观幸福感也可能下降，但这些负面变化在后续年份有所缓和。专家认为，这说明手机禁令可能是恢复课堂注意力的必要前提，但不能被视为快速提高成绩的万能方案。学校仍需要配合更系统的支持，例如学生心理健康、课堂管理和线下社交环境建设。

### 8 个核心词汇或短语

| 英文 | 中文 | 原文语境 | 可替换表达 |
|---|---|---|---|
| cell phone ban | 手机禁令 | 学校限制学生在校期间使用手机 | phone restriction / smartphone policy |
| mixed results | 喜忧参半的结果 | 研究发现禁令有效减少手机使用，但其他效果有限 | inconclusive findings / uneven outcomes |
| academic achievement | 学业成绩 | 文章讨论禁令是否能提高标准化考试表现 | academic performance / learning outcomes |
| discipline | 纪律处分；行为管理 | 禁令初期可能导致停学率上升 | behavioural control / school conduct |
| subjective well-being | 主观幸福感 | 学生自我报告的心理状态和感受 | self-reported well-being / mental state |
| enforcement | 执行；落实 | 手机禁令是否被严格执行影响研究可信度 | implementation / rule application |

#### Question 5：Summary Completion

Complete the summary with words from the box.

**Box**：discipline / attention / immediate / mixed / policy / adults

The study presents a more ______ picture of school phone bans than many supporters expected. While the bans reduced phone use, they did not produce strong ______ academic gains. In the first year, schools even saw some issues with ______, although these effects appeared to weaken later.

### 答案与解析

1. **False**  
解析：文章指出学业成绩提升很有限，不能说“immediately improved”。

2. **True**  
解析：研究发现手机禁令确实大幅减少学生在校期间的手机使用，这是文章最明确的结论之一。

3. **B**  
解析：文章核心不是宣传禁令，而是呈现大型研究的复杂结果：手机使用下降，但成绩、出勤、欺凌等效果有限。

4. **C**  
解析：可上锁手机袋的数据比“学校声称有禁令”更可靠，因为它更能证明手机确实被限制使用。

5. **mixed / immediate / discipline**  
解析：文章整体结论是 mixed results；学业效果不是 immediate；第一年出现 discipline 相关问题。

### 长难句拆解

**训练句**：  
Although school phone bans appear to reduce students’ access to devices during the day, the study found little evidence that these policies quickly translate into higher test scores, better attendance, or improved classroom attention.

**结构拆解**：  
- **Although school phone bans appear to reduce...**：让步状语从句，先承认“手机禁令确实有作用”。  
- **the study found little evidence that...**：主句，核心信息是“研究几乎没有发现证据”。  
- **these policies quickly translate into...**：that 引导的宾语从句，说明“没有证据证明什么”。  
- **higher test scores, better attendance, or improved classroom attention**：三个并列结果，典型雅思阅读和写作表达。

**逻辑关系**：  
“虽然 A 有效果，但不能证明 B、C、D 会立刻改善。”这是 IELTS 7.0 很重要的让步逻辑。

**可模仿表达**：  
Although X appears to reduce Y, there is little evidence that it can quickly translate into Z.

例句：  
Although online learning appears to offer greater flexibility, there is little evidence that it can quickly translate into better academic outcomes for all students.

### 易听错点提醒

- **连读**：spend too much time on screens 中，time on 容易连成 /taɪmɒn/。  
- **弱读**：children spend too much time 中的 to、too、and、of 等功能词会弱化，别逐词等中文对应。  
- **近音词**：screen /skriːn/ 和 scream /skriːm/；measure /ˈmeʒə/ 和 major /ˈmeɪdʒə/。  
- **数字信息**：注意 one in five / one in ten / one in twenty 这类比例表达。  
- **人名和身份**：听到 expert、clinical psychologist、research associate 时，重点抓“观点”，不必纠结职称每个词。  
- **转折信号**：but, rather than, however, instead, not as much as... but... 这些词后面通常是答案重点。

### 听后复述模板：80-120 词英文

Today’s episode discusses children’s screen time and whether it should be limited. The speakers explain that many parents are worried about the amount of time children spend on smartphones, tablets and laptops. Some governments are also considering online safety measures, especially for social media. However, the experts do not simply argue that children should be kept away from technology. Instead, they suggest that parents should be more intentional and that technology products should be designed with children’s development in mind. A useful practical idea is to make small changes at home, such as moving devices out of sight, so children are better supported rather than simply blamed.

## 4. 口语训练：从中式表达到 IELTS 7.0

### Part 1 问题 1

**Question**：How much time do you usually spend on your phone every day?

**普通答案思路**：  
说一个大概时间，比如 3-4 小时；解释主要用于学习、聊天、刷短视频。

**7 分答案结构**：  
直接回答 + 区分学习和娱乐 + 轻微反思。

**高分表达**：  
- I’d say I spend roughly three to four hours a day on my phone.  
- Part of it is productive, but quite a lot of it is just mindless scrolling.  
- I’m trying to be more intentional about how I use it.

### Part 1 问题 2

**Question**：Do you think students should be allowed to use phones at school?

**普通答案思路**：  
可以说“上课不应该用，但紧急情况可以用”。

**7 分答案结构**：  
平衡观点 + 场景区分 + 具体理由。

**高分表达**：  
- I don’t think it should be a simple yes-or-no issue.  
- During lessons, phones can be a major distraction.  
- But outside class, limited access might be reasonable for safety reasons.

### Part 1 问题 3

**Question**：What do you usually do when you want to reduce your screen time?

**普通答案思路**：  
关通知、把手机放远、设定使用时间。

**7 分答案结构**：  
方法 + 为什么有效 + 个人例子。

**高分表达**：  
- I turn off unnecessary notifications.  
- I put my phone somewhere out of reach when I study.  
- It reduces the temptation to check it every few minutes.

### Part 2 Cue Card

**Describe a rule about technology that you think is useful.**

You should say:  
- what the rule is  
- where or when people should follow it  
- why it is useful  
- and explain whether it would be difficult to follow

**普通答案思路**：  
讲“上课期间禁止使用手机”。说它能减少分心、提高课堂互动，但学生一开始会不适应。

**7 分答案结构**：  
1. 开头明确规则：phone-free classrooms  
2. 解释使用场景：during lessons, especially in secondary schools  
3. 说明好处：attention, interaction, discipline  
4. 让步：not a magic solution, needs support  
5. 总结：useful if implemented fairly

**高分表达**：  
- A rule I find quite sensible is a phone-free classroom policy.  
- It creates a better learning environment by reducing constant interruptions.  
- That said, it shouldn’t be implemented in a purely punitive way.  
- Schools need to explain the purpose of the rule and provide offline support.  
- In the long run, it could help students rebuild their attention span.

### Part 3 深度追问 1

**Question**：Do you think banning phones can improve students’ academic performance?

**普通答案思路**：  
可能有帮助，但不能保证成绩提高。

**7 分答案结构**：  
观点 + 解释机制 + 限制 + 结论。

**高分表达**：  
- It may improve the conditions for learning, but it doesn’t automatically improve learning itself.  
- Reducing distractions is only the first step.  
- Students still need effective teaching, motivation and study habits.

### Part 3 深度追问 2

**Question**：Why are young people so attracted to smartphones?

**普通答案思路**：  
社交、娱乐、即时反馈、害怕错过信息。

**7 分答案结构**：  
心理需求 + 技术设计 + 社交压力。

**高分表达**：  
- Smartphones satisfy several needs at once: entertainment, communication and identity.  
- Many apps are designed to reward users instantly.  
- For teenagers, being offline can sometimes feel like being socially excluded.

### Part 3 深度追问 3

**Question**：Should governments regulate children’s use of social media?

**普通答案思路**：  
应该有一定监管，但不能完全靠政府。

**7 分答案结构**：  
支持有限监管 + 家庭和学校责任 + 隐私风险。

**高分表达**：  
- Some regulation is necessary because children are not always able to judge online risks.  
- However, regulation alone cannot replace digital literacy education.  
- The challenge is to protect children without creating excessive surveillance.

### Part 3 深度追问 4

**Question**：How can schools help students build healthier digital habits?

**普通答案思路**：  
教数字素养，设置规则，增加线下活动。

**7 分答案结构**：  
三点式回答：rules + education + alternatives。

**高分表达**：  
- Schools should combine clear rules with digital literacy lessons.  
- Students need to understand how algorithms shape their attention.  
- More importantly, schools should offer attractive offline activities, so phones are not the only source of stimulation.
