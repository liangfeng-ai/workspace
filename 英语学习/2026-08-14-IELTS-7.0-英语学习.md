## 2. 阅读训练：从“看懂”到“雅思阅读速度”

**文章中文摘要，约 190 词**

这篇文章讨论了美国学校引入生成式 AI 后引发的家长和专家担忧。文章以纽约一位家长 Kelly Clancy 的经历开头：她的孩子被要求用 Google Gemini 给科学实验提反馈，她认为这会让孩子习惯让机器替自己思考。随后文章指出，纽约、俄勒冈等地的家长组织和儿童权益团体正在推动限制或暂停学校使用面向学生的 AI 工具。支持者认为，AI 可以帮助学生理解复杂内容、提升学习效率，也可能帮助有阅读障碍等特殊需求的学生。但反对者强调，目前缺乏充分证据证明 AI 真能促进儿童学习，反而可能造成 cognitive off-loading，即学生把本该自己完成的思考外包给工具。文章还提到，部分教育科技公司已经与多个学区合作，一些教师联盟也接受了科技公司的 AI 培训资助。最终，争论的核心不是“是否完全禁止技术”，而是学校应如何设置年龄限制、证据标准、家长参与和使用规范，避免让技术公司主导儿童学习方式。

**Questions 6-8: Summary Completion**

Complete the summary with words from the box.

Some parents and experts argue that schools should introduce stronger 6. ______ before allowing AI tools into classrooms. In several places, parents have asked for a 7. ______ on certain student-facing AI products. Their concern is that children may become less willing to practise 8. ______ if AI gives them answers too easily.

**答案与解析**

1. True  
解析：文章开头的家长案例明确说明，她担心孩子学会让机器代替自己思考。

2. False  
解析：文章说有家长和组织推动限制或暂停，但并不是所有美国学校都已经禁止。

3. True  
解析：支持者提到，AI 可能帮助有阅读障碍等学习困难的学生。

4. B  
解析：全文重点是报道家长、专家、学校、科技公司之间围绕 AI 进课堂的争议。

5. B  
解析：cognitive off-loading 指把思考负担转移给外部工具，这是专家担忧的核心之一。

6. guardrails  
解析：这里指学校在使用 AI 前需要明确限制和保护规则。

7. moratorium  
解析：a moratorium on student-facing AI products 是文章中的关键政策诉求。

8. critical thinking  
解析：文章多次围绕“孩子是否还会独立思考”展开。

**长难句拆解**

改写句：  
While technology companies argue that AI can help students learn more efficiently, many parents and child-development experts say there is still little evidence that these tools improve learning, and they may even weaken children’s ability to think independently.

结构拆解：

- `While technology companies argue that...`：让步状语从句，表示“虽然科技公司认为……”
- `AI can help students learn more efficiently`：that 引导的宾语从句，是 argue 的内容。
- `many parents and child-development experts say...`：主句，真正表达作者要强调的另一方观点。
- `there is still little evidence that...`：there be 句型，表示“证据不足”。
- `and they may even weaken...`：并列补充，进一步说明潜在负面影响。

可模仿表达：

- While some people argue that..., others believe there is little evidence that...
- The key concern is not whether..., but whether...
- These tools may improve efficiency, but they could also weaken...

**易听错点提醒**

- 连读：`spend too much time` 容易听成一串，要按意群听：spend / too much time。
- 弱读：`to be`, `of`, `for`, `and` 在 BBC 对话里会明显弱化，不要逐词硬听。
- 近音：`shift` 不要听成 `shit`；注意 /f/ 音。
- 数字：`one in twenty`, `one in ten`, `one in five` 是听力选择题常见陷阱，要记录完整比例。
- 转折信号：`rather than`, `but`, `therefore` 后面通常是说话人真正态度。
- 抽象词：`intentional`, `enable`, `expectations` 这些词在 IELTS 7.0 口语和写作中都很有用。

**听后复述模板，80-120 词**

The speakers explain that screen time includes the use of smartphones, tablets and laptops, and many parents are worried that children use these devices too much. One practical idea is to move devices out of sight, because it is difficult for children to resist a screen when it is nearby. Overall, the episode suggests that small changes at home can help children build healthier habits.

## 4. 口语训练：从中式表达到 IELTS 7.0

**Part 1 Question 1: How often do you use AI tools or digital devices for studying?**

普通答案思路：  
我经常用手机、电脑、AI 查资料、背单词，但有时会分心。

7 分答案结构：  
频率 + 具体用途 + 优点 + 小缺点

可直接套用的高分表达：

- I use digital tools on a daily basis, mainly for vocabulary review and quick research.
- They save me a lot of time, but they can also be distracting if I don’t set clear limits.
- I try to use them as a support tool rather than a replacement for thinking.

**Part 1 Question 2: Do you think children should have their own smartphones?**

普通答案思路：  
不应该太早拥有，可以用于联系父母，但娱乐和社交媒体要限制。

7 分答案结构：  
部分同意 + 年龄限制 + 使用目的 + 家长监督

可直接套用的高分表达：

- I don’t think very young children need their own smartphones.
- For safety reasons, a basic phone might be useful, but unrestricted internet access is a different matter.
- Parents should set boundaries instead of giving children unlimited freedom online.

**Part 1 Question 3: What do you usually do to avoid being distracted by your phone?**

普通答案思路：  
关通知，把手机放远，用番茄钟。

7 分答案结构：  
具体方法 + 为什么有效 + 个人例子

可直接套用的高分表达：

- I usually put my phone out of reach when I need to concentrate.
- Turning off notifications helps me stay focused for longer stretches of time.
- It sounds simple, but changing the environment often works better than relying on willpower.

**Part 2 Cue Card**

Describe a time when technology helped you learn something.

You should say:

- what technology you used
- what you were trying to learn
- how it helped you
- and explain whether you think it improved your learning effectively

普通答案思路：  
讲一次用 App / AI / YouTube 学英语、听力或专业知识的经历。

7 分答案结构：

1. 简短交代背景：什么时候、为什么学  
2. 描述工具：用什么功能  
3. 展示效果：效率、反馈、可重复练习  
4. 加入反思：技术有帮助，但不能代替主动思考

可直接套用的高分表达：

- It gave me instant feedback, which made the learning process more efficient.
- I could pause, replay and compare my answer with the original version.
- The biggest benefit was convenience, but I still had to process the information actively.
- Technology worked best when I had a clear goal instead of using it passively.

**Part 3 Question 1: Should schools allow students to use AI for homework?**

普通答案思路：  
可以有限使用，比如查资料、改语法，但不能直接生成答案。

7 分答案结构：  
承认现实 + 区分用途 + 提出规则

可直接套用的高分表达：

- A complete ban may be unrealistic, but unrestricted use would be harmful.
- Schools need to distinguish between using AI for support and using it to outsource the whole task.
- The focus should be on transparency, clear guidelines and students’ own reasoning.

**Part 3 Question 2: What are the risks of children relying too much on technology?**

普通答案思路：  
注意力下降、独立思考变弱、社交减少。

7 分答案结构：  
列 2-3 个风险 + 举例 + 上升到长期影响

可直接套用的高分表达：

- Excessive reliance on technology may weaken children’s attention span.
- It can also reduce opportunities for problem-solving and face-to-face communication.
- In the long run, children may become less comfortable with uncertainty and mental effort.

**Part 3 Question 3: Can AI replace teachers in the future?**

普通答案思路：  
不能完全替代，AI 可以辅助，但老师提供情感支持和判断。

7 分答案结构：  
明确立场 + AI 能做什么 + 教师不可替代的部分

可直接套用的高分表达：

- AI can deliver information, but teaching is not just information delivery.
- Good teachers notice confusion, motivate students and adapt to individual needs.
- Human interaction is especially important for younger learners.

**Part 3 Question 4: How should governments regulate AI tools used by children?**

普通答案思路：  
年龄限制、隐私保护、透明度、学校审批。

7 分答案结构：  
监管原则 + 具体措施 + 平衡创新和保护

可直接套用的高分表达：

- Governments should require stronger evidence before AI tools are used with children.
- Data privacy, age-appropriate design and parental consent should be basic requirements.
- Regulation should not block innovation, but it must put children’s development first.
