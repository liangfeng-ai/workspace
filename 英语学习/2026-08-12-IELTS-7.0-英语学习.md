## 2. 阅读训练：从“看懂”到“雅思阅读速度”

### 文章中文摘要 150-250 词

2026 年《Digital News Report》指出，全球新闻消费正在进一步“平台化”：越来越多的人不再主要通过电视、报纸或新闻机构自己的官网和 App 获取新闻，而是在社交媒体、视频平台和部分 AI chatbot 中接触新闻。报告显示，社交媒体和视频网络首次成为全球范围内最常见的新闻获取渠道之一，尤其在年轻群体中更明显。与此同时，传统电视新闻和新闻网站的使用率继续下降。报告并不简单地认为这是一次突然转变，而更像是一种长期“漂移”：人们日常使用平台时顺便接触新闻，而不是专门打开新闻网站。文章还指出，AI chatbot 用于新闻的比例正在增长，但仍未爆发式普及；用户更看重其解释和追问功能。另一个重要问题是信任：虽然平台方便、覆盖面广，但受众往往认为社交媒体和视频平台上的新闻可靠性较低。因此，新闻机构面临的核心挑战是：如何在受众迁移到第三方平台的同时，继续保持可信度、影响力和商业可持续性。

### 8 个核心词汇或短语

| 英文 | 中文 | 原文语境 | 可替换表达 |
|---|---|---|---|
| platformisation | 平台化 | 新闻消费越来越依赖社交媒体、视频网络和 AI 工具 | shift towards platforms |
| news consumption | 新闻消费 / 新闻获取 | 报告讨论不同群体如何获取新闻 | news use / access to news |
| social media and video networks | 社交媒体和视频网络 | 成为重要新闻来源 | third-party platforms |
| overtake | 超过，赶超 | 平台新闻使用率超过部分传统渠道 | surpass / move ahead of |
| AI chatbots | AI 聊天机器人 | 一部分人开始用 chatbot 获取新闻解释 | AI assistants / conversational AI |
| audience reach | 受众覆盖 | 新闻机构担心在自有平台上的触达能力下降 | audience access / public reach |
| reliability | 可靠性 | 用户认为平台新闻不一定可信 | trustworthiness / credibility |
| generational contrast | 代际差异 | 年轻人与年长群体的新闻来源不同 | age-based difference |

Complete the summary using ONE phrase from the box.

The report argues that news habits are shaped by the growing ________ of news consumption. Although platforms are convenient, they also raise concerns about trust and credibility.

### 答案与解析

1. **True**  
解析：报告指出平台新闻变得更突出，与电视新闻和新闻机构自有网站 / App 的下降有关。

2. **False**  
解析：AI chatbot 用于新闻的比例在增长，但并不是全球多数人的主要新闻来源。

3. **Not Given**  
解析：文章讨论新闻机构面临挑战，但没有说它们应该停止投资官网和 App。

4. **B**  
解析：报告提到用户喜欢 chatbot 的解释深度和追问功能，尤其是能继续提问。

5. **C**  
解析：文章把这种变化描述为长期“drift”，不是突然彻底的 revolution。

6. **B**  
解析：年轻群体更倾向通过社交媒体、视频平台和 AI 工具获取新闻。

7. **A**  
解析：新闻机构必须面对受众被第三方平台分流的问题。

8. **platformisation**  
解析：全文核心概念就是新闻消费的平台化。

### 长难句拆解

**句子**：  
As traditional sources lose ground, news organisations are increasingly forced to compete for attention on platforms where audiences may encounter journalism alongside entertainment, personal content and unreliable information.

**结构拆解**：

- **As traditional sources lose ground**：时间 + 原因状语，表示“随着传统来源逐渐失势”。
- **news organisations are increasingly forced to compete for attention**：主句，核心是“新闻机构被迫争夺注意力”。
- **on platforms where...**：地点状语 + 定语从句，说明竞争发生在哪里。
- **audiences may encounter journalism alongside...**：解释平台环境复杂，新闻和娱乐、私人内容、不可靠信息混在一起。

**逻辑关系**：  
传统渠道下降 → 新闻机构进入平台竞争 → 平台信息环境复杂 → 可信新闻更难被识别。

**可模仿表达**：  
As traditional ways of learning lose ground, students are increasingly forced to develop digital skills in environments where useful information appears alongside distractions and low-quality content.

## 3. 听力训练：精听 + 抓主旨

2. mistakes  
3. confidence

### 第一遍泛听任务：回答 3 个主旨问题

1. Why do many learners feel nervous when speaking a foreign language?  
2. What attitude towards mistakes does the episode encourage learners to develop?  
3. What practical suggestion can help learners become less afraid of speaking?

### 第二遍精听任务：6 句关键表达听写练习

以下句子为基于本期主题改写的 IELTS 训练句。听完材料后，先遮住文本，自己尝试听写 / 跟读，再核对。

1. Many learners feel anxious because they are afraid of making mistakes in front of other people.  
2. Speaking a foreign language can feel stressful even when your grammar and vocabulary are quite good.  
3. The more you practise short conversations, the more natural your responses will become.  
4. Instead of aiming for perfect English, learners should focus on communicating clearly.  
5. Confidence usually grows when people receive encouragement and practical feedback.  
6. One useful strategy is to prepare common phrases before joining a real conversation.  
7. If you pause or forget a word, you can rephrase your idea in a simpler way.  
8. Regular speaking practice helps reduce fear and builds automatic language habits.

### 易听错点提醒

- **连读**：`feel anxious` 容易听成 `fee-lanxious`；`focus on` 容易连成 `focuson`。  
- **弱读**：`can`, `to`, `of`, `and` 在自然语速里会弱化，不要逐词等重音。  
- **近音词**：`fear` / `feel`，`stressful` / `successful`，`practice` / `practise`。  
- **转折信号**：注意 `but`, `even though`, `instead of`, `however`，这些后面常是观点重点。  
- **抽象名词**：`anxiety`, `confidence`, `fluency`, `communication` 是口语复述高频词。  
- **停顿处理**：BBC 对话中说话人会用停顿、重复和补充解释，不要因为一句没听清就停止理解。

### 听后复述模板：80-120 词英文

The episode discusses why many learners feel nervous when they have to speak English or another foreign language. A common reason is that they worry about making mistakes or being judged by others. However, the speakers suggest that mistakes are a normal part of learning and should not stop people from communicating. One useful idea is to practise short, realistic conversations instead of waiting until your English is perfect. The episode also reminds learners that confidence develops gradually through repeated practice, encouragement and practical feedback. For IELTS speaking, this means I should focus on clear communication, not perfect sentences.

## 4. 口语训练：从中式表达到 IELTS 7.0

### IELTS Speaking Part 1

#### Question 1: Do you often read news online?

**普通答案思路**：  
说自己经常看新闻，主要通过手机、社交媒体或新闻 App，看科技、教育、社会类内容。

**7 分答案结构**：  
直接回答 → 说明频率 → 说明渠道 → 解释原因 → 加一个具体例子。

**高分表达**：  
- I tend to get most of my news online.  
- It is convenient, but I try to be selective.  
- I usually cross-check important stories.

**示范答案**：  
Yes, I tend to get most of my news online, usually through news apps or short videos. It is convenient because I can catch up with major stories during breaks. However, I try to be selective, especially when the news comes from social media. For important topics, such as health or education, I usually cross-check the information with more reliable sources.

#### Question 2: What kind of news are you most interested in?

**普通答案思路**：  
科技、教育、社会变化，因为和学习、未来职业有关。

**7 分答案结构**：  
主题偏好 → 为什么相关 → 具体内容 → 个人影响。

**高分表达**：  
- I am particularly drawn to stories about...  
- They feel relevant to my daily life.  
- They help me understand broader social changes.

**示范答案**：  
I am particularly drawn to stories about technology and education because they feel relevant to my daily life. For example, I like reading about how AI is changing the way people study or work. These stories help me understand broader social changes, and they also give me useful ideas for my own learning.

#### Question 3: Do you trust news on social media?

**普通答案思路**：  
不完全信，社交媒体方便但信息质量参差不齐。

**7 分答案结构**：  
给出谨慎态度 → 承认优点 → 指出风险 → 说明自己的做法。

**高分表达**：  
- I would not say I fully trust it.  
- The quality can be quite uneven.  
- I pay attention to the source and evidence.

**示范答案**：  
I would not say I fully trust news on social media. It is fast and convenient, but the quality can be quite uneven. Some posts are accurate, while others are exaggerated or taken out of context. So I normally pay attention to the source and evidence before I believe or share anything.

### IELTS Speaking Part 2

#### Cue Card

Describe a time when you used English to get information online.

You should say:

- what information you wanted to find  
- where you found it  
- whether it was easy or difficult to understand  
- and explain how using English helped you

**普通答案思路**：  
讲一次查 IELTS 资料、科技新闻、课程信息或 YouTube 英文视频的经历。

**7 分答案结构**：  
背景一句 → 信息需求 → 查找过程 → 理解困难 → 学到什么 → 个人反思。

**高分表达**：  
- I came across a detailed article about...  
- At first, the vocabulary was a bit demanding.  
- It gave me access to a wider range of perspectives.  
- I realised that English is not just a subject, but a tool.

**示范答案框架**：  
One experience that comes to mind is when I used English to look up information about IELTS speaking practice. I came across a detailed article and several videos explaining how candidates can develop answers instead of memorising them. At first, the vocabulary was a bit demanding, especially words related to fluency and coherence. However, after reading the transcript and replaying some parts, I gradually understood the main ideas. Using English helped me because I could access more authentic advice and a wider range of perspectives. It also made me realise that English is not just a subject to pass, but a practical tool for learning.

### IELTS Speaking Part 3

#### Question 1: Why do young people increasingly get news from social media?

**普通答案思路**：  
方便、快、算法推送、视频更容易理解。

**7 分答案结构**：  
原因 1：便利性 → 原因 2：形式吸引人 → 风险：信息被动接收。

**高分表达**：  
- It fits naturally into their daily routines.  
- Short-form videos make information easier to digest.  
- The downside is that people may consume news passively.

#### Question 2: Should schools teach students how to judge online information?

**普通答案思路**：  
应该，因为假信息多，学生需要判断来源和证据。

**7 分答案结构**：  
明确同意 → 说明现实问题 → 说明学校能教什么 → 长期意义。

**高分表达**：  
- media literacy should be treated as a basic skill  
- distinguish evidence-based reporting from opinion  
- make more informed decisions

#### Question 3: Are traditional news organisations still important?

**普通答案思路**：  
重要，因为有专业编辑、事实核查、公共责任。

**7 分答案结构**：  
承认平台兴起 → 强调传统媒体价值 → 说明它们也要适应新渠道。

**高分表达**：  
- They still play a crucial role in public life.  
- Professional standards matter, especially during crises.  
- They need to adapt without sacrificing credibility.

#### Question 4: What problems might arise if people only read news recommended by algorithms?

**普通答案思路**：  
信息茧房、偏见加强、只看喜欢的观点。

**7 分答案结构**：  
解释算法推荐 → 后果 1：视野变窄 → 后果 2：观点极化 → 解决方式。

**高分表达**：  
- people may end up in an information bubble  
- it can reinforce existing beliefs  
- exposure to different viewpoints is essential
