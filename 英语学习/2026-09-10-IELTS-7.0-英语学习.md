## 2. 阅读训练：从“看懂”到“雅思阅读速度”

**文章中文摘要**

这篇文章讨论了美国学校中使用生成式 AI 所引发的争议。一方面，科技公司和部分教育倡导者认为，AI 工具可以帮助学生获得反馈、提高课堂效率，并让学生提前具备未来职场所需的 AI 素养。另一方面，越来越多家长、儿童发展专家和教育研究者担心，学生过早依赖 AI 会削弱独立思考能力，使教师从教学决策者变成技术支持人员。文章提到，纽约、俄勒冈等地已有家长组织请愿，要求暂停或限制学生直接使用生成式 AI。一些专家指出，目前关于 AI 是否真正提升 K-12 学生长期学习能力的证据仍然不足，尤其要区分“帮助完成任务”和“帮助形成可持续能力”。文章最后呈现了两种政策取向：一种主张设置禁令或暂停使用，另一种认为完全禁止不现实，更应通过 AI 素养教育让学生理解工具的局限。

| 词汇/短语 | 中文 | 原文语境 | 可替换表达 |
|---|---|---|---|

**Questions 6-8: Summary Completion**

Complete the summary with words from the list.

The article presents a debate about AI in schools. Supporters argue that AI can improve classroom efficiency and prepare students for future work. Critics, however, say there is limited **6. ______** that AI improves learning. They worry that students may rely on tools instead of developing **7. ______**. Some communities have therefore called for a temporary **8. ______** on student-facing AI.

Word list:  
evidence / confidence / foundational skills / entertainment / moratorium / punishment

**答案与解析**

1. True。文章提到纽约部分家长和议员支持暂停 AI 在学校中的使用。  
2. False。文章提到的是部分教师每周至少一次使用 AI，并不是“每节课”。  
3. False。文章强调长期学习效果证据不足，而不是已经被证明有效。  
4. B。核心担忧是学生把思考交给机器，削弱独立思考。  
5. C。支持者提到 AI 可能帮助有阅读障碍等学习困难的学生。  
6. evidence。搭配 limited evidence，表示“证据有限”。  
7. foundational skills。批评者强调基础学术能力。  
8. moratorium。表示临时暂停或暂缓实施。

**长难句拆解**

句子：While technology companies argue that AI can make classrooms more efficient, some parents and experts question whether these tools help children develop lasting skills or simply help them complete tasks faster.

- 主干：some parents and experts question whether...
- 让步从句：While technology companies argue that...
- 宾语从句：whether these tools help... or simply help...
- 对比逻辑：make classrooms more efficient vs develop lasting skills
- 可模仿表达：  
  - While some people argue that..., others question whether...  
  - The real issue is not whether..., but whether...

**第二遍精听任务：听写 8 句关键表达**

请听音频后暂停，逐句听写。先不要看 transcript。

1. One area of technology which has been in the news a lot recently is robotics.  
2. Humanoid robots appear and behave like humans.  
3. Not everyone feels comfortable with the idea of inviting a human-like robot into their home.  
4. With advances in AI, robots are becoming smart enough to develop meaningful relationships with humans.  
5. There are reports of people telling their secrets to robot therapists.

7. Because it’s important people feel safe around robots, engineers design them to look friendly.  
8. And as usual, we’ll learn some useful new vocabulary.

**易听错点提醒**

- 连读：with advances in AI 中 with advances 容易连成 /wɪðədˈvɑːnsɪz/。
- 弱读：of technology, to develop, for people 中 of / to / for 通常弱读。
- 近音：robotics / robots；human / humanoid；comfortable / companion。
- 人名地名：Abi / Melbourne / Australia，注意 Abi 不是 Abby 的普通拼写。
- 转折信号：but not everyone..., however, on the other hand 之后通常出现反方观点。
- 数字：如果听到年龄、年份、比例，先写数字，再补单位。

**听后复述模板：80-120 词英文**

The episode discusses whether people would like to live with a robot companion. It explains that humanoid robots are becoming more advanced because of artificial intelligence, and some of them are designed to look friendly so that people feel safe around them. The programme focuses on a home companion robot called Abi, which interacts with elderly residents in Melbourne. Although robots may offer help and emotional support, the speakers also suggest that not everyone is comfortable with forming relationships with machines. Overall, the episode raises an important question: can robots support human life without replacing real human connection?

## 4. 口语训练：从中式表达到 IELTS 7.0

**Part 1 Question 1: Do you often use AI tools when you study?**

- 普通答案思路：说会用，用来查单词、改句子、找资料。
- 7 分答案结构：直接回答 + 具体用途 + 限制意识。
- 高分表达：  
  - I use AI as a study assistant rather than a shortcut.  
  - It helps me clarify difficult concepts.  
  - I still try to do the thinking myself.

**Part 1 Question 2: Do you think technology makes students more independent?**

- 普通答案思路：有时会，有时不会。
- 7 分答案结构：平衡观点 + 条件判断 + 例子。
- 高分表达：  
  - It depends on how the tool is used.  
  - Technology can encourage independent learning if students use it critically.  
  - Over-reliance can weaken problem-solving skills.

**Part 1 Question 3: Would you like to have a robot assistant at home?**

- 普通答案思路：愿意，因为方便；但不想太像人。
- 7 分答案结构：功能接受 + 情感边界 + 现实例子。
- 高分表达：  
  - I would welcome it for practical tasks.  
  - I’d feel uneasy if it tried to imitate human emotions.  
  - Convenience should not come at the cost of real interaction.

**Part 2 Cue Card**

Describe a piece of technology that has changed the way people learn.

You should say:

- what it is
- how people use it
- what advantages it has
- and explain whether you think it is completely positive

- 普通答案思路：讲 AI、在线课程或翻译软件。
- 7 分答案结构：背景介绍 + 个人例子 + 优点 + 风险评估。
- 高分表达：  
  - It has lowered the barrier to learning.  
  - Learners can receive instant feedback.  
  - The downside is that students may become passive consumers of answers.  
  - Used wisely, it can amplify learning rather than replace it.

**Part 3 Question 1: Should schools ban students from using AI tools?**

- 普通答案思路：不应完全禁止，应管理。
- 7 分答案结构：反对绝对禁令 + 支持年龄分层 + 强调规则。
- 高分表达：  
  - A blanket ban may be unrealistic.  
  - Younger students need stricter boundaries.  
  - Schools should teach AI literacy alongside academic integrity.

**Part 3 Question 2: What skills will be more important in an AI-driven society?**

- 普通答案思路：批判思维、沟通、创造力。
- 7 分答案结构：列三类能力 + 解释为什么机器难替代。
- 高分表达：  
  - critical thinking  
  - human judgement  
  - emotional intelligence  
  - the ability to evaluate information rather than merely collect it

**Part 3 Question 3: Can robots replace human teachers?**

- 普通答案思路：不能完全替代。
- 7 分答案结构：AI 可辅助 + 教师提供情感支持和判断 + 结论。
- 高分表达：  
  - Robots may deliver information efficiently.  
  - Teaching is also about motivation, empathy and adaptation.  
  - A great teacher responds to subtle emotional signals.

**Part 3 Question 4: How can parents help children use technology responsibly?**

- 普通答案思路：限制时间，陪孩子用。
- 7 分答案结构：规则 + 示范 + 讨论风险。
- 高分表达：  
  - Parents should model healthy digital habits.  
  - Instead of simply policing screen time, they should discuss online choices.  
  - Children need guidance, not just restrictions.
