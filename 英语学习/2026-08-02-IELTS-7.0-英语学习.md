## 2. 阅读训练：从“看懂”到“雅思阅读速度”

**文章中文摘要**

这篇文章讨论了美国学校中生成式 AI 工具的快速扩散，以及家长和儿童发展专家对此产生的担忧。一些家长认为，学生在完成科学实验、写作反馈或课堂任务时过早依赖聊天机器人，可能会削弱他们独立思考、合作讨论和解决问题的能力。文章提到，纽约、俄勒冈等地已有家长组织和教育倡议团体要求暂停或限制面向学生的 AI 工具，尤其是小学阶段的使用。反对者担心，科技公司正在影响课堂决策，而教师被迫变成技术支持人员。支持者则认为，AI 能帮助学生理解复杂内容，也可能支持有阅读障碍等特殊需求的学生。文章的核心冲突不是“AI 是否有用”，而是“学校是否有足够证据、规则和家庭参与，来决定 AI 应该如何进入课堂”。对雅思阅读来说，这篇文章适合练习识别作者如何呈现不同观点，以及区分事实、主张和推论。

**8 个核心词汇或短语**

| 英文 | 中文 | 原文语境 | 可替换表达 |
|---|---|---|---|
| prompt concern | 引发担忧 | AI in the classroom prompts concern among parents and experts | raise alarm / trigger anxiety |
| generative AI | 生成式人工智能 | students use generative AI tools on school devices | AI content-generation tools |
| moratorium | 暂停令；暂缓使用 | parents call for a two-year moratorium on AI use | temporary ban / suspension |
| cognitive development | 认知发展 | experts worry AI may affect children’s cognitive development | mental growth / intellectual development |
| cognitive off-loading | 认知卸载；把思考外包 | students may use AI to avoid mental effort | outsourcing thinking / mental dependency |
| ubiquitous | 无处不在的 | AI is becoming ubiquitous in modern life | widespread / everywhere |
| foundational academic skills | 基础学术能力 | schools should focus on handwriting, reading and reasoning | core learning skills / basic academic abilities |
| guardrails | 保护性规则；限制措施 | schools are developing guardrails for AI use | safeguards / rules / boundaries |

**雅思阅读题型练习**

**Questions 1-2: True / False / Not Given**

1. Some parents in the article believe AI may encourage students to let machines do their thinking.  
2. The article states that all American schools have already banned generative AI tools for elementary students.

**Questions 3-4: Matching Information**

Match each statement with the correct group.

A. Parents and child development experts  
B. AI education advocates  
C. School authorities

3. They argue that AI can help students access difficult material or support learners with disabilities.  
4. They have begun discussing rules, approved tools or limits on educational technology.

**Question 5: Multiple Choice**

5. What is the main purpose of the article?

A. To prove that AI should be completely removed from all schools  
B. To show the growing debate over whether AI in classrooms is evidence-based and age-appropriate  
C. To explain how students can use AI to improve exam scores  
D. To compare American schools with British schools

**答案与解析**

1. **True**。文章中家长担心 AI 会让孩子把思考交给机器，这是核心担忧之一。  
2. **False**。文章提到一些地区和组织呼吁暂停或限制，但不是“所有美国学校已经禁止”。  
3. **B**。支持者认为 AI 可帮助学生理解复杂内容，也可辅助有阅读障碍的学生。  
4. **C**。学校和教育部门正在讨论 guardrails、approved applications、standards of use 等。  
5. **B**。文章不是单方面反 AI，而是呈现围绕证据、年龄、规则、家庭参与的争议。

**长难句拆解**

原句：some parents and child development experts argue that there is little evidence that AI helps children and may even harm their cognitive development.

结构拆解：

- 主语：some parents and child development experts
- 谓语：argue
- 宾语从句：that there is little evidence...
- evidence 后的同位/解释内容：that AI helps children
- 转折递进：and may even harm their cognitive development

逻辑关系：

- 前半句：缺乏证据证明 AI 有帮助
- 后半句：甚至可能有害
- “little evidence that...” 是雅思阅读和写作都很常见的谨慎表达，不是绝对否定，而是强调证据不足。

可模仿表达：

- Some researchers argue that there is little evidence that screen-based learning improves children’s concentration.
- Many teachers believe that there is limited evidence that AI tutoring can replace human interaction.

**第二遍精听任务：6-10 句关键表达听写练习**

请打开 TED transcript，对照音频完成听写。以下为“基于演讲内容改写的听写句”，你要听原文相近段落，写下你听到的真实句子或最接近表达。

1. For years, people have been told that smartphones are damaging a whole generation.  
2. Decades of data tell a more complicated story.  
3. Social media may not be the main cause of young people’s mental health problems.  
4. Some indicators of teenage risk-taking have actually improved over time.  
5. We should be careful before blaming one technology for every social problem.  
6. Banning teenagers from online spaces may remove support as well as risk.  
7. The real question is not whether technology exists, but how young people use it.  
8. Policy decisions should be based on evidence rather than panic.

**易听错点提醒**

- 连读：`what we're getting` 可能听成 `whatwe're getting`，注意 /t/ 弱化。
- 弱读：`kind of`, `sort of`, `a lot of` 中的 `of` 常弱读成 /əv/ 或 /ə/。
- 近音：`teen` / `team`，`data` / `date`，`cause` / `course`。
- 数字与时间：注意 `decades`, `historic lows`, `for years` 这类时间范围，不一定是精确年份。
- 转折信号：重点抓 `but`, `however`, `actually`, `instead`, `the problem is`，这些地方通常是答案位置。
- 人名：Candice Odgers 可只记为 “the speaker / the psychologist”，听力复述时不必强行拼写。

**听后复述模板：80-120 词英文**

The talk challenges the common idea that smartphones and social media are simply destroying teenagers’ lives. The speaker argues that the evidence is more complicated than many headlines suggest. Although young people do face serious mental health challenges, social media may not be the single main cause. Some long-term data even show improvements in areas such as violence or risky behaviour. The speaker suggests that banning teenagers from online spaces could create new problems, because online communities may also provide support and connection. Overall, the talk encourages a more evidence-based discussion about technology, teenagers and wellbeing.

## 4. 口语训练：从中式表达到 IELTS 7.0

**Part 1 Question 1: Do you often use technology when you study?**

- 普通答案思路：说自己经常用手机、词典、AI 或视频学习。
- 7 分答案结构：直接回答 + 具体工具 + 好处 + 小限制。
- 高分表达：  
  - I use it on a daily basis.  
  - It helps me access information more efficiently.  
  - That said, I try not to rely on it completely.

示范思路：Yes, I use technology almost every day, especially online dictionaries, podcasts and AI tools. They help me check meanings quickly and expose me to natural English. That said, I still think handwritten notes and face-to-face discussion are important, because they force me to process ideas more deeply.

**Part 1 Question 2: Do you think students today spend too much time on screens?**

- 普通答案思路：是的，学习和娱乐都在屏幕上。
- 7 分答案结构：承认普遍现象 + 区分学习屏幕和娱乐屏幕 + 给出个人判断。
- 高分表达：  
  - screen time is not automatically harmful  
  - it depends on the purpose and quality of use  
  - passive scrolling can easily become a distraction

**Part 1 Question 3: Would you prefer learning from a teacher or an AI tutor?**

- 普通答案思路：更喜欢老师，但 AI 有帮助。
- 7 分答案结构：明确偏好 + 对比优势 + 给出平衡观点。
- 高分表达：  
  - human feedback is more emotionally responsive  
  - AI can provide instant explanations  
  - it should be used as a supplement rather than a replacement

**Part 2 Cue Card**

Describe a time when technology helped you learn something.

You should say:

- what technology you used
- what you were trying to learn
- how it helped you
- and explain whether you would use it again

**Part 2 7 分答案结构**

1. 背景：I’d like to talk about a time when I used...
2. 学习目标：At that time, I was trying to improve...
3. 具体过程：What I found useful was that...
4. 效果：It made the learning process more efficient because...
5. 反思：However, I don’t think technology can replace...
6. 总结：So I would definitely use it again, but in a more controlled way.

**可套用高分表达**

- It lowered the barrier to learning.
- I could get immediate feedback instead of waiting for a teacher.
- The biggest benefit was convenience.
- The downside was that it was easy to become over-dependent.
- It worked best when I used it with a clear purpose.

**Part 3 深度追问**

1. Should schools allow students to use AI tools in class?

- 普通答案思路：可以，但要限制。
- 7 分答案结构：立场 + 年龄差异 + 使用场景 + 监管。
- 高分表达：AI tools should be introduced gradually and with clear guardrails.

2. What skills might students lose if they rely too much on technology?

- 普通答案思路：思考、写作、注意力。
- 7 分答案结构：列 2-3 个技能 + 解释原因 + 举例。
- 高分表达：Students may lose the habit of struggling with difficult ideas.

3. Can technology make education more equal?

- 普通答案思路：能让资源更多，但不一定公平。
- 7 分答案结构：先说机会 + 再说数字鸿沟 + 最后给条件。
- 高分表达：Technology can widen access, but it does not automatically create equality.

4. Who should decide how much technology children use: parents, schools or governments?

- 普通答案思路：三方都要参与。
- 7 分答案结构：分工回答：家长管习惯，学校管学习场景，政府管规则。
- 高分表达：It requires shared responsibility rather than a single decision-maker.

| 项目 | 你要检查什么 |
