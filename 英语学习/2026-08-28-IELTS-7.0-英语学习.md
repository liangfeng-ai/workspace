## 2. 阅读训练：从“看懂”到“雅思阅读速度”

### 文章摘要
古巴将按计划在下周开学，但它面临严重的交通、电力和物资短缺问题。官方说，9 月初将有近 140 万名儿童和青少年返校，但教师覆盖率已经降到 73%，很多教师因为移民或追求更高收入而离开。教育部门准备临时招聘大学生、其他行业的专业人士以及退休教师来补位，同时让学校在校服短缺的情况下保持灵活。文章的重点不只是“开学”，而是一个公共教育系统如何在长期危机中勉强维持运转。

### 8 个核心词汇或短语
1. **kick off**  
   **中文**：开始，启动  
   **原文语境**：文章说古巴将按计划“kick off the school year”。  
   **可替换表达**：begin, start, launch

2. **scant supplies**  
   **中文**：匮乏的物资  
   **原文语境**：校服等物资供应严重不足。  
   **可替换表达**：limited supplies, shortage of materials

3. **as planned**  
   **中文**：按计划  
   **原文语境**：尽管危机持续，开学仍按计划进行。  
   **可替换表达**：on schedule, according to plan

4. **teacher coverage**  
   **中文**：教师覆盖率，教师配置率  
   **原文语境**：官方统计显示教师覆盖率降到 73%。  
   **可替换表达**：staffing level, teacher availability

5. **viability**  
   **中文**：可行性，生存能力  
   **原文语境**：文章提到外界开始质疑这种教育模式的可行性。  
   **可替换表达**：feasibility, sustainability

6. **call back retirees**  
   **中文**：召回退休人员  
   **原文语境**：教育部门打算让退休教师回到岗位。  
   **可替换表达**：bring retirees back, rehire retired staff

7. **keep schools afloat**  
   **中文**：维持学校运转  
   **原文语境**：临时招聘是为了让学校撑下去。  
   **可替换表达**：keep schools running, sustain school operations

8. **meet only 12% of demand**  
   **中文**：只能满足 12% 的需求  
   **原文语境**：校服供应远远跟不上需求。  
   **可替换表达**：cover just 12% of demand, satisfy only a small fraction

### 1 个长难句拆解
**原句**：Cuba has long touted its free and universal state-provided education as one of its greatest achievements, but severe shortages, aggravated by the U.S. sanctions, have led many to question the viability of the model.

**结构拆解**：  
- 前半句：`Cuba has long touted ... as one of its greatest achievements`  
  主语是 Cuba，谓语是 has long touted，意思是“长期宣称/强调”。  
- 转折：`but`  
  说明前后形成强对比。  
- 后半句：`severe shortages ... have led many to question ...`  
  真正的结论是：严重短缺让很多人开始怀疑这个模式是否还能持续。

**逻辑关系**：  
“官方长期自豪” vs “现实中短缺严重”，形成典型的新闻评论式转折。

**可模仿表达**：  
`X has long been seen as ..., but recent shortages/problems have led many to question whether ...`  
这是 IELTS 写作和口语里很好用的让步句型。

2. 更容易受影响的是 routine、repetitive 的白领工作和 entry-level work。

### 第二遍精听任务
请逐句听写下面 6 句：

1. At the moment, I think I can do what I do better than a machine can do it.  
2. Undoubtedly, there is a lot of hype, and there are a lot of promises being made that aren't going to be kept.  
3. I would definitely take that with a grain of salt.  
4. What it looks like at this moment in time is that even though the general perception is that knowledge-type work is higher skill and probably requires more training, that's probably going to be more susceptible in the near term.  
5. The best advice in terms of white collar work is to stay away from that.  
6. You want to be doing things that are non-routine things that are creative.

### 易听错点提醒
- **take that with a grain of salt**：这个固定搭配很容易漏掉或听成别的意思。  
- **white collar**：连读快，容易和 `white color` 混。  
- **non-routine**：重音在 `routine`，前面的 `non-` 很轻。  
- **knowledge-type work**：中间的 `type` 很短，容易漏。  
- **five or 10 years**：数字转折要特别警觉。  
- **at this moment in time**：这是口语里很常见的冗余表达，别漏掉 `in time`。  
- **grain of salt**：`grain` 的元音不要听偏。  
- **entry level work**：`entry` 和 `level` 都要抓住。

## 4. 口语训练：从中式表达到 IELTS 7.0

### 主题
今天围绕 **education, AI, and future work** 练口语。

### Part 1 问题
#### 题 1: Do you prefer studying alone or with other people?
- **普通答案思路**：说你更喜欢哪一种，再给一个原因。  
- **7 分答案结构**：立场 + 原因 1 + 原因 2 + 小例子。  
- **可直接套用的高分表达**：  
  `I prefer..., mainly because...`  
  `It helps me stay focused / bounce ideas off others / avoid passive learning.`

#### 题 2: What makes a good teacher?
- **普通答案思路**：讲清楚耐心、清晰、负责。  
- **7 分答案结构**：核心品质 + 课堂效果 + 对学生长期影响。  
- **可直接套用的高分表达**：  
  `A good teacher doesn't just explain content; they build confidence.`  
  `They can break down difficult ideas into manageable steps.`

#### 题 3: Do you think AI is useful for students?
- **普通答案思路**：先表态，再说利与弊。  
- **7 分答案结构**：观点 + 优点 + 风险 + 平衡结论。  
- **可直接套用的高分表达**：  
  `AI is useful as a support tool, but it shouldn't replace independent thinking.`  
  `The key is to use it responsibly.`

### Part 2 Cue Card
**Describe a time when a new technology changed the way you studied or worked.**  
You should say:
- what the technology was
- when you started using it
- how it changed your routine
- and explain whether it was helpful

**普通答案思路**：  
可以选 ChatGPT、Notion、Anki、在线词典、耳机降噪、网课平台之类的真实经历。

**7 分答案结构**：  
- 开头点题，说明是哪种技术  
- 讲使用前的旧习惯  
- 讲使用后的变化  
- 评价利弊  
- 给一个具体结果

**可直接套用的高分表达**：  
- `It completely changed the way I approached ...`  
- `What used to take me an hour now takes me half the time.`  
- `The biggest benefit was that it made my routine more efficient.`  
- `At the same time, I had to be careful not to become over-reliant on it.`

### Part 3 深度追问
#### 题 1: Should schools limit students' use of AI tools?
- **普通答案思路**：说应该有限制，但不能完全禁。  
- **7 分答案结构**：立场 + 原因 + 例子 + 让步。  
- **高分表达**：  
  `A complete ban is unrealistic.`  
  `Schools should regulate use rather than reject it outright.`

#### 题 2: What skills will matter most in the future?
- **普通答案思路**：强调创造力、沟通、批判性思维。  
- **7 分答案结构**：列举 2-3 个技能 + 说明为什么。  
- **高分表达**：  
  `Problem-solving, adaptability, and empathy will matter more than rote memorisation.`

#### 题 3: Why do some people feel anxious about AI?
- **普通答案思路**：担心失业、失控、信息真假。  
- **7 分答案结构**：现实风险 + 心理风险 + 社会影响。  
- **高分表达**：  
  `People are not just worried about jobs; they are worried about losing control.`

#### 题 4: How can teachers keep students motivated in a tech-heavy world?
- **普通答案思路**：课程要更互动、更有意义。  
- **7 分答案结构**：课堂设计 + 学生参与 + 目标感。  
- **高分表达**：  
  `Students stay motivated when learning feels relevant and interactive.`
